Math Challenge Mode • Game Night

HOW TO PLAY

1Build a 2 × 4 grid with 8 face-down cards.
2Before play begins, reveal any 2 cards in your grid.
3On each turn, choose ONE: draw from the Draw Pile or take the top card from the Discard Pile.
4Replace cards to build better scoring combinations.
5Only vertical pairs score. Horizontal and diagonal pairs do not score.
6If all 4 cards match in a 2 × 2 block, score both columns first, then add a +5 bonus.
7An unmatched YELLOW CARD scores -3. Other unmatched single cards score 0.
8The game ends after 9 rounds, or after Final Turn. Highest total score wins.
