HOW TO PLAY

1For 2–6 players, each player builds a 2 × 4 card grid.
2Draw cards and replace cards in your grid. Face-up cards stay visible.
3Cards score mainly by forming valid combinations. Unmatched single cards score 0.
4YELLOW CARD is the exception: an unmatched Yellow is -3 points.
5The game ends after 9 rounds, or after a player reveals all cards and the Final Turn is completed.
6Highest total score wins.
