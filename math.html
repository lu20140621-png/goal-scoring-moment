MATH CHALLENGE MODE • GAME NIGHT

HOW TO PLAY

1Build a 2 × 4 grid with 8 face-down cards.
2Before play begins, reveal any 2 cards in your grid.
3On each turn, choose ONE: draw from the Draw Pile or take the top card from the Discard Pile.
4Use the card according to the normal replacement rules to improve your scoring combinations.
5Unmatched single cards score 0; an unmatched YELLOW CARD scores -3.
6The game ends after 9 rounds, or after Final Turn. Highest score wins.
