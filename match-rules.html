1. SETUP, DEAL & CLOCK

TEAMS
Two teams of 3: Defender 1, Defender 2, Goalkeeper. Roles are public.
DEAL
Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. Deal exactly 8 to each of the 6 players = 48 cards. Discard the remaining 3 face-down.
CLOCK
Start 10:00 immediately after dealing. Trading and final setup already use match time.
TEAM TRADE
Before locking the team, teammates may exchange cards 1-for-1. Everyone still begins with 8 cards.
FIRST POSSESSION
Rock-Paper-Scissors. Repeat ties. Winner receives Soccer.

GOALKEEPER HAND TIP: A Goalkeeper's YELLOW has no defensive use at the goal. If you do not plan to let the GK attack later, trade YELLOW to a field teammate before locking the team.

2. POSSESSION & WHO MAY ACT

The Soccer Card is the ball. Whoever holds Soccer is the active ballholder.

BALLHOLDER OPTIONS
PASS for free, or play SHOOT if that player holds SHOOT.
GOALKEEPER AS BALLHOLDER
After the GK receives Soccer, the GK is a normal attacker: the GK may PASS or may SHOOT if holding SHOOT.
NO FREE TACKLE
A defender cannot throw TACKLE just because the opponent has Soccer. TACKLE needs a legal PASS or SHOOT-line window.

3. CARD FUNCTIONS

SHOOTStarts a scoring attack. Cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
DEFENSEField: stop this SHOOT only. GK: start RPS save duel.
TACKLEIntercept PASS or challenge a SHOOT line. Success wins Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
YELLOWReaction card tied to the current SHOOT line. Never cancels SHOOT or DEFENSE.

4. COMPLETE PASS FLOW

SOCCER HOLDER→PASS→D1 TACKLE?→D2 TACKLE?→PASS COMPLETE
  1. PASS costs no Action Card.
  2. D1 gets the first TACKLE interception window.
  3. If D1 does not TACKLE, or D1's TACKLE is beaten, D2 gets the second TACKLE window.
  4. If both windows clear, Soccer reaches the intended teammate.
  5. Goalkeeper does not use TACKLE on a normal PASS.
  6. YELLOW is not active during a normal PASS.

Example: BLUE 1 PASS → GREEN D1 TACKLE → BLUE 1 DRIBBLE. D1 is beaten; move to GREEN D2's TACKLE window. If D2 does nothing, BLUE 2 receives Soccer.

5. COMPLETE SHOOT / ATTACK FLOW

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER
  1. Ballholder plays SHOOT. The SHOOT card is spent and cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  2. D1 gets the first defensive line. D1 may play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or not defend.
  3. Resolve D1 completely before D2 acts.
  4. After D1 is cleared, D2 gets the second defensive line and may also play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or not defend.
  5. If a defender plays nothing, that defender did not defend. The attacker does not spend DRIBBLE; the attack moves directly to the next line.
  6. If D2 plays nothing, the SHOOT goes directly to Goalkeeper.

NO SIMULTANEOUS DEFENSE: D2 never throws a card while D1 still has an unresolved response.

6. DEFENSE — FIELD RESULT

  1. Current field defender plays DEFENSE.
  2. DEFENSE itself cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  3. Attacker may play DRIBBLE PAST to beat that DEFENSE.
  4. If attacker does not beat it, the current SHOOT ends.
  5. Soccer stays with the same attacking ballholder.
  6. All temporary beaten/chase states reset.
  7. The attacker may PASS or later SHOOT again. A new SHOOT restarts at D1.

KEY DIFFERENCE: DEFENSE stops the shot but does not win the ball.

7. TACKLE — WINNING SOCCER

TACKLE has two legal uses: intercepting a PASS and challenging the attacker on the current field defensive line after SHOOT.

TACKLE SUCCEEDSCurrent PASS/SHOOT ends immediately. Tackler takes Soccer.
DRIBBLE BEATS TACKLETACKLE fails. During SHOOT, the defender is beaten and gets the chase decision.
ATTACKER YELLOW vs TACKLEDuring a SHOOT line, the current shooter may use YELLOW to cancel that current defender's TACKLE.
NORMAL POSSESSIONNo PASS and no SHOOT-line window = TACKLE is illegal.

8. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

  1. One DRIBBLE beats exactly one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
  2. If the defender never played DEFENSE/TACKLE, no DRIBBLE is required.
  3. Every time a field defender is beaten by DRIBBLE during a SHOOT, pause immediately.
  4. Ask that exact defender: CHASE BACK or LET NEXT LINE DEFEND?
  5. CHASE BACK costs 1 extra discarded card, then the defender must still play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE.
  6. If the same defender is beaten again, ask again and pay again.
  7. DRIBBLE does not bypass Goalkeeper DEFENSE.

9. YELLOW — CURRENT LINE ONLY

YELLOW is tied to the defensive line currently being attacked. It is not owned by Defender 1 for the whole SHOOT.

D1 LINE
While D1 is the current line, only D1 has the defending-side YELLOW window for that line.
D2 LINE
Once play reaches D2, D1's YELLOW window is over. D2 now owns the defending-side YELLOW window.
DEFENDING-SIDE YELLOW
The current defender may use YELLOW only after the attacker plays DRIBBLE or YELLOW against that defender's line.
ATTACKING-SIDE YELLOW
The current shooter may use YELLOW against the current defender's TACKLE, or to counter that defender's YELLOW.
CANNOT CANCEL
SHOOT, DEFENSE, normal PASS, or an action belonging to another defensive line.
YELLOW → YELLOW
A YELLOW may cancel the immediately previous YELLOW on the same current line. Continue until the chain stops.

D2 example: SHOOT → D1 cleared → D2 DEFENSE → attacker DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW. D2 cancels that DRIBBLE, so D2 DEFENSE succeeds unless the attacker answers with YELLOW.

GOALKEEPER + YELLOW: While defending the goal, GK has no defensive YELLOW window. But after GK receives Soccer and later becomes the SHOOTER, GK may use YELLOW as the attacking player against the current field defender's TACKLE / YELLOW. If GK only PASSes, normal PASS still has no YELLOW.

10. GOALKEEPER FLOW

  1. GK stage begins only after D1 and D2 are cleared or chose not to defend.
  2. If GK has no DEFENSE: automatic GOAL +1.
  3. If GK plays DEFENSE: shooter and GK play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
  4. GK wins = SAVE. Shooter wins = GOAL +1. Tie = repeat RPS.
  5. GK DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  6. After any GK-stage result — save, RPS loss, or no-DEFENSE goal — Soccer finishes in the defending Goalkeeper's hand.
  7. The GK is now the new ballholder and may PASS or SHOOT if holding SHOOT.

11. AFTER A TACKLE TURNOVER

  1. Old PASS or SHOOT ends immediately.
  2. Tackler physically takes Soccer.
  3. Old attack, beaten, chase, and YELLOW states are cleared.
  4. Tackler's team becomes the attacking team.
  5. No card is drawn.
  6. New ballholder may PASS or SHOOT if holding SHOOT.
  7. The next new SHOOT begins fresh at opponent D1.

12. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

  1. 00:00: if an active PASS / SHOOT / YELLOW / chase / GK duel is already resolving, finish that current chain first, then end.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards.
  3. Both teams can no longer attack. For this playtest, a team can attack if any teammate still has SHOOT because Soccer can be passed to that teammate.

DO NOT END EARLY: If BLUE has no SHOOT but GREEN still has SHOOT, keep playing. BLUE's DEFENSE and TACKLE still matter.

Higher score wins. Tie → Penalty Shootout.

13. PENALTY SHOOTOUT — PAIRED DRAWS

Do not use one Goalkeeper draw for the whole team. Each field Player gets a separate draw against a fresh opposing Goalkeeper draw.

  1. Collect all 51 Action Cards: hands, played cards, chase-cost discards, and the 3 setup discards. Shuffle all 51.
  2. GREEN 1 draws 1. BLUE GK draws 1 against GREEN 1.
  3. GREEN 2 draws 1. BLUE GK draws a new second card against GREEN 2.
  4. BLUE 1 draws 1. GREEN GK draws 1 against BLUE 1.
  5. BLUE 2 draws 1. GREEN GK draws a new second card against BLUE 2.
  6. Field Player: SHOOT = +1 for that team. Other cards = 0.
  7. Opposing GK: DEFENSE = -1 on that paired draw. Other cards = 0.
  8. Add both paired results for each team. Higher total wins.
  9. If tied, collect all 51 again, reshuffle, and repeat all four paired draws.
GREEN 1 DRAW↔BLUE GK DRAW #1·GREEN 2 DRAW↔BLUE GK DRAW #2
BLUE 1 DRAW↔GREEN GK DRAW #1·BLUE 2 DRAW↔GREEN GK DRAW #2

Example: GREEN 1 draws SHOOT (+1) and BLUE GK draws DEFENSE (-1) → that pair = 0. GREEN 2 draws SHOOT (+1) and BLUE GK's second draw is YELLOW (0) → that pair = +1. GREEN shootout total = 1.

14. COMMON TABLE RULINGS

D2 PLAYS NOTHING
D2 did not defend. No DRIBBLE required. Go directly to GK.
DEFENSE WORKS
SHOOT ends; attacker keeps Soccer.
TACKLE WORKS
Tackler takes Soccer; old action ends.
D1 YELLOW AFTER PLAY MOVED TO D2?
No. D1's line is finished. D2 owns the current defensive YELLOW window.
GK YELLOW WHILE DEFENDING?
No. GK has no defensive YELLOW window at the goal.
GK YELLOW WHILE ATTACKING?
Yes, if GK is the current shooter and uses YELLOW against the current defender's TACKLE / YELLOW.
ONE TEAM NO SHOOT
Match continues if the other team can still attack.
PLAYED CARDS RETURN?
No normal recycle during the 10-minute main match.
