1. SETUP, DEAL & CLOCK

TEAMS
Standard Match Mode playtest is 3v3. Each team has Defender 1, Defender 2, and one Goalkeeper.
ROLES
Roles are public. Everyone knows each team's two field defenders and Goalkeeper.
DEAL
Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. Deal exactly 8 cards to each of the 6 players: 48 cards total. Discard the remaining 3 cards face-down. They are not used during the main match.
10-MINUTE CLOCK
Start the timer immediately after dealing. Team trading and final setup are already part of the 10 minutes.
TEAM TRADE
Before the team is locked, teammates may trade cards. Every trade must keep hand sizes equal; the web version uses 1-for-1 swaps. After the team is locked, normal trading ends.
HAND SIZE AFTER START
Everyone begins with the same 8 cards. Once play begins, hand sizes naturally become different as cards are played or discarded.
FIRST POSSESSION
Use Rock-Paper-Scissors. Repeat ties. The winner receives the Soccer Card and becomes the first attacking team.

2. POSSESSION & PASSING

The Soccer Card represents the ball. Whoever physically holds Soccer is the active ballholder. A new possession is not automatically an attack; the ballholder decides what to do next.

WHO CAN ACT?
The Soccer holder may initiate PASS or play SHOOT if that player has a SHOOT card. This also applies when the Goalkeeper later becomes the Soccer holder.
PASS
PASS is free and uses no Action Card. The Soccer holder may pass to either teammate.
PASS DEFENSE
During a PASS, opposing field Players may attempt TACKLE in formation order: Defender 1 first, then Defender 2. Goalkeepers do not use TACKLE to intercept a normal PASS.
PASS → TACKLE
If a TACKLE succeeds, the pass immediately ends and the tackler takes Soccer.
PASS → TACKLE → DRIBBLE
The passer may play DRIBBLE PAST against that TACKLE. If the DRIBBLE succeeds, that TACKLE is beaten and the pass continues to the next interception window.
YELLOW ON PASS
YELLOW is not active during a normal PASS because a SHOOT chain has not started.
SOCCER HOLDER→PASS→D1 TACKLE?→D2 TACKLE?→PASS COMPLETE

RULING: A defender cannot simply throw TACKLE the instant the other team receives Soccer. Outside a SHOOT, TACKLE's possession-interception window is created by a PASS.

3. WHAT EACH ACTION CARD DOES

SHOOTStarts a scoring attack. SHOOT cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
DEFENSEField Player: ends this SHOOT but does not steal Soccer. Goalkeeper: starts the RPS save duel.
TACKLEIntercepts PASS or challenges during a SHOOT. Successful TACKLE steals Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt. One DRIBBLE = one defensive attempt beaten.
YELLOWOnly active inside a SHOOT chain. Cancels TACKLE, DRIBBLE or YELLOW. Never SHOOT or DEFENSE.

4. COMPLETE SHOOT / ATTACK FLOW

Playing SHOOT creates one attack chain. That chain must be fully resolved before a new PASS or new SHOOT can begin.

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER
  1. Ballholder plays SHOOT. The SHOOT card is spent and the attack begins. YELLOW cannot cancel the SHOOT itself.
  2. Defender 1 receives the first defensive window. D1 may play DEFENSE, play TACKLE, or let the attack through.
  3. If D1 plays DEFENSE or TACKLE, the attacker may answer with DRIBBLE PAST.
  4. If the attacker beats D1 with DRIBBLE, D1 immediately decides whether to pay the chase-back cost or allow the attack to move on.
  5. Defender 2 then receives a defensive window. D2 may also play DEFENSE or TACKLE during the same SHOOT. TACKLE is therefore not limited to passing.
  6. Resolve D2 exactly the same way: DEFENSE/TACKLE → possible DRIBBLE → possible chase-back.
  7. If all active field defense is cleared, the attack reaches the Goalkeeper.

NO SIMULTANEOUS DEFENSE: Both field Players may defend in the same SHOOT, but resolve them in order. Defender 2 does not throw a card while Defender 1 still has an unresolved defensive response. This prevents arguments about which card was first.

If a defender chooses LET ATTACK THROUGH

No card is spent and the attacker does not need DRIBBLE. The attack simply advances to the next defensive line.

If a defender has no DEFENSE or TACKLE

That line is passed automatically. The attack advances without requiring DRIBBLE.

5. DEFENSE — COMPLETE RESULT FLOW

DEFENSE is the safer defensive card. It can stop a SHOOT, but a field Player's DEFENSE does not win possession.

SHOOT→FIELD DEFENSE→DRIBBLE?
NO DRIBBLE / ATTACKER DECLINESDEFENSE succeeds. The current SHOOT ends. Soccer stays with the attacking player who had it.
DRIBBLE SUCCEEDSThe defender is beaten. The defender chooses chase-back or lets the attack continue to the next line.
YELLOW vs DEFENSENot allowed. DEFENSE is immune to YELLOW.
YELLOW CANCELS THE DRIBBLEThe DRIBBLE disappears, so the earlier DEFENSE succeeds. The SHOOT ends, but Soccer still stays with the attacking side.

What happens immediately after a successful field DEFENSE?

  1. The current SHOOT chain ends.
  2. The SHOOT and DEFENSE cards stay spent/discarded.
  3. Soccer remains with the same attacking ballholder.
  4. All temporary beaten/chase states from that SHOOT reset.
  5. The game returns to normal possession.
  6. The ballholder may PASS, or may later use another SHOOT if available.
  7. A new SHOOT always starts again at Defender 1.

Example: BLUE 1 SHOOT → GREEN 1 DEFENSE → BLUE has no DRIBBLE. The attack ends. BLUE 1 still holds Soccer. BLUE may PASS to BLUE 2 or, if BLUE 1 has another SHOOT, attack again. The next SHOOT starts again at GREEN 1.

6. TACKLE — STEALING SOCCER & WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

TACKLE is the possession-winning defensive card. It has two legal families of use: intercepting a PASS and challenging a SHOOT at a field defensive line.

A. TACKLE during PASS

PASS→TACKLE→DRIBBLE?→STEAL OR PASS CONTINUES

B. TACKLE during SHOOT

SHOOT→D1 / D2 TACKLE→YELLOW?→DRIBBLE?→STEAL OR ATTACK CONTINUES
TACKLE SUCCEEDSThe current PASS or SHOOT ends immediately. The tackler physically takes the Soccer Card.
DRIBBLE BEATS TACKLETACKLE fails. During a SHOOT, the defender is beaten and enters the chase-back decision. During a PASS, the pass continues through the remaining interception windows.
YELLOW CANCELS TACKLEOnly possible inside a SHOOT chain. The TACKLE is canceled, no possession changes, and the attack advances to the next defensive line.
TACKLE DURING NORMAL POSSESSIONNot legal merely because the opponent has Soccer. A legal trigger must exist: PASS or a SHOOT defensive window.

After TACKLE successfully steals Soccer

  1. The old PASS or SHOOT chain ends immediately.
  2. The tackler becomes the new Soccer holder.
  3. The tackler's team becomes the attacking team; the old attacking team becomes the defending team.
  4. All beaten, chase-back, and unresolved attack status from the old attack are cleared.
  5. No card is drawn. The game simply enters a new possession phase.
  6. The new Soccer holder may PASS to a teammate, or play SHOOT if holding a SHOOT card.
  7. When the new team eventually plays SHOOT, the opponent's defense begins fresh at Defender 1 → Defender 2 → Goalkeeper.
  8. YELLOW windows from the old attack are closed. A new YELLOW window will not exist until a new SHOOT chain begins.

IMPORTANT: DEFENSE stopping a shot and TACKLE winning the ball are intentionally different. DEFENSE = stop this SHOOT but leave Soccer with the attacker. TACKLE = stop the action and change possession.

7. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE-BACK

DRIBBLE PAST beats exactly one field defensive attempt. There is no fixed per-attack DRIBBLE limit; the attacker may use another DRIBBLE against another legal field defensive attempt if cards remain.

BEATS
One field DEFENSE or one field TACKLE.
DOES NOT BYPASS GK
Once the SHOOT reaches Goalkeeper DEFENSE, DRIBBLE does not skip the Goalkeeper duel.

Every time a field defender is beaten during a SHOOT

  1. Pause the attack immediately.
  2. Ask that exact defender: CHASE BACK or LET THE NEXT LINE DEFEND?
  3. To CHASE BACK, that defender must be able to discard 1 extra card and still have a legal DEFENSE or TACKLE to play.
  4. Discard 1 card as the stamina/recovery cost.
  5. Then play DEFENSE or TACKLE again.
  6. If DRIBBLE beats the same defender again, ask again. Every additional chase requires another 1-card discard.
  7. If the defender chooses not to chase, or cannot pay the cost, move to the next defensive line.

Example: GREEN 1 DEFENSE → BLUE DRIBBLE. GREEN 1 is beaten. GREEN 1 chooses CHASE BACK, discards 1 card, then plays TACKLE. BLUE may now DRIBBLE again. If BLUE beats GREEN 1 again, GREEN 1 must decide again whether to discard another card and chase again.

CHASE COST: The discarded stamina card is spent and does not count as the new defensive card. The defender must still play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE after paying the cost.

8. YELLOW — EXACT TIMING & COUNTER-CHAIN

YELLOW is not a general anytime card in Match Mode. It is a reaction card that only operates once a SHOOT chain has started.

WHO CONTROLS YELLOW?
Each team's Defender 1 is that team's YELLOW controller for the current SHOOT chain. Defender 2 and Goalkeeper do not play YELLOW in that chain.
CAN CANCEL
TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and another YELLOW.
CANNOT CANCEL
SHOOT or DEFENSE.
IMMEDIATE TIMING
YELLOW must be played before the target Action has fully resolved. Once the table has moved on to the next line or new possession, it is too late.
D1 ALREADY BEATEN?
Defender 1 remains that team's YELLOW controller for the rest of that SHOOT chain even if D1 has already been dribbled past.

YELLOW vs DRIBBLE after DEFENSE

SHOOT→DEFENSE→DRIBBLE→YELLOW

The YELLOW cancels DRIBBLE. The earlier DEFENSE therefore succeeds. The SHOOT ends and Soccer remains with the attacker.

YELLOW vs DRIBBLE after TACKLE

The YELLOW cancels DRIBBLE. The earlier TACKLE therefore succeeds and the tackler takes Soccer.

YELLOW directly on TACKLE

The TACKLE is canceled. No one steals Soccer. The current SHOOT continues to the next defensive line.

YELLOW → YELLOW → YELLOW

A YELLOW may cancel the immediately previous YELLOW. Each additional YELLOW reverses the result of the immediately previous one. Resolve the chain from the last YELLOW backward until the final active/canceled state is clear.

Example: BLUE DRIBBLE → GREEN D1 YELLOW (DRIBBLE canceled) → BLUE D1 YELLOW (GREEN YELLOW canceled, DRIBBLE restored) → GREEN D1 YELLOW (BLUE YELLOW canceled, GREEN YELLOW becomes active again, DRIBBLE canceled).

NOT LEGAL: SHOOT → YELLOW to erase SHOOT. SHOOT cannot be canceled in Match Mode.

9. GOALKEEPER — EVERY POSSIBLE RESULT

The Goalkeeper stage begins only after the current SHOOT has cleared both field defensive lines.

GK HAS NO DEFENSE
Automatic GOAL. The attacking team scores +1. Then Soccer goes to the defending Goalkeeper.
GK PLAYS DEFENSE
Goalkeeper DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW. Shooter and Goalkeeper play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
GK WINS RPS
SAVE. No goal. Soccer goes to the Goalkeeper.
SHOOTER WINS RPS
GOAL +1. Even though the shot scored, Soccer still goes to the defending Goalkeeper for the restart.
RPS TIE
Repeat RPS until there is a winner.
DRIBBLE vs GK
DRIBBLE PAST does not skip Goalkeeper DEFENSE. The Goalkeeper stage is resolved by the rule above.

After the Goalkeeper receives Soccer

  1. The old SHOOT chain is completely finished.
  2. Any previous beaten/chase status resets.
  3. The Goalkeeper is now the Soccer holder.
  4. The Goalkeeper's team becomes the attacking team.
  5. The Goalkeeper may PASS to a teammate, or may play SHOOT if holding SHOOT.
  6. The next new SHOOT by that team starts against the opponent's Defender 1.

KEY GOALKEEPER RULE: Once a SHOOT reaches the Goalkeeper stage, Soccer ends in the Goalkeeper's hand afterward whether the Goalkeeper saves it, loses the RPS and concedes, or has no DEFENSE and concedes automatically.

10. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

The main match ends when one of these conditions is reached:

  1. 10:00 reaches 00:00. If a PASS, SHOOT, YELLOW chain, chase-back, or Goalkeeper duel is already resolving, finish that current chain first, then end the match.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards left.
  3. Both teams can no longer attack. For this playtest, a team can attack if any player on that team still has a SHOOT card. The Soccer holder can PASS to a teammate who has SHOOT.

DO NOT END EARLY: If BLUE has no SHOOT anywhere but GREEN still has SHOOT, the match continues. BLUE's DEFENSE and TACKLE cards still matter because GREEN can continue attacking.

BLUE NO SHOOT · GREEN HAS SHOOTContinue the match. BLUE may defend and try to protect the score.
GREEN NO SHOOT · BLUE HAS SHOOTContinue the match. BLUE may still create attacks.
NEITHER TEAM HAS SHOOTEnd the main match and compare the score.
TIME EXPIRES MID-ATTACKFinish that active chain, apply any goal/turnover/save, then end the match.

Higher score wins. If tied, go to the Penalty Shootout.

11. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

  1. Collect all 51 Action Cards: played cards, cards still in hands, chase-cost discards, and the 3 cards discarded during setup.
  2. Shuffle the full deck.
  3. Each field Player draws 1 card. SHOOT = +1 for that team; every other card = 0.
  4. Each Goalkeeper draws 1 card. DEFENSE = -1 against the opponent's shootout total; every other card = 0.
  5. Calculate both team totals.
  6. Higher total wins the match.
  7. If tied, collect all 51 cards again, reshuffle, and repeat the shootout.

12. COMMON TABLE RULINGS — READ THIS BEFORE ARGUING

CAN I TACKLE IMMEDIATELY AFTER THEY GET SOCCER?
No. Normal possession alone is not a TACKLE trigger. TACKLE becomes legal on a PASS or in a field defensive window after SHOOT.
CAN DEFENDER 2 DEFEND THE SAME SHOOT?
Yes. Both field Players may use DEFENSE/TACKLE in one SHOOT, but resolve D1 first and D2 second.
CAN D2 THROW A CARD BEFORE D1 FINISHES?
No. Resolve only one defensive window at a time.
DEFENSE WORKED. WHO HAS SOCCER?
The attacker still has Soccer. Only the current SHOOT is stopped.
TACKLE WORKED. WHO HAS SOCCER?
The tackler takes Soccer immediately. Old attack ends; new possession starts.
DOES A TACKLE TURNOVER KEEP THE OLD ATTACK GOING?
No. The old PASS/SHOOT is over. Reset all attack status. The tackler's team now controls possession.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL SHOOT?
No. SHOOT always starts the attack.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL DEFENSE?
No. Field and Goalkeeper DEFENSE are both immune.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL TACKLE ON A PASS?
No. YELLOW is not active before SHOOT starts.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL TACKLE DURING SHOOT?
Yes. The TACKLE disappears, Soccer stays with the attacker, and the SHOOT moves to the next line.
I DRIBBLED A DEFENDER. CAN THEY DEFEND AGAIN?
Yes. During a SHOOT, ask immediately. They must discard 1 extra card, then play another DEFENSE/TACKLE.
THE SAME DEFENDER IS DRIBBLED TWICE.
Ask again. Every additional chase requires another extra 1-card discard.
GK LOST RPS AND CONCEDED. WHO RESTARTS?
The defending Goalkeeper receives Soccer and becomes the new ballholder.
GK SAVED. WHO HAS SOCCER?
The Goalkeeper has Soccer.
ONE TEAM HAS NO SHOOT.
Do not end the match if the other team can still attack.
DO PLAYED CARDS COME BACK?
No. There is no normal draw/recycle pile during the 10-minute main match. Resource management matters.

13. MAIN-MATCH CARD RESOURCE RULE

There is no normal draw pile during the 10-minute main match. Cards used for SHOOT, DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE, YELLOW, or chase-back costs are spent. The 3 setup extras stay out of play until a possible Penalty Shootout. This is why pre-match trading and card conservation matter.
