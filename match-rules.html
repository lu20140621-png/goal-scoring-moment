GOAL-SCORING MOMENT · MODE 05

MATCH MODE

A 10-minute 3v3 playtest built around possession, passing, two field defensive lines, Goalkeeper duels, limited card resources, and tactical card trading.

👥 3v3 TEST
2 field Players + 1 Goalkeeper per team.
🃏 8 CARDS EACH
48 dealt; 3 extras discarded face-down.
⏱️ 10:00 TOTAL
Trading and setup time count.
⚽ WIN BY SCORE
Higher score wins; ties go to the shootout.

1. SETUP

ROLES
Roles are public. Each team has Defender 1, Defender 2, and a Goalkeeper.
DEAL
Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. Deal exactly 8 cards to each of the 6 players: 48 total. Discard the remaining 3 Action Cards face-down for the main match.
CLOCK
Start the 10-minute timer as soon as the deal is complete. Team trading and formation setup are included in the same 10-minute clock.
TEAM TRADE
Teammates may exchange cards before locking the team. Everyone must keep the same hand size. The web prototype uses 1-for-1 swaps.

2. FORMATION & FIRST POSSESSION

DEFENDER 2

LAST LINE

After setup, use Rock-Paper-Scissors. The winner receives the Soccer Card and attacks first. The Soccer Card always shows who has possession.

3. PASS & POSSESSION

PASS
The Soccer holder may PASS Soccer to any teammate without using an Action Card.
PASS → TACKLE
During a PASS, an opposing field Player may use TACKLE to intercept the ball. A successful TACKLE gives Soccer to the tackler.
PASS → DRIBBLE
The passer may use DRIBBLE PAST to beat a TACKLE attempt on the pass. If the tackle is beaten, the pass continues.
YELLOW ON PASS
YELLOW is not used on a normal PASS because a SHOOT chain has not started.

4. ACTION CARD ROLES

SHOOTStarts a scoring attack. SHOOT itself cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
DEFENSEField Player: stops this SHOOT but does not take Soccer. Goalkeeper: starts the RPS duel.
TACKLEIntercepts a PASS or challenges during a SHOOT. If successful, the tackler takes Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
YELLOWFirst-line reaction during a SHOOT chain. Cancels TACKLE, DRIBBLE, or YELLOW — never SHOOT or DEFENSE.

5. SHOOT & FIELD DEFENSE

When the Soccer holder plays SHOOT, the attack moves through the field lines in order:

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER

Both field Players may defend during the same attack, but their normal defensive windows are resolved in order: Defender 1 first, then Defender 2.

Field Player uses DEFENSE

If DEFENSE is not beaten by DRIBBLE PAST, that SHOOT ends immediately. Soccer stays with the attacking player. DEFENSE does not steal possession and cannot be canceled by YELLOW.

Example: BLUE 1 SHOOT → GREEN 1 DEFENSE. BLUE 1 has no DRIBBLE. The SHOOT ends, but BLUE 1 still holds Soccer and may later PASS or use another SHOOT.

Field Player uses TACKLE

TACKLE can also defend during a SHOOT. If it is not beaten by DRIBBLE PAST and is not canceled by YELLOW, the SHOOT ends and the tackler takes Soccer.

6. DRIBBLE & TRACK BACK

DRIBBLE PAST beats one field defensive attempt. Every time a field Player is successfully dribbled past, that exact defender must immediately decide whether to chase back.

  1. CHASE BACK: discard 1 card from that defender's hand as the recovery/stamina cost.
  2. After paying the cost, that defender may use DEFENSE or TACKLE again.
  3. LET NEXT LINE DEFEND: do not pay a card; the attack moves to the next defensive line.
  4. If the same defender is dribbled past again, make the same decision again. Every extra chase costs another 1 card.

7. YELLOW

YELLOW only becomes active after a SHOOT has started. Each team's Defender 1 is the YELLOW controller for that SHOOT chain.

CAN CANCEL
TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and another YELLOW.
CANNOT CANCEL
SHOOT or DEFENSE.
YELLOW CHAIN
YELLOW may cancel YELLOW. Each new YELLOW reverses the immediately previous YELLOW result, so longer YELLOW chains are allowed.

Example: SHOOT → GREEN 1 DEFENSE → BLUE DRIBBLE → GREEN 1 YELLOW. The YELLOW cancels DRIBBLE, so GREEN 1's DEFENSE succeeds. The SHOOT ends, but Soccer stays with BLUE.

8. GOALKEEPER

After both field defensive lines are beaten or bypassed, the SHOOT reaches the Goalkeeper.

GK HAS DEFENSE
The Goalkeeper may play DEFENSE. This DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW. Then the shooter and Goalkeeper play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
GK WINS RPS
SAVE. No goal is scored. Soccer is placed in the Goalkeeper's hand.
SHOOTER WINS RPS
GOAL +1 for the attacking team. After the goal, Soccer is still placed in the defending Goalkeeper's hand for the restart.
GK HAS NO DEFENSE
The SHOOT scores automatically. The attacking team gets +1, then Soccer goes to the defending Goalkeeper for the restart.
RPS TIE
Repeat until there is a winner.

Goalkeeper possession rule: once the attack reaches the Goalkeeper stage, whether the defense succeeds or fails, Soccer ends up in the Goalkeeper's hand afterward.

9. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

The main match ends when any of these conditions is reached:

  1. The 10-minute clock reaches 00:00. If a card chain is already resolving, finish that current chain first.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards left.
  3. Both teams can no longer make an attack. In the current playtest, this means neither team has a SHOOT available anywhere in its team hands.

Important: if only one team has no SHOOT but the other team can still attack, the match does not end. The no-SHOOT team may still defend with DEFENSE/TACKLE and use its remaining cards while the other side continues attacking.

When the main match ends, the team with the higher score wins. If tied, go to the Penalty Shootout.

10. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

  1. Collect all 51 Action Cards, including played cards, remaining hands, and the 3 setup discards.
  2. Shuffle the full deck.
  3. Every field Player draws 1 card: SHOOT = +1, every other card = 0.
  4. Every Goalkeeper draws 1 card: DEFENSE = -1 against the opponent, every other card = 0.
  5. The team with the higher final shootout score wins.
  6. If tied again, reshuffle all 51 cards and repeat.

11. MAIN-MATCH CARD RESOURCE RULE

There is no normal draw pile during the 10-minute main match. Cards played, used for chase-back costs, or otherwise discarded do not return to hand. Team trading and card conservation therefore matter throughout the entire match.
