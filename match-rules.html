GOAL-SCORING MOMENT · MODE 05

MATCH MODE

A 10-minute team match built around possession, passing, fixed defensive lines, tackles, dribbles, goalkeeper duels, and limited card resources.

👥 STANDARD TEST
3v3: 2 Players + 1 Goalkeeper per team.
🃏 8 CARDS EACH
48 cards are dealt. The 3 extras are discarded face-down.
⏱️ 10:00 TOTAL
The clock includes team trading and formation setup.
⚽ WIN BY SCORE
Higher score wins. A tie goes to the Penalty Shootout.

1. SETUP

TEAMS
Split into two equal teams. The current standard playtest is 3v3. Each team has two Players and one Goalkeeper.
ROLES
Role Cards are public in Match Mode. Everyone knows who the Players and Goalkeepers are.
DEAL
Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. In 3v3, deal exactly 8 Action Cards to every player: 6 × 8 = 48. Discard the remaining 3 Action Cards face-down. They are not used during the main match.
START CLOCK
As soon as the deal is complete, start a 10-minute timer. Team trading and formation setup use this same 10-minute match clock.
TEAM TRADE
Teammates may exchange cards before locking their team. Every player must keep the same 8-card hand size, so trades must exchange equal numbers of cards. The web prototype uses 1-for-1 swaps.

2. FORMATION

After trading, each team locks its defensive order. In standard 3v3, an attack must work through the two outfield defensive lines before reaching the Goalkeeper.

DEFENDER 1
FIRST LINE
DEFENDER 2
SECOND LINE
GOALKEEPER
LAST LINE

The current defender is the player allowed to make the normal defensive response. A defender who has already been beaten may still chase back by paying the extra-card recovery cost described below.

3. FIRST POSSESSION & PASSING

FIRST SOCCER
After setup, use Rock-Paper-Scissors. The winner receives the Soccer Card and attacks first.
POSSESSION
The Soccer Card represents the ball. The player holding Soccer has the active possession and chooses the next attacking action.
PASS
The Soccer holder may pass Soccer to any teammate. PASS does not require an Action Card.
TACKLE THE BALL
The current outfield defender may use TACKLE to try to steal Soccer even when no pass is happening. TACKLE may also be used to intercept a PASS.

Example: BLUE 1 holds Soccer. GREEN 1 may challenge with TACKLE before BLUE 1 shoots or passes. If BLUE 1 passes, GREEN 1 may also use TACKLE against that pass.

4. ACTION CARDS

SHOOTStarts an attack toward goal.
DEFENSEStops a field attack unless the attacker gets past the defender. GK DEFENSE starts the RPS duel.
TACKLEChallenges possession, intercepts passes, and can stop a SHOOT while stealing Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTGets past one outfield defensive attempt. No per-attack usage limit.
YELLOWCancels the immediately previous cancellable Action. DEFENSE is immune. YELLOW may cancel YELLOW.

5. SHOOTING THROUGH THE DEFENSIVE LINES

When the Soccer holder plays SHOOT, resolve the defense in formation order.

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER

Outfield Defender

The current outfield defender may use DEFENSE or TACKLE. If the defender has no legal defensive card, or chooses not to defend, the attack moves through that line without requiring DRIBBLE.

If DEFENSE or TACKLE is played, the attacker may use DRIBBLE PAST to beat that defender. One DRIBBLE PAST gets past one outfield defensive attempt. DRIBBLE PAST may be used multiple times during the same attack as long as the attacker still has cards.

Successful DEFENSE

If an outfield DEFENSE is not beaten by DRIBBLE PAST, the attack ends and the successful defender takes Soccer.

Successful TACKLE against SHOOT

If TACKLE is not beaten or cancelled, the attack ends and the tackler takes Soccer. Because the turnover happened after a SHOOT, it creates an immediate counterattack and the shooter is caught out of position.

6. BEATEN DEFENDER & TRACK BACK

DRIBBLE PAST normally moves the attack to the next defensive line. However, a defender who has already been beaten may choose to chase the attacker and defend again.

  1. Discard 1 extra card from that defender's hand as the recovery/stamina cost.
  2. Then play a legal defensive card, such as DEFENSE or TACKLE.
  3. If that defender is beaten again and wants to defend again, pay the same extra 1-card recovery cost again before the new defensive card.

Example: GREEN 1 plays DEFENSE → BLUE plays DRIBBLE PAST. GREEN 1 is beaten. If GREEN 1 wants to stay with the attack instead of leaving it to GREEN 2, GREEN 1 first discards 1 extra card, then plays another DEFENSE or TACKLE.

7. SHOOT → TACKLE COUNTERATTACK

A successful TACKLE after a SHOOT is more dangerous than a normal turnover. The tackler wins Soccer immediately and the previous shooter is caught forward.

The shooter's team therefore begins the counterattack with that player's defensive line missing. If the out-of-position player wants to recover before the counterattack reaches the defense, that player must discard 1 card from hand to track back.

BLUE SHOOT→GREEN TACKLE WINS→GREEN COUNTERATTACK→BLUE SHOOTER OUT OF POSITION

A TACKLE that steals Soccer before a SHOOT or during a normal PASS changes possession, but the special “shooter out of position” counterattack penalty is triggered specifically by a successful TACKLE against a SHOOT.

8. GOALKEEPER STAGE

After all active outfield defensive lines are beaten or bypassed, the SHOOT reaches the Goalkeeper.

NO DEFENSE
If the Goalkeeper does not play DEFENSE, the SHOOT scores. The attacking team gains +1 goal.
GK DEFENSE
If the Goalkeeper plays DEFENSE, the shooter and Goalkeeper use Rock-Paper-Scissors. YELLOW cannot cancel this DEFENSE.
SHOOTER WINS
GOAL. The attacking team gains +1.
GOALKEEPER WINS
SAVE. The Goalkeeper takes Soccer and may pass it to any teammate.
RPS TIE
Repeat Rock-Paper-Scissors until there is a winner.

DRIBBLE PAST does not bypass the Goalkeeper's DEFENSE. Once the attack reaches the Goalkeeper stage, the result is decided by the Goalkeeper rule above.

9. YELLOW CARD

YELLOW is a reaction/cancel card. It cancels the immediately previous cancellable Action Card before that Action fully resolves.

CAN CANCEL
SHOOT, TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and another YELLOW.
CANNOT CANCEL
DEFENSE. This includes an outfield Player's DEFENSE and the Goalkeeper's DEFENSE.

YELLOW can be answered by another YELLOW, so it is never an unbeatable card.

SHOOT→YELLOW→YELLOW

In the example above, the second YELLOW cancels the first YELLOW, so the SHOOT becomes active again. Longer YELLOW chains are allowed; each YELLOW cancels the immediately previous YELLOW/effect.

10. AFTER A GOAL OR SAVE

AFTER GOAL
The team that conceded receives Soccer for the restart. In the current web prototype, Defender 1 receives the restart and may immediately PASS to any teammate.
AFTER SAVE
The Goalkeeper keeps Soccer and may PASS it to any teammate.
AFTER FIELD DEFENSE
A successful field defender takes Soccer and the teams switch from attack to defense.

11. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

The main match ends when any one of these conditions is reached:

  1. The 10-minute clock reaches 00:00.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards left.
  3. Both teams still have cards, but neither team can make a legal attack anymore.

If 00:00 occurs while an already-started card chain is still resolving, finish that current chain, then end the main match.

If one team has the higher score, that team wins. If the score is tied, go immediately to the Penalty Shootout.

12. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

The shootout does not use the remaining hands from the main match.

  1. Collect all 51 Action Cards, including cards played during the match, cards still in hands, and the 3 cards discarded at setup.
  2. Shuffle the full Action deck again.
  3. Every outfield Player draws 1 card.
  4. Every Goalkeeper draws 1 card.
  5. An outfield Player scores +1 only if that Player draws SHOOT. Any other card is 0.
  6. A Goalkeeper makes -1 only if that Goalkeeper draws DEFENSE. That -1 is applied against the opposing team's shootout total. Any other Goalkeeper draw is 0.
  7. The team with the higher final shootout score wins the match.

Example: BLUE Player 1 draws SHOOT (+1), BLUE Player 2 draws TACKLE (0), GREEN Goalkeeper draws DEFENSE (-1 against BLUE). BLUE's shootout total for that draw is 0.

If the shootout draw is still tied, collect all 51 Action Cards again, reshuffle, and repeat the draw until one team has the higher score.

13. MAIN-MATCH CARD RESOURCE RULE

There is no normal draw pile during the 10-minute match. The Action Cards you receive at setup are your team's limited resources. Played cards and recovery-cost discards do not return to hand during the main match. This is why team trading, card conservation, and deciding when to chase back matter.
