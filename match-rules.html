1. SETUP, DEAL & CLOCK

TEAMS
Play 3v3. Each team has Defender 1, Defender 2 and one Goalkeeper. Roles are public.
DEAL
Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. Deal exactly 8 to each of the 6 players = 48 cards. Discard the remaining 3 face-down for the main match.
CLOCK
Start the 10:00 timer immediately after dealing. Team trading and final setup already count against the clock.
TRADING
Before teams are locked, teammates may exchange cards while keeping hand sizes equal. The web version uses 1-for-1 swaps.
FIRST SOCCER
Use Rock-Paper-Scissors. Repeat ties. The winner receives Soccer and starts with possession.

2. POSSESSION & PASSING

Soccer represents possession. Only the player physically holding Soccer is the active ballholder.

BALLHOLDER OPTIONS
The holder may PASS to either teammate for free, or play SHOOT if holding a SHOOT card.
PASS
PASS uses no Action Card.
PASS DEFENSE
During a PASS, Defender 1 gets the first TACKLE window, then Defender 2. Goalkeeper does not TACKLE a normal PASS.
PASS TACKLE
Successful TACKLE ends the PASS and gives Soccer to the tackler. The passer may answer that TACKLE with DRIBBLE PAST.
YELLOW ON PASS
No YELLOW during a normal PASS. A SHOOT defensive line has not started yet.
SOCCER HOLDER→PASS→D1 TACKLE?→D2 TACKLE?→PASS COMPLETE

RULING: You cannot TACKLE simply because an opponent just received Soccer. Outside a SHOOT, TACKLE needs a PASS window.

3. ACTION CARD FUNCTIONS

SHOOTStarts one scoring attack. It cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
DEFENSEField Player: stops this SHOOT but does not steal Soccer. GK: starts RPS.
TACKLEIntercept PASS or challenge a SHOOT line. Successful TACKLE wins Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
YELLOWLine-specific reaction. Defender uses it only against opponent DRIBBLE/YELLOW on that line.

4. COMPLETE SHOOT / ATTACK FLOW

One SHOOT creates one attack chain. Resolve it completely before any new PASS or SHOOT.

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER
  1. Ballholder spends SHOOT. SHOOT cannot be erased by YELLOW.
  2. D1 receives the first defensive line. D1 may play DEFENSE, play TACKLE, or not defend.
  3. If D1 plays DEFENSE/TACKLE, attacker may respond with DRIBBLE. Resolve all reactions on D1 before moving on.
  4. If D1 is passed, D1 may pay the chase-back cost after a successful DRIBBLE, otherwise the attack advances.
  5. D2 now receives its own separate defensive line and the same choices.
  6. If D2 uses no DEFENSE or TACKLE, D2 has not defended. The attacker needs no DRIBBLE and goes directly to the Goalkeeper.
  7. If both field lines are cleared, resolve the Goalkeeper stage.

VERY IMPORTANT: D1 and D2 are separate windows. D2 does not play before D1 finishes. Once the attack moves to D2, D1's normal defensive-line reactions are over.

Example: SHOOT → D1 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D1 does not chase → D2 plays nothing → no second DRIBBLE is required → attack goes straight to GK.

5. FIELD DEFENSE — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER IT WORKS

WHEN USED
Only when the SHOOT is currently at that field defender's line.
YELLOW IMMUNITY
DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
IF NOT DRIBBLED
The current SHOOT ends immediately.
SOCCER AFTER DEFENSE
Soccer stays with the attacking ballholder. DEFENSE stops the shot; it does not win possession.
NEXT ACTION
Reset temporary attack states. The holder may PASS or later use another SHOOT. A new SHOOT starts again at D1.

Example: BLUE SHOOT → GREEN D1 DEFENSE → BLUE does not DRIBBLE. This SHOOT ends, but BLUE still has Soccer.

6. TACKLE — WINNING POSSESSION

TACKLE is the aggressive possession-winning defense.

NORMAL PASS
D1 then D2 may TACKLE a PASS. YELLOW is not active during the normal PASS.
DURING SHOOT
The current field defender may choose TACKLE instead of DEFENSE in that defender's own line.
ATTACKER RESPONSE
The attacker may use DRIBBLE PAST. The attacker may also use YELLOW against the current defender's TACKLE. If the attacker plays YELLOW, that same current defender may answer with YELLOW because the opponent has now played YELLOW against this line.
TACKLE SUCCEEDS
The current action ends immediately and the tackler takes Soccer.
AFTER STEAL
Old attack status clears. The tackler's team is now attacking. The new holder may PASS or SHOOT; the next SHOOT starts at the opponent's D1.

KEY DIFFERENCE: DEFENSE = stop the SHOOT, attacker keeps Soccer. TACKLE = stop the action and change possession.

7. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE-BACK

BEATS
Exactly one field DEFENSE or one field TACKLE attempt.
NO FREE BYPASS
If a defender plays nothing, no DRIBBLE is required. DRIBBLE is only needed to beat an actual DEFENSE/TACKLE.
AFTER EVERY SUCCESSFUL DRIBBLE
Immediately ask that exact beaten defender: CHASE BACK or LET THE NEXT LINE DEFEND?
CHASE COST
Discard 1 extra card, then play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE. The discarded card is only the stamina cost.
BEATEN AGAIN
Ask again. Every additional chase costs another discard.
GOALKEEPER
DRIBBLE does not bypass Goalkeeper DEFENSE/RPS.

8. YELLOW — CURRENT DEFENSIVE LINE ONLY

YELLOW is tied to the current field defensive line. It is not controlled by Defender 1 for the whole attack.

WHO MAY USE DEFENSIVE YELLOW?
The field defender whose line is currently being attacked. At D1, D1 controls that line's defensive YELLOW. After play moves to D2, D2 controls that line's defensive YELLOW.
DEFENDER MAY USE YELLOW ONLY WHEN...
The opponent has just played DRIBBLE PAST against this defender's DEFENSE/TACKLE, or the opponent has just played YELLOW against this line.
DEFENDER CANNOT YELLOW
SHOOT, DEFENSE, a normal PASS, an action on a different defensive line, or an action that has already fully resolved.
ATTACKER YELLOW vs TACKLE
During the current SHOOT line, the attacker may use YELLOW to cancel that defender's TACKLE. The current defender may then use YELLOW to cancel the attacker's YELLOW.
YELLOW vs DRIBBLE
If the current defender uses YELLOW on DRIBBLE, that DRIBBLE is canceled and the earlier defensive card becomes active again.
YELLOW CHAIN
YELLOW may cancel the immediately previous YELLOW. The attacker and the current defender may continue the chain while they have YELLOW cards.
WHEN THE LINE CHANGES
Once D1 is finished and the attack moves to D2, D1 can no longer use YELLOW for D2's line. D2 now owns the defensive YELLOW window.
GOALKEEPER STAGE
This field-line YELLOW window ends when the SHOOT reaches the Goalkeeper.

Example — Defender 1

SHOOT → D1 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D1 YELLOW. D1's YELLOW cancels the DRIBBLE. D1 DEFENSE succeeds, the SHOOT ends, and Soccer stays with the attacker.

Example — Defender 2

SHOOT → D1 passed → D2 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW. D2, not D1, cancels the DRIBBLE. D2 DEFENSE succeeds.

Example — TACKLE & YELLOW counter

SHOOT → D2 TACKLE → attacker YELLOW → D2 YELLOW. Attacker's YELLOW tried to cancel D2 TACKLE. D2's YELLOW cancels that YELLOW, so the TACKLE becomes active again.

DO NOT CROSS LINES: A player cannot use YELLOW to protect a teammate's different defensive line.

9. GOALKEEPER FLOW

REACHING GK
The SHOOT reaches GK only after D1 and D2 have both been cleared or chose not to defend.
GK NO DEFENSE
Automatic GOAL +1. Soccer then goes to the defending Goalkeeper.
GK DEFENSE
Starts Rock-Paper-Scissors between shooter and Goalkeeper. GK DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
GK WINS
SAVE. No goal. Soccer is in the Goalkeeper's hand.
SHOOTER WINS
GOAL +1. Soccer still goes to the defending Goalkeeper for the restart.
RPS TIE
Repeat until there is a winner.

KEY GK RULE: Once a SHOOT reaches the GK stage, Soccer ends with the defending GK whether the GK saves, concedes in RPS, or has no DEFENSE and concedes automatically.

10. AFTER POSSESSION CHANGES

After successful TACKLE

  1. Old PASS/SHOOT ends.
  2. Tackler takes Soccer.
  3. All old line, DRIBBLE, chase and YELLOW status clears.
  4. Tackler's team becomes attacking team.
  5. New holder may PASS or SHOOT.
  6. A new SHOOT begins at opponent D1.

After field DEFENSE

  1. Only the SHOOT ends.
  2. Attacker keeps Soccer.
  3. Temporary attack status resets.
  4. Attacker may PASS or start another SHOOT later.

After Goalkeeper stage

  1. Old SHOOT ends completely.
  2. Defending GK receives Soccer.
  3. GK's team becomes attacking team.
  4. GK may PASS or SHOOT if holding SHOOT.

11. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

  1. 00:00. If a PASS, SHOOT, line-specific YELLOW chain, chase-back or GK duel is already resolving, finish that current chain first, then end.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards left.
  3. Both teams can no longer attack. A team can still attack if any teammate has a SHOOT because Soccer can be passed to that teammate.

ONE TEAM WITH NO SHOOT DOES NOT END THE MATCH. If BLUE has no SHOOT but GREEN still has SHOOT, continue. BLUE's DEFENSE/TACKLE still matter.

Higher score wins. If tied, use the Penalty Shootout.

12. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

  1. Collect all 51 Action Cards, including hands, played cards, chase-cost discards and the 3 setup extras.
  2. Shuffle all 51.
  3. Each field Player draws 1. SHOOT = +1; all other cards = 0.
  4. Each GK draws 1. DEFENSE = -1 against the opponent total; all other cards = 0.
  5. Higher team total wins.
  6. If tied, collect all 51, reshuffle and repeat.

13. COMMON TABLE RULINGS

D2 DOES NOT PLAY DEFENSE/TACKLED2 did not defend. No DRIBBLE is required. Go directly to GK.
D1 WAS PASSED. CAN D1 YELLOW D2'S DRIBBLE?No. Once the attack is at D2, D2 owns that defensive YELLOW window.
D2 DEFENSE → ATTACKER DRIBBLED2 may play YELLOW to cancel that DRIBBLE before it resolves.
D2 TACKLE → ATTACKER YELLOWD2 may answer with YELLOW because the opponent played YELLOW against D2's line.
DEFENDER WANTS YELLOW ON SHOOTNot legal. SHOOT cannot be canceled.
DEFENDER WANTS YELLOW ON DEFENSENot legal. DEFENSE is immune.
DEFENSE WORKEDShooter keeps Soccer; only this SHOOT ends.
TACKLE WORKEDTackler immediately takes Soccer; old action ends.
SAME DEFENDER DRIBBLED TWICEAsk chase-back again. Every extra chase costs another discard.
ONE TEAM HAS NO SHOOTContinue if the other team can still attack.

14. MAIN-MATCH RESOURCE RULE

There is no normal draw/recycle pile during the 10-minute main match. Played cards and chase-back cost cards are spent. The 3 setup extras remain outside the main match until a possible Penalty Shootout.
