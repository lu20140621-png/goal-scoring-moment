STRATEGY MODE 2

GOALKEEPER HUNT RULEBOOK

Goal

One player is the hidden Goalkeeper. All other players are normal Players. The normal Players win the moment the Goalkeeper is eliminated. The Goalkeeper wins if every other living Player is eliminated and the Goalkeeper is the last survivor.

Set Up

  1. Use 1 Goalkeeper Role Card and enough Player Role Cards for the group.
  2. Shuffle the Role Cards and deal 1 face down to each player. Look at your own role only.
  3. There are no teams. Sit in one fixed clockwise turn order.
  4. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  5. Use Rock–Paper–Scissors to choose the first player. That player starts with the Soccer Card.

Your Turn

Draw 1 Action Card at the start of your normal turn. Then play any legal actions you want. Finish each reaction chain completely before starting another action. End your turn when you are finished.

PASS / SHOOT / DEFENSE / DRIBBLE

PASS: If you have the Soccer Card, you may give it to any living player. Your turn continues.

SHOOT: You need the Soccer Card. Choose any other living player.

DEFENSE: Because there are no teams, only the player being attacked may use DEFENSE.

DRIBBLE PAST: After the target's first DEFENSE, the attacker may use DRIBBLE PAST to continue the same attack. The target may answer with a second DEFENSE if available.

TACKLE / YELLOW

TACKLE: On your turn, take the Soccer Card from the living player who currently has it. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE.

YELLOW: Skip the next living player in the fixed clockwise order. That player does not take a normal turn.

Soccer Tokens = Information

A successful goal gives the target 1 Soccer Token and the Soccer Card moves to that target. At 1 Token the role stays hidden. At 2 Tokens the target immediately reveals their Role Card.

If the revealed role is PLAYER, everyone now knows that person is not the Goalkeeper. At a successful third Token, a normal Player is eliminated and discards their hand.

Goalkeeper Reveal & Passive

When the hidden Goalkeeper reaches 2 Tokens, reveal the Goalkeeper immediately. The Goalkeeper's one-time passive is now armed.

The next opponent attack that would successfully give the Goalkeeper a third Token is automatically canceled once. No third Token is added, the Soccer Card goes to the Goalkeeper, and the passive is used up. The next successful third-token goal eliminates the Goalkeeper and ends the game.

Important Deduction Rule

Using DEFENSE, TACKLE, YELLOW, or bluffing does not prove that someone is the Goalkeeper. The reliable reveal happens at 2 Soccer Tokens. Use actions to control possession and choose who gets tested.
