Chapter 1 — What Are You Trying to Do?

Strategy Mode is a team game for 2–8 players. Your team tries to attack the other team, give their players Soccer Tokens, and discover which opponent is the hidden Goalkeeper.

  • A successful attack gives the target 1 Soccer Token.
  • A player with 3 Soccer Tokens is eliminated.
  • If the eliminated player is the other team's Goalkeeper, your team wins immediately.
  • You can also win if every player on the other team is eliminated.
Easy idea: Think of the Goalkeeper as the opponent your team most wants to find. Keep attacking and watching who gets eliminated.

Chapter 2 — Make Two Teams

  1. Split all players into a Blue Team and a Green Team.
  2. Make the teams as even as possible.
  3. Each player takes the Role Card color that matches their team.
Example: With 4 players, make two teams of 2. With 6 players, make two teams of 3.

Chapter 3 — Give Everyone a Secret Role

Each team must have exactly 1 Goalkeeper. Everyone else on that team is a Player.

1For the Blue Team, prepare 1 Blue Goalkeeper card plus enough Blue Player cards for everyone else on Blue.
2Do the same for the Green Team.
3Shuffle each team's Role Cards and give each teammate 1 card.
4Look at your own Role Card, then keep it hidden. Do not reveal the Goalkeeper until that player is eliminated.
Team color is public. The exact role is secret. Do not place your Role Card face-up at the start.

Chapter 4 — Set Up the Cards

  1. Put the Soccer Card aside. It is used to show who has possession.
  2. Shuffle all Action Cards together to make the Draw Pile.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to every player.
  4. Keep your Action Cards hidden in your hand.
  5. Leave space for a Discard Pile.
  6. Place the Soccer Tokens where everyone can reach them.

IMPORTANTThe Soccer Card is not a normal Action Card. The player holding it is the player who currently has the ball.

Chapter 5 — Decide Who Attacks First

Use Rock–Paper–Scissors to decide which team attacks first.

  1. Choose one player from each team to play Rock–Paper–Scissors.
  2. The winning side becomes the first attacking team.
  3. The winning side chooses its first player.
  4. That first player receives the Soccer Card and takes the first turn.
If Blue wins Rock–Paper–Scissors, Blue attacks first and starts with possession.

Chapter 6 — Decide the Turn Order

Turns alternate between the two teams:

Blue → Green → Blue → Green → …

Before the game starts, each team chooses a fixed order for its own players. The first time Blue acts, Blue Player 1 goes. The next time Blue acts, Blue Player 2 goes, and so on. Green does the same.

Keep the same player order for the whole game. This matters because YELLOW CARD always affects the next living player in that established order.

Chapter 7 — What Happens on Your Turn?

1Draw 1 Action Card from the Draw Pile.
2Look at your hand and decide what you want to do.
3You may play more than one legal Action Card during your turn.
4If you have the Soccer Card, you may PASS it to a teammate or use cards that require possession, such as SHOOT.
5When you are finished, say your turn is over. The next player in the established order takes a turn.

After an Action Card finishes resolving, place it in the Discard Pile unless the card is still part of an unfinished response chain.

Chapter 8 — Soccer Possession & PASS

The Soccer Card shows who has possession. Only one player can have it at a time.

PASS is not an Action Card. If you have possession during your own turn, you may give the Soccer Card to a living teammate.

  • You cannot PASS to an opponent.
  • You cannot PASS for another player.
  • You may only PASS during your own turn.
You have the Soccer Card but your teammate has a better hand. You may PASS possession to that teammate.

Chapter 9 — SHOOT

SHOOT is how you try to score against an opponent.

  1. You must have the Soccer Card.
  2. Play 1 SHOOT card.
  3. Choose one living opponent as the target.
  4. Give the defending side a chance to respond with DEFENSE.
  5. If nobody successfully stops the attack, the target receives 1 Soccer Token.
  6. After a successful goal, the Soccer Card goes to the player who was scored on.
No Soccer Card = no SHOOT. Having a SHOOT card by itself is not enough.

Chapter 10 — DEFENSE

DEFENSE is a reaction card. You normally play it because the other team just attacked.

  • The player targeted by SHOOT may play DEFENSE.
  • A living teammate of that target may also play DEFENSE to protect them.
  • If DEFENSE successfully stops the attack, the attack does not give a Soccer Token.
  • The Soccer Card goes to the player who played the successful DEFENSE.
Blue SHOOTs Green 1. Green 2 plays DEFENSE for Green 1. If the attack ends there, Green 2 gets possession.

Chapter 11 — DRIBBLE PAST

DRIBBLE PAST lets the attacker keep the attack alive after the first DEFENSE.

  1. You play SHOOT.
  2. The defending side plays DEFENSE.
  3. If you have DRIBBLE PAST, you may now play it.
  4. DRIBBLE PAST may be played at most once during your turn.

If the defending side cannot make a second DEFENSE, the original attack continues successfully and the target receives the Soccer Token.

DRIBBLE PAST is not a free skill. You must actually have the DRIBBLE PAST card in your hand and discard it when played.

Chapter 12 — The Second DEFENSE

After DRIBBLE PAST, the defending side gets one more chance to stop the attack.

To make this second DEFENSE, the defending player must:

  1. Play 1 DEFENSE card, and
  2. Discard 1 additional card from their hand as the extra cost.

If this second DEFENSE succeeds, the attack is stopped and the Soccer Card goes to the player who made that second DEFENSE.

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → DEFENSE + discard 1 extra card. The attack is stopped.

Chapter 13 — TACKLE

TACKLE is used to steal possession.

  • Play TACKLE only during your own turn.
  • Choose a player who currently has the Soccer Card.
  • Take the Soccer Card from that player and place it in front of you.
  • DEFENSE cannot be used against TACKLE.
TACKLE is not a reaction card. Do not play it during another player's turn.

Chapter 14 — YELLOW CARD

In Strategy Mode, YELLOW CARD makes the next living player in the established turn order lose their next turn.

  1. Play YELLOW CARD during your own turn.
  2. Look at who would normally act next.
  3. That player is skipped once.
  4. Continue with the next living player after them.
You do not freely choose any target. YELLOW CARD only affects the next player in turn order.

Chapter 15 — Soccer Tokens & Elimination

Whenever an attack succeeds, give the target 1 Soccer Token.

  • 0–2 Tokens: the player is still in the game.
  • 3 Tokens: that player is immediately eliminated.
  • When eliminated, reveal that player's Role Card.
  • If the eliminated player is a normal Player, the game continues.
  • An eliminated player may give their remaining Action Cards to a living teammate.
A Player with 2 Tokens is hit by one more successful SHOOT. They now have 3 Tokens, so they are eliminated and reveal their Role Card.

Chapter 16 — How to Win

Your team wins immediately if either of these happens:

  1. You eliminate the other team's hidden Goalkeeper, or
  2. You eliminate every player on the other team.
You do not need to eliminate every opponent if the Goalkeeper is already eliminated. The moment the Goalkeeper is revealed after reaching 3 Tokens, the match is over.

Chapter 17 — A Full Attack Example

  1. It is Blue 1's turn. Blue 1 draws 1 card.
  2. Blue 1 has the Soccer Card, so Blue 1 plays SHOOT at Green 1.
  3. Green 2 plays DEFENSE to protect Green 1.
  4. Blue 1 plays DRIBBLE PAST. This is Blue 1's only DRIBBLE PAST for this turn.
  5. Green 1 wants to use a second DEFENSE. Green 1 plays DEFENSE and discards 1 extra card.
  6. The second DEFENSE succeeds, so Green 1 gets the Soccer Card.
  7. Blue 1 finishes the turn. Next comes the next living Green player in the established order.

Remember the chain: SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → second DEFENSE + 1 extra discard.

Chapter 18 — Quick Check Before You Start

✓Two teams are made.
✓Each team has exactly 1 hidden Goalkeeper.
✓Each player has 5 Action Cards.
✓The Soccer Card is separate and ready.
✓Rock–Paper–Scissors decided the first attacking side.
✓Both teams know the fixed player order.
✓Everyone knows: 3 Tokens = eliminated; Goalkeeper eliminated = immediate loss.
