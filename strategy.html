Chapter 1 — What Is the Goal?

This is a team game for 2–8 players. There are 2 teams: Blue and Green.

1Attack players on the other team.
2When your attack works, that player gets 1 Soccer Token.
33 Soccer Tokens means that player is out.
4When a player is out, turn over that player's secret Role Card.
5If that player is the other team's Goalkeeper, your team wins right away.
Easy memory rule: Attack → give Tokens → find the hidden Goalkeeper.

Chapter 2 — Make 2 Teams

  1. Make a Blue Team and a Green Team.
  2. Try to put the same number of people on each team.
  3. Sit so everyone knows who is Blue and who is Green.
4 players: 2 Blue and 2 Green. 6 players: 3 Blue and 3 Green.

Chapter 3 — Give Out the Secret Roles

Each team has 1 Goalkeeper. Everyone else on that team is a Player.

  1. For Blue, take 1 Blue Goalkeeper card and enough Blue Player cards for the Blue team.
  2. Shuffle those Blue Role Cards face-down.
  3. Give 1 Role Card to each Blue player.
  4. Do the same thing for Green.
  5. Look at your own Role Card. Keep it secret.
Everyone may know your TEAM color. Nobody should know who the Goalkeeper is.

Chapter 4 — Set Up the Game

  1. Put the Soccer Card to the side for now.
  2. Shuffle all Action Cards together.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to every player.
  4. Keep your hand hidden from the other players.
  5. Put the rest of the Action Cards in one Draw Pile.
  6. Leave a place next to it for used cards.
  7. Put the Soccer Tokens where everyone can reach them.
The Soccer Card is NOT part of your normal hand. It only shows who has the ball.

Chapter 5 — Who Starts the Game?

  1. Blue chooses 1 person. Green chooses 1 person.
  2. Those 2 people play Rock–Paper–Scissors.
  3. The winning team attacks first.
  4. The winning team chooses which teammate takes the first turn.
  5. That first player gets the Soccer Card.
If Blue wins, Blue chooses its first player. That player starts with the Soccer Card.

Chapter 6 — The Turn Order

The teams take turns back and forth.

Example with 4 players:
Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2 → Blue 1 → ...

Choose the order before the game. Keep using the same order.

Moving the Soccer Card does NOT move the turn. The turn order stays the same unless YELLOW CARD skips the next player.

Chapter 7 — What Do I Do on My Turn?

1Draw 1 Action Card.
2Look at your hand.
3Play any legal cards you want. You may play more than 1 card.
4You may stop whenever you want.
5When you want to stop, say “My turn is over.”
6Only then does the next player in the fixed turn order begin.
WHO ENDS YOUR TURN? YOU DO.
Your turn does not end because you PASS, SHOOT, score, get blocked, use TACKLE, or finish an attack chain. Your turn ends only when you say it is over.
You SHOOT. They DEFEND. You still have a TACKLE, so you may keep playing. If you do not want to keep playing, say “My turn is over.”

Chapter 8 — Soccer Card & PASS

The player holding the Soccer Card has the ball.

If it is your turn and you have the Soccer Card, you may PASS it to a living teammate.

PASS MOVES THE BALL. PASS DOES NOT MOVE THE TURN.
Turn order: Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2.
It is Blue 1's turn. Blue 1 PASSES the Soccer Card to Blue 2. Blue 2 now has the ball, but it is still Blue 1's turn. When Blue 1 says “My turn is over,” the next player is Green 1, because Green 1 is next in the fixed order.
  • You may PASS only during your own turn.
  • You may PASS only to a living teammate.
  • Your teammate does not get an extra turn just because they received the Soccer Card.

Chapter 9 — SHOOT

SHOOT is how you attack an opponent.

  1. Make sure you have the Soccer Card.
  2. Play 1 SHOOT card.
  3. Choose 1 living opponent.
  4. Wait. The defending team may use DEFENSE.
  5. If the attack is not stopped, the target gets 1 Soccer Token.
  6. After a successful SHOOT, give the Soccer Card to the player you scored on.
A successful SHOOT does not end your turn. You decide when your turn ends.
You score on Green 1. Green 1 gets 1 Token and the Soccer Card. If you have TACKLE, you may take the Soccer Card back before you say your turn is over.

Chapter 10 — DEFENSE

DEFENSE is used when the other team SHOOTs.

  1. An opponent plays SHOOT.
  2. The player being attacked may play DEFENSE.
  3. One of that player's living teammates may also play DEFENSE for them.
  4. If DEFENSE is the final successful defense, the attack is stopped.
  5. No Soccer Token is given.
  6. The player who played the successful DEFENSE gets the Soccer Card.
DEFENSE is a reaction. Using DEFENSE does not mean the attacker’s turn is over.

Chapter 11 — DRIBBLE PAST

DRIBBLE PAST gives the attacker one more chance after the first DEFENSE.

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST
  1. You SHOOT.
  2. The defending team plays DEFENSE.
  3. If you have DRIBBLE PAST, you may play it.
  4. Now the defending team gets one more chance to DEFEND.
You may use DRIBBLE PAST only once during your whole turn, even if you SHOOT more than once.

Chapter 12 — The Second DEFENSE

If the attacker used DRIBBLE PAST, the defending team may try a second DEFENSE.

  1. Play 1 DEFENSE card.
  2. Also discard 1 extra card from your hand.
  3. If you do both, the second DEFENSE works.
  4. The attack is stopped.
  5. The player who played that second DEFENSE gets the Soccer Card.
SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → DEFENSE + DISCARD 1 EXTRA CARD
The active player still decides when their turn ends.

Chapter 13 — TACKLE

TACKLE lets you take the Soccer Card from another player.

  1. It must be your own turn.
  2. Choose the player who has the Soccer Card.
  3. Play TACKLE.
  4. Take the Soccer Card and put it in front of you.
DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE.
After you score: SHOOT succeeds → opponent gets the Soccer Card → you TACKLE it back → you may SHOOT again.
After they block you: SHOOT → DEFENSE → defender gets the Soccer Card → you TACKLE it back → you may SHOOT again.
After the full defense chain: SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → second DEFENSE + discard 1 → defender gets the Soccer Card → you TACKLE it back → you may SHOOT again.

Chapter 14 — YELLOW CARD

YELLOW CARD skips the next living player in the fixed turn order.

  1. Play YELLOW CARD during your own turn.
  2. Look at the fixed turn order.
  3. The next living player loses their next turn.
  4. After you say “My turn is over,” skip that player and go to the next living player after them.
You do not choose any player you want. YELLOW CARD only affects the next player in order.

Chapter 15 — Soccer Tokens & Getting Out

A successful attack gives the target 1 Soccer Token.

  • 0 Tokens: still playing.
  • 1 Token: still playing.
  • 2 Tokens: still playing, but one more successful attack will knock that player out.
  • 3 Tokens: that player is out immediately.

When a player is out, turn over their Role Card.

A player has 2 Tokens. You score on them once. Now they have 3 Tokens, so they are out and their secret role is shown.

Chapter 16 — How Do We Win?

Your team wins right away if either one happens:

  1. The other team's Goalkeeper reaches 3 Tokens and is out.
  2. Every player on the other team is out.
If the Goalkeeper is out, stop the game. The other team wins immediately.

Chapter 17 — One Full Turn, Step by Step

Turn order: Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2.

  1. It is Blue 1's turn. Blue 1 draws 1 card.
  2. Blue 1 has the Soccer Card.
  3. Blue 1 SHOOTs Green 1.
  4. Green 2 plays DEFENSE for Green 1.
  5. Blue 1 plays DRIBBLE PAST.
  6. Green 1 plays the second DEFENSE and discards 1 extra card.
  7. Green 1 gets the Soccer Card.
  8. It is still Blue 1's turn.
  9. If Blue 1 has TACKLE, Blue 1 may take the Soccer Card back and keep playing.
  10. When Blue 1 wants to stop, Blue 1 says “My turn is over.”
  11. Now Green 1 takes the next turn because Green 1 is next in the fixed order.

Chapter 18 — Ready Check

✓We made Blue and Green teams.
✓Each team has exactly 1 hidden Goalkeeper.
✓Everyone has 5 Action Cards.
✓Rock–Paper–Scissors chose the first team.
✓We know the fixed turn order.
✓We know PASS moves the ball, not the turn.
✓We know the active player says when their turn is over.
✓We know 3 Tokens = out.

Chapter 19 — Advanced Attack Combos

These are not new rules. They show how the cards can work together during one turn.

Combo 1 — Take the ball, then attack
TACKLE → SHOOT
1. It is your turn. An opponent has the Soccer Card.
2. Play TACKLE and take the Soccer Card.
3. If you have SHOOT, you may attack.
Combo 2 — Score, take the ball back, score again
SHOOT SUCCESS → TACKLE → SHOOT
1. Your first SHOOT works.
2. The target gets 1 Token and the Soccer Card.
3. Your turn is still going.
4. Play TACKLE and take the Soccer Card back.
5. If you have another SHOOT, attack again.
Combo 3 — They stop your first SHOOT
SHOOT → DEFENSE → TACKLE → SHOOT
1. You SHOOT.
2. They DEFEND. The defender gets the Soccer Card.
3. It is still your turn.
4. TACKLE the Soccer Card back.
5. SHOOT again if you have another SHOOT.
Combo 4 — First DEFENSE, then DRIBBLE PAST
SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → ATTACK SUCCEEDS → TACKLE → SHOOT
1. You SHOOT.
2. They play the first DEFENSE.
3. You play DRIBBLE PAST.
4. They cannot make the second DEFENSE.
5. Your SHOOT succeeds. The target gets 1 Token and the Soccer Card.
6. TACKLE it back if you want to keep attacking.
Combo 5 — Full defense chain, then take it back
SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → SECOND DEFENSE + DISCARD 1 → TACKLE → SHOOT
1. You SHOOT.
2. They DEFEND.
3. You use DRIBBLE PAST.
4. They play a second DEFENSE and discard 1 extra card.
5. The defender gets the Soccer Card.
6. It is still your turn.
7. TACKLE it back and SHOOT again if you have the cards.
Combo 6 — PASS without changing the turn order
PASS SOCCER → SAY “MY TURN IS OVER” → NEXT PLAYER IN ORDER
Example order: Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2.
Blue 1 PASSES the Soccer Card to Blue 2.
It is still Blue 1's turn.
Blue 1 says “My turn is over.”
Green 1 goes next. Blue 2 does not jump ahead.
Remember these 5 rules:
1. You need the Soccer Card before every SHOOT.
2. You decide when your own turn ends.
3. PASS moves the ball, not the turn.
4. TACKLE works only on your own turn and DEFENSE cannot stop it.
5. DRIBBLE PAST can be used only once during your whole turn.
