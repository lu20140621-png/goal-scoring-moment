1 — What Is the Goal?

Play in Blue and Green teams. Successful attacks normally give the target 1 Soccer Token. A normal Player with 3 Tokens is out. A player with 2 Tokens keeps the Role Card hidden. If the would-be third scoring attack hits a hidden Goalkeeper, reveal the Goalkeeper and use the one-time Goalkeeper Skill: that attack does not count, the Goalkeeper stays at 2 Tokens, and the Soccer Card goes to the Goalkeeper. After that skill is used, the next successful attack gives the third Token and eliminates the Goalkeeper. Eliminating the Goalkeeper wins immediately.

2 — Make 2 Teams

Make Blue and Green teams. Try to keep team sizes even.

3 — Secret Roles & Goalkeeper Skill

Each team has exactly 1 Goalkeeper. Everyone else is a Player. Role Cards stay hidden at 0, 1, and 2 Tokens.

GOALKEEPER SKILL: If a hidden Goalkeeper is hit by the successful attack that would normally give a third Token, reveal the Goalkeeper. Cancel that scoring attack once. The Goalkeeper stays at 2 Tokens and gets the Soccer Card. The skill is then used up. The next successful attack gives the third Token and eliminates the Goalkeeper.

4 — Set Up

  1. Put the Soccer Card aside.
  2. Shuffle Action Cards.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  4. Keep hands hidden.
  5. Make a Draw Pile and used-card area.
  6. Put Soccer Tokens where everyone can reach them.

5 — Who Starts?

Blue and Green play Rock–Paper–Scissors. The winning team chooses its first player. That player starts with the Soccer Card.

6 — Turn Order

Example: Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2.

Keep the same order. Moving the Soccer Card does not move the turn.

7 — Your Turn

  1. Draw 1 Action Card.
  2. Play any legal cards you want.
  3. When finished, say “My turn is over.”
  4. Only then does the next player begin.
PASS, SHOOT, scoring, DEFENSE, TACKLE, and Goalkeeper saves do not automatically end your turn.

8 — Soccer Card & PASS

The Soccer Card shows who has the ball. On your turn, if you have the ball, you may PASS it to a living teammate.

PASS moves possession only. It does not change the active player or turn order.

9 — SHOOT

  1. You must have the Soccer Card.
  2. Play SHOOT and choose a living opponent.
  3. The defending team may use DEFENSE.
  4. If not stopped, the target normally gets 1 Token and the Soccer Card.
If the would-be third Token reveals a Goalkeeper whose skill is unused, the Goalkeeper cancels that scoring attack once and takes the Soccer Card.

10 — DEFENSE

The target or a living teammate may play DEFENSE against SHOOT. If DEFENSE is the final successful defense, no Token is given and the defender gets the Soccer Card.

11 — DRIBBLE PAST

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST

After the first DEFENSE, the attacker may play DRIBBLE PAST once during that turn to continue the same attack.

12 — Second DEFENSE

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → DEFENSE + DISCARD 1

The second DEFENSE requires 1 DEFENSE card plus 1 extra discarded card. If successful, the second defender gets the Soccer Card.

13 — TACKLE

On your own turn, play TACKLE to take the Soccer Card from the player who has it. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE.

14 — YELLOW CARD

On your turn, YELLOW CARD skips the next living player in the fixed turn order. You do not choose any player you want.

15 — Tokens & Elimination

  • 0–2 Tokens: still playing; Role Card stays hidden.
  • Normal Player reaches 3: out and reveal Role Card.
  • Hidden Goalkeeper would reach 3: reveal Goalkeeper and use the one-time save instead; stay at 2 and take Soccer.
  • After the save is used, the next successful attack gives the third Token and eliminates the Goalkeeper.

16 — How to Win

Win immediately by eliminating the other team's Goalkeeper, or by eliminating every player on the other team.

17 — Full Turn Example

Blue 1 SHOOTs. Green 2 DEFENDs. Blue 1 uses DRIBBLE PAST. Green 1 uses the second DEFENSE and discards 1 extra card. Green 1 gets the Soccer Card. It is still Blue 1's turn. Blue 1 may TACKLE the ball back. Blue 1 decides when the turn ends.

18 — Ready Check

✓5 Action Cards each.
✓Fixed turn order.
✓PASS moves ball, not turn.
✓2 Tokens keeps Role hidden.
✓Goalkeeper saves the would-be third scoring attack once and takes the ball.

19 — Advanced Combos

TACKLE → SHOOT
SHOOT SUCCESS → TACKLE → SHOOT
SHOOT → DEFENSE → TACKLE → SHOOT
SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → TACKLE → SHOOT

20 — Goalkeeper Save → TACKLE → SHOOT

SHOOT → GK SAVE → BALL TO GK → TACKLE → SHOOT → 3 TOKENS → WIN

After the Goalkeeper save, the Goalkeeper has the Soccer Card. If your turn is still active and you have TACKLE, steal the ball back. If you also have another SHOOT, attack again. The Goalkeeper skill is already used, so a successful attack gives the third Token and wins.
