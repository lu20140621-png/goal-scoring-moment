1 — Goal

Attack the other team. A normal successful attack gives the target 1 Soccer Token. A normal Player with 3 Tokens is out. Eliminate the other team's Goalkeeper, or eliminate every opponent, to win.

2 — Teams & Secret Roles

Make Blue and Green teams. Each team has exactly 1 Goalkeeper; everyone else is a Player. Players know team colors, but opponent Role Cards stay hidden at 0, 1, and 2 Tokens.

3 — Set Up

  1. Shuffle Action Cards.
  2. Deal 5 Action Cards to every player.
  3. Keep hands hidden.
  4. Put Soccer Tokens where everyone can reach them.
  5. The Soccer Card shows possession; it is not a normal hand card.

4 — Start & Turn Order

Use Rock–Paper–Scissors. The winning team attacks first and its first player gets the Soccer Card. Set a fixed alternating order such as Blue 1 → Green 1 → Blue 2 → Green 2.

5 — Your Turn

  1. Draw 1 Action Card.
  2. Play any legal cards you want.
  3. Your turn ends only when you say, “My turn is over.”
PASS, SHOOT, scoring, DEFENSE, TACKLE, and Goalkeeper saves do not automatically end your turn.

6 — PASS

If you have the Soccer Card on your turn, you may pass it to a living teammate. PASS moves the ball only. The receiver does not act immediately. The fixed turn order stays the same.

7 — SHOOT

  1. You must have the Soccer Card.
  2. Play SHOOT and choose a living opponent.
  3. The defending team may use DEFENSE.
  4. If not stopped, the target normally gets 1 Token and the Soccer Card.

8 — DEFENSE

The target or a living teammate may play DEFENSE against SHOOT. If DEFENSE is the final successful defense, no Token is given and the player who played that DEFENSE gets the Soccer Card.

9 — DRIBBLE PAST

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST

In Strategy Mode, DRIBBLE PAST does not look at the Draw Pile. It breaks through the first DEFENSE so the same attack can continue. You may use DRIBBLE PAST only once during your whole turn.

10 — Second DEFENSE

SHOOT → DEFENSE → DRIBBLE PAST → DEFENSE + DISCARD 1

After DRIBBLE PAST, the defending side may use a second DEFENSE only by also discarding 1 extra hand card. If it works, the second defender gets the Soccer Card.

11 — TACKLE

On your own turn, TACKLE steals the Soccer Card from the player who has it. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE. TACKLE is not a reaction card.

12 — YELLOW CARD

Use YELLOW on your own turn. It skips the next living player in the fixed turn order once. You do not choose any player you want.

13 — Tokens & Goalkeeper Skill

  • 0–2 Tokens: still playing; Role Card stays hidden.
  • Normal Player reaches 3: out; reveal Role Card.
  • If a hidden Goalkeeper would receive the third Token, reveal the Goalkeeper and activate the one-time save.
  • That scoring attack does not count. Goalkeeper stays at 2 Tokens and gets the Soccer Card.
  • The Goalkeeper Skill is now used.
  • The next successful attack against that Goalkeeper gives the third Token and eliminates the Goalkeeper.

14 — How to Win

Your team wins immediately when the opposing Goalkeeper is eliminated. You also win if every player on the opposing team is eliminated.

15 — Goalkeeper Finish Combo

SHOOT → GOALKEEPER SAVE → BALL TO GOALKEEPER → TACKLE → SHOOT → 3 TOKENS → WIN

After the Goalkeeper saves, the Goalkeeper has the Soccer Card. Your turn is still active. If you have TACKLE, steal the ball back. If you also have another SHOOT, attack again. The Goalkeeper Skill is already used, so the next successful attack gives the third Token and wins.
