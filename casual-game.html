COURSES

CASUAL PARTY MODE

Goal

Draw Soccer twice and you are out. Last active player wins.

Your Turn

Play any legal Action Cards you want, or none. Your own final draw ends your turn. A forced draw caused by another effect does not end the original active player’s turn.

Soccer + Defense

Whenever Soccer is drawn, resolve the strike, then return the Soccer card to the Draw Pile at position 2 or deeper — never on top. On your second Soccer you are eliminated unless you use DEFENSE; DEFENSE keeps you at 1 strike before Soccer is reinserted.

Dribble Past

Look at the actual top 3 cards in order. Do not reorder them.

Shoot

Choose yourself or any other active player. The chosen player immediately draws 1 card from the Draw Pile. This forced draw does not end the original active player’s turn.

Tackle

Choose another active player with at least 1 card and steal 1 random card from their hand.

Yellow

YELLOW cancels the immediately previous Action/effect before it fully resolves. YELLOW may also cancel another YELLOW.

Elimination / Empty Pile

Eliminated players discard their remaining hand and take no more turns. If the Draw Pile runs out, keep the top discard separate and shuffle the other discarded Action Cards into a new Draw Pile.
