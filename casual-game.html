CASUAL PARTY MODE — START HERE

DO THIS BEFORE THE FIRST TURN

  1. Choose the players and sit in a fixed turn order. Decide who takes the first turn before play begins.
  2. Prepare the Casual Action deck. The playable Action cards are DEFENSE, DRIBBLE PAST, SHOOT, YELLOW, TACKLE, plus the SOCCER card used as the danger card.
  3. Put the Draw Pile face-down in the middle. The top card is the next card that will be drawn.
  4. Make a Discard Pile beside it. Played Action Cards go here unless a rule says otherwise.
  5. Each player keeps their own hand hidden. Your hand is separate from the Draw Pile and Discard Pile.

SETUP NOTE: The current Casual rules on the website do not define one official starting-hand size. Do not guess from the tutorial hand; the tutorial uses teaching cards. The turn rules below are the official flow.

YOUR TURN — ALWAYS FOLLOW THESE STEPS

  1. PLAY ACTION CARDS FIRST — OPTIONAL. You may play any legal Action Cards you want, one at a time, or play none.
  2. FINISH EACH ACTION COMPLETELY. If somebody reacts with YELLOW, or a card makes somebody draw, resolve that whole effect before doing anything else.
  3. WHEN YOU ARE DONE PLAYING ACTIONS, DRAW 1 CARD YOURSELF. Take exactly the top card from the face-down Draw Pile and add/resolve it.
  4. YOUR OWN DRAW ENDS YOUR TURN. After that draw is completely resolved, the next active player begins their turn.

IMPORTANT: A card drawn because SHOOT forced someone to draw does not end the original active player’s turn. Only the active player’s own final draw ends that turn.

WHAT DOES “DRAW 1” MEAN?

Take exactly 1 card from the top of the face-down Draw Pile. Do not choose a card from the middle. Do not take from the Discard Pile. If it is a normal Action Card, add it to your hand. If it is SOCCER, resolve SOCCER immediately.

IF YOU DRAW SOCCER

  1. First SOCCER: you now have 1 Soccer strike and stay in the game.
  2. Second SOCCER: you are OUT unless you legally use DEFENSE before the second strike eliminates you.
  3. After SOCCER resolves: put the SOCCER card back into the Draw Pile at position 2 or deeper. Never put it back on top.
  4. If you are eliminated: discard your remaining hand and take no more turns.

Last active player remaining wins.

DEFENSE

When SOCCER would give you your second strike, play DEFENSE to survive. You remain at 1 Soccer strike. Then return SOCCER to the Draw Pile at position 2 or deeper — never on top.

DRIBBLE PAST

Play DRIBBLE PAST to look at the actual top 3 cards of the Draw Pile in order: #1 is the next card to be drawn. Look only. Do not reorder the cards.

SHOOT

  1. Play SHOOT.
  2. Choose yourself or any other active player.
  3. That chosen player immediately draws the top card of the Draw Pile.
  4. Resolve that forced draw completely.
  5. The original active player’s turn continues. That forced draw did not end the turn.

TACKLE

  1. Play TACKLE.
  2. Choose another active player who has at least 1 card.
  3. Steal 1 random card from that player’s hand and add it to your own hand.

YELLOW

YELLOW cancels the immediately previous Action/effect before it fully resolves. Another YELLOW may cancel that YELLOW, so the chain may continue: Action → YELLOW → YELLOW → YELLOW… Each YELLOW cancels the card/effect immediately before it.

FIRST-TURN EXAMPLE

PLAYER 1 starts. PLAYER 1 may play DRIBBLE PAST and look at the top 3. Then PLAYER 1 may play SHOOT and force PLAYER 3 to draw 1. PLAYER 3’s forced draw resolves, but PLAYER 1 is still taking the turn. When PLAYER 1 is done playing actions, PLAYER 1 draws exactly 1 card from the top of the Draw Pile. That personal draw resolves and then PLAYER 1’s turn ends. PLAYER 2 now starts.

EMPTY DRAW PILE

If the Draw Pile runs out, keep the top card of the Discard Pile separate and shuffle the other discarded Action Cards into a new face-down Draw Pile.
