CASUAL PARTY MODE

COMPLETE RULEBOOK

Goal

Draw SOCCER twice and you are eliminated. The last active player wins. Free-for-all; no Goalkeeper special ability.

Set Up

  1. 2–8 players.
  2. Shuffle Action Cards.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  4. Keep hands secret.
  5. Put SOCCER into the Draw Pile, not on top.

Your Turn

Play any legal Action Cards you want, including none. When finished, draw exactly 1 card from the top. Your own final draw ends your turn.

A draw forced by SHOOT does not end the active player's turn.

SOCCER + DEFENSE

SOCCER resolves immediately. The player who drew it may immediately use DEFENSE. Without DEFENSE, take 1 Soccer Strike. A second Strike eliminates that player. If DEFENSE is used, that player puts SOCCER back at position 2 or deeper.

DRIBBLE PAST

Look at the actual top 3 Draw Pile cards. Do not rearrange them.

SHOOT

Choose any active player. That player immediately draws 1. Resolve SOCCER immediately if drawn. Then the original active player's turn continues.

TACKLE

Choose another active player with at least 1 card and steal exactly 1 random hand card. TACKLE does not steal the Soccer Card in this mode.

YELLOW CARD

YELLOW cancels the immediately previous Action Card or YELLOW before that effect resolves. YELLOW may cancel another YELLOW.

Win

At 2 Soccer Strikes, discard the eliminated player's remaining hand. The last active player wins.
