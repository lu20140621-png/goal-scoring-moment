START HERE — DO THIS IN ORDER

FIRST GAME?
DO NOT TRY TO MEMORIZE EVERY CARD.Set up the table first. Then follow Step 1, Step 2, Step 3… When the first turn begins, every player always starts by drawing 1 Action Card.
FIRST: KNOW THE 3 DIFFERENT KINDS OF CARDS
ROLE CARDS

PLAYER and GOALKEEPER tell you who you are. They are not Action Cards and are not part of your Action hand.

SOCCER CARD

This is the ball. It shows who has possession. Keep it separate from the Action deck.

ACTION CARDS

SHOOT, DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and YELLOW. These are the cards you shuffle, deal, draw, hold in your hand, and play.

SET UP THE GAME — ONE STEP AT A TIME
1MAKE TWO TEAMS

Split everyone into BLUE and GREEN as evenly as possible. Each team must have exactly 1 Goalkeeper Role Card; all other Role Cards are Players.

2GIVE EVERY PLAYER 1 ROLE CARD

Each player gets one Role Card and looks at it. Keep Role Cards hidden from opponents. Do not mix Role Cards into the Action deck.

3PUT THE SOCCER CARD ASIDE

The Soccer Card is not shuffled. It is the possession marker and will be handed from player to player during the game.

4SHUFFLE ONLY THE ACTION CARDS

Shuffle SHOOT, DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and YELLOW together. This becomes the Action deck.

5DEAL 5 ACTION CARDS TO EACH PLAYER

These 5 cards are your starting hand. Hold them so other players cannot see them. Put all remaining Action Cards face-down in the middle. That face-down stack is the DRAW PILE.

6SET THE TURN ORDER

Choose the fixed circle of players before the game starts. Alternate BLUE and GREEN when possible. Keep this same order for the whole game.

7ROCK–PAPER–SCISSORS FOR THE START

The winning side starts the game and receives the Soccer Card. The player taking the first turn is now ready to begin.

8START THE FIRST TURN

Before playing anything, DRAW 1. Take the top card from the face-down Draw Pile and add it to your hand. Then decide what legal action you want to take.

WHAT DOES “DRAW 1” MEAN?

Take exactly 1 card from the TOP of the face-down Action Draw Pile and put it into your hand. Do not draw a Role Card. Do not draw the Soccer Card. Do not automatically play the card you just drew. You simply add it to your hand, then continue your turn.

THE MOST IMPORTANT BEGINNER RULE: Having the Soccer Card does not decide whose turn it is. The fixed turn order decides whose turn it is. When your normal turn starts, draw 1 first — even if you already have the Soccer Card.

1 — GOAL OF THE GAME

WHO ARE YOU TRYING TO ELIMINATE?
NORMAL PLAYER

The Role Card stays hidden at 0, 1, and 2 Tokens. A successful third Token eliminates that player. Only after the player is eliminated do they reveal their Role Card.

0→1→2→3 OUT
GOALKEEPER

The moment the opposing Goalkeeper is finally eliminated, your team wins immediately. Stop the game at once.

0→1→2→GK OUT
A successful goal gives the targeted player 1 Soccer Token. The opposing Goalkeeper is the true win condition.

4 — YOUR NORMAL TURN: DO THESE STEPS EVERY TIME

STEP 1 IS ALWAYS DRAW 1

Unless YELLOW skipped your turn, take the top card from the face-down Action Draw Pile and add it to your hand. Do this before PASS, SHOOT, TACKLE, or any other normal on-turn action.

🃏1 · DRAW 1 ACTION CARD

Top card of the face-down Draw Pile → into your hand.

⚽2 · LOOK FOR THE SOCCER CARD

If you hold Soccer, you have possession. If someone else holds it, they have possession.

🎴3 · CHOOSE A LEGAL ACTION

With Soccer: you may PASS or use SHOOT. Without Soccer: TACKLE can take it. Other legal cards may also be played when their timing allows.

🔗4 · FINISH THAT ACTION COMPLETELY

If somebody reacts with DEFENSE or DRIBBLE PAST, finish the whole chain before doing anything else.

➡️5 · END YOUR TURN WHEN YOU ARE DONE

Say “END TURN.” The next living player in the fixed order now begins at Step 1 and draws 1.

IF YOU HAVE THE SOCCER CARD

You have possession, but that does not give you an extra turn. Wait until your place in the fixed turn order. On your turn, draw 1 first, then act.

IF YOU DO NOT HAVE THE SOCCER CARD

You still take your normal turn. Draw 1 first. You may use legal cards such as TACKLE when allowed, or end your turn if you do not want or cannot take another legal action.

PASS, scoring, being defended, TACKLE, or losing possession does not automatically end your turn. Finish the current action, then decide whether to take another legal action or announce END TURN.

5 — ACTION CARDS

MATCH THE RULE TO THE CARD IN YOUR HAND

PASS

On your own turn, if you hold Soccer, give it to a living teammate. Your turn continues.

NO PASS CARD — PASS IS AN ACTION

SHOOT

On your own turn, you must hold Soccer. Choose one living opponent and give the defending team a reaction chance.

DEFENSE

The target or any living teammate may use it. A successful DEFENSE stops the goal and gives Soccer to the player who actually defended.

DRIBBLE PAST

After the first DEFENSE, beat that DEFENSE and continue the same attack. It does not look at the top of the deck in Strategy Mode.

SECOND DEFENSE

After DRIBBLE PAST, any one living defender may DEFENSE again. If cards remain after playing it, discard 1 additional card.

HAND BECOMES 0? NO EXTRA DISCARD

TACKLE

On your own turn, take Soccer from whoever currently has it. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE. Your turn continues.

YELLOW CARD

Affects the next living player in fixed turn order. You do not choose the target.

If the second defender still has cards after playing DEFENSE, discard 1 additional card. If playing DEFENSE leaves that hand at 0, no extra discard is required.

8 — TOKENS, ELIMINATION & ROLE REVEAL

THE ROLE STAYS HIDDEN UNTIL THE PLAYER IS OUT

DO NOT REVEAL AT 1 OR 2 TOKENS

Every player's Role Card stays hidden while that player is still alive. 0 Tokens = hidden. 1 Token = hidden. 2 Tokens = still hidden.

0 HIDDEN→1 HIDDEN→2 HIDDEN→3 OUT
WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THE THIRD TOKEN IS SCORED?
1 · TARGET RECEIVES THE 3RD TOKEN
→
2 · THAT PLAYER IS ELIMINATED
→
3 · NOW REVEAL THE ROLE CARD
→
4 · PLAYER = GAME CONTINUES · GOALKEEPER = GAME ENDS
  • 0 Tokens: alive; Role Card stays hidden.
  • 1 Token: alive; Role Card stays hidden.
  • 2 Tokens: alive; Role Card still stays hidden. Do not reveal it.
  • 3 Tokens: eliminate that player first, then reveal the Role Card.
  • If the revealed role is PLAYER: discard that player's entire hand, skip that seat from now on, and continue the game.
  • If the revealed role is GOALKEEPER: that team loses immediately. Stop the game at once.
NO EARLY ROLE REVEAL: A player does not reveal their identity at 1 or 2 Tokens. Identity is shown only after elimination at the third Token.

9 — WHAT IF SOMETHING RUNS OUT?

🔄DRAW PILE EMPTY

Shuffle all Action Cards in the Discard Pile into a new face-down Draw Pile, then draw normally. Never shuffle Role Cards, Soccer, Tokens, hands, or cards currently resolving.

0️⃣YOU HAVE 0 CARDS

You are still alive. On your next normal turn draw 1 first. If you hold Soccer, PASS is still legal even with no Action Cards.

🛡️SECOND DEFENSE = LAST CARD

It is legal. Play the DEFENSE; if your hand becomes 0, you do not pay the extra discard.
