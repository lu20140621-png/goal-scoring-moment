1 — GOAL OF THE GAME

WHO ARE YOU TRYING TO ELIMINATE?
NORMAL PLAYER

Roles stay hidden at 0–1 Tokens. Reveal immediately at 2 Tokens. A successful third Token eliminates that Player.

0→1→2→3 OUT
GOALKEEPER

The moment the opposing Goalkeeper is finally eliminated, your team wins immediately. Stop the game at once.

0→1→2→GK OUT
A successful goal gives the targeted player 1 Soccer Token. The opposing Goalkeeper is the true win condition.

4 — YOUR NORMAL TURN

🃏1 · DRAW 1

Draw first unless YELLOW skips your turn.

⚽2 · CHECK POSSESSION

See who currently holds Soccer.

🎴3 · PLAY LEGAL ACTIONS

You may play multiple legal actions.

🔗4 · FINISH THE CHAIN

Resolve an attack/reaction chain completely.

➡️5 · END TURN

Announce the end, then move to the next living seat.

PASS, scoring, being defended, TACKLE, or losing possession does not automatically end your turn.

5 — ACTION CARDS

MATCH THE RULE TO THE CARD IN YOUR HAND

PASS

On your own turn, if you hold Soccer, give it to a living teammate. Your turn continues.

NO PASS CARD — PASS IS AN ACTION

SHOOT

On your own turn, you must hold Soccer. Choose one living opponent and give the defending team a reaction chance.

DEFENSE

The target or any living teammate may use it. A successful DEFENSE stops the goal and gives Soccer to the player who actually defended.

DRIBBLE PAST

After the first DEFENSE, beat that DEFENSE and continue the same attack. It does not look at the top of the deck in Strategy Mode.

SECOND DEFENSE

After DRIBBLE PAST, any one living defender may DEFENSE again. If cards remain after playing it, discard 1 additional card.

HAND BECOMES 0? NO EXTRA DISCARD

TACKLE

On your own turn, take Soccer from whoever currently has it. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE. Your turn continues.

YELLOW CARD

Affects the next living player in fixed turn order. You do not choose the target.

SECOND DEFENSE COST

If the second defender still has cards after playing DEFENSE, discard 1 additional card. If playing DEFENSE leaves that hand at 0, no extra discard is required.

After any attack fully resolves, if it is still your turn and another player now has Soccer, you may legally TACKLE, take possession, and continue with another legal action such as SHOOT.

8 — TOKENS, REVEAL, ELIMINATION & GOALKEEPER

GOALKEEPER PASSIVE

At 2 Tokens, reveal the Goalkeeper and automatically arm the one-time passive.

0→1→2 REVEAL
WHAT HAPPENS TO THE NEXT THIRD-TOKEN ATTACK?
1 · OPPONENT ATTACK WOULD GIVE GK A THIRD TOKEN
→
2 · PASSIVE AUTO-CANCELS THAT ATTACK ONCE
→
3 · NO THIRD TOKEN · SOCCER GOES TO GOALKEEPER
→
4 · PASSIVE IS USED UP · NEXT SUCCESSFUL THIRD-TOKEN GOAL ELIMINATES GK
  • 0 Tokens: alive, role hidden.
  • 1 Token: alive, role hidden.
  • 2 Tokens: reveal the role immediately.
  • Normal Player: the next successful third Token eliminates that Player. Discard that player's entire hand and skip that seat forever.
  • Goalkeeper: after the second Token, reveal the Goalkeeper and arm the passive automatically.
When the opposing Goalkeeper is eliminated, stop immediately. The game is over — do not continue the current turn.

9 — WHAT IF SOMETHING RUNS OUT?

🔄DRAW PILE EMPTY

Shuffle all Action Cards in the Discard Pile into a new face-down Draw Pile, then draw normally. Never shuffle Role Cards, Soccer, Tokens, hands, or cards currently resolving.

0️⃣YOU HAVE 0 CARDS

You are still alive. On your next normal turn draw 1 first. If you hold Soccer, PASS is still legal even with no Action Cards.

🛡️SECOND DEFENSE = LAST CARD

It is legal. Play the DEFENSE; if your hand becomes 0, you do not pay the extra discard.
