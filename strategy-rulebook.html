START HERE — DO THIS IN ORDER

FIRST GAME?
DO NOT TRY TO MEMORIZE EVERY CARD.Set up the table first, then follow the steps in order. Whenever a player's turn begins, that player must first draw 1 Action Card from the top of the Draw Pile. Only after drawing may that player take normal actions.
FIRST: KNOW THE 3 DIFFERENT KINDS OF CARDS
ROLE CARDS

PLAYER and GOALKEEPER tell you who you are. They are not Action Cards and are not part of your Action hand.

SOCCER CARD

This is the ball. It shows who has possession. Keep it separate from the Action deck.

ACTION CARDS

SHOOT, DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and YELLOW. These are the cards you shuffle, deal, draw, hold in your hand, and play.

SET UP THE GAME — ONE STEP AT A TIME
1MAKE TWO TEAMS

Split everyone into BLUE and GREEN as evenly as possible. Each team must have exactly 1 Goalkeeper Role Card; all other Role Cards are Players.

2GIVE EVERY PLAYER 1 ROLE CARD

For each team, prepare 1 GOALKEEPER plus enough PLAYER Role Cards for everyone else on that team. Shuffle each team’s Role Cards separately and deal 1 to each teammate. Keep roles hidden from opponents; teammates may privately share and know one another’s roles. Do not shuffle Role Cards into the Action deck.

3PUT THE SOCCER CARD ASIDE

Do not shuffle the Soccer Card into the Action deck. It marks possession and moves from player to player during the game.

4SHUFFLE ONLY THE ACTION CARDS

Shuffle SHOOT, DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE PAST, and YELLOW together. This becomes the Action deck.

5DEAL 5 ACTION CARDS TO EACH PLAYER

These 5 cards are your starting hand. There is no maximum hand size during the game. Hold them so other players cannot see them. Put all remaining Action Cards face-down in the middle. That face-down stack is the DRAW PILE. Leave space beside it for a face-up DISCARD PILE.

6SET THE TURN ORDER

Choose the fixed circle of players before the game starts. Alternate BLUE and GREEN when possible. Keep this same order for the whole game.

7ROCK–PAPER–SCISSORS FOR THE START

Use Rock–Paper–Scissors to determine one starting player. That player receives the Soccer Card and takes the first turn: draw 1 first, then take legal actions. Continue from that seat in the fixed turn order; do not rearrange the seats.

8START THE FIRST TURN

Before taking any normal action, DRAW 1. Take the top card from the face-down Draw Pile and add it to your hand. Then choose a legal action.

WHAT DOES “DRAW 1” MEAN?

Take exactly 1 card from the TOP of the face-down Action Draw Pile and add it to your hand. Do not draw a Role Card or the Soccer Card. You do not have to play the card you just drew. Add it to your hand, then continue your turn.

TEAM RULES — PASSING & ELIMINATED PLAYERS

TWO RULES EVERY TEAM MUST KNOW
⚽➡️🤝

PASS TO A TEAMMATE — NO CARD NEEDED

If you have the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a living teammate only during your own turn. PASS does not use an Action Card. The immediate ball handoff when a PLAYER is eliminated is an exception to the own-turn rule; see below.

YOUR TURN STARTS→DRAW 1 FIRST→PASS SOCCER
IMPORTANT: The teammate who receives the Soccer Card gains possession, but does not immediately take a turn.
3️⃣💥🃏🤝

PLAYER OUT — TRANSFER THE BALL & HAND

When a normal PLAYER receives the 3rd Token, eliminate that player first, then reveal the Role Card. Give the eliminated player's remaining Action Cards to a living teammate. If they received the Soccer Card from that goal, they immediately pass it to a living teammate of their choice before play continues. This elimination handoff is allowed outside their own turn and does not start the recipient’s turn.

3RD TOKEN→OUT→REVEAL ROLE→BALL + HAND → TEAMMATE
STILL PART OF THE TEAM: An eliminated player may still talk with the team, discuss strategy, and give advice. They may not take turns or play cards.

4 — YOUR NORMAL TURN: DO THESE STEPS EVERY TIME

STEP 1 IS ALWAYS DRAW 1

Unless YELLOW caused you to skip your turn, take the top card from the face-down Action Draw Pile and add it to your hand. Do this before PASS, SHOOT, TACKLE, or any other normal action on your turn.

🃏1 · DRAW 1 ACTION CARD

Take the top card of the face-down Draw Pile and add it to your hand.

⚽2 · LOOK FOR THE SOCCER CARD

If you hold the Soccer Card, you have possession. If another player holds it, that player has possession.

🎴3 · CHOOSE A LEGAL ACTION

If you have the Soccer Card, you may PASS it to a living teammate for free or play SHOOT, but only during your own turn. PASS does not require a card. If you do not have the Soccer Card, you may use TACKLE to take possession. Other cards may be played when their timing allows.

🔗4 · FINISH THAT ACTION COMPLETELY

Resolve the defending team’s DEFENSE and the original attacker’s optional DRIBBLE PAST completely before taking another normal action.

➡️5 · END YOUR TURN WHEN YOU ARE DONE

Say “END TURN.” Play moves to the next living player in the fixed turn order, skipping any turn affected by YELLOW. The next normal turn starts with DRAW 1.

IF YOU HAVE THE SOCCER CARD

You have possession, but that does not give you an extra turn. Wait for your place in the fixed turn order. When your turn begins, draw 1 card first, then take your actions.

IF YOU DO NOT HAVE THE SOCCER CARD

You still take your normal turn. Draw 1 card first. You may then play any legal card, such as TACKLE when allowed, or end your turn if you cannot or do not want to take another action.

NO ACTION LIMIT: After drawing 1, you may take as many legal actions as you want. There is no one-card-per-turn limit. Keep taking legal actions, or say “END TURN” when you decide to stop. Playing a reaction during another player’s turn does not start your own turn.
Passing, scoring, having your attack stopped, using TACKLE, or losing possession does not automatically end your turn. Finish the current action, then either take another legal action or announce “END TURN.”

5 — ACTION CARDS

PLAY → RESOLVE → DISCARD: Play Action Cards face-up. Keep cards in the current attack chain visible until the entire attack resolves, then put all played Action Cards in the face-up Discard Pile. Other Action Cards go there when their effect finishes resolving; extra discard costs also go there. Never discard the Soccer Card or Role Cards. When the Draw Pile runs out, shuffle the discarded Action Cards to form a new face-down Draw Pile.
MATCH THE RULE TO THE CARD IN YOUR HAND

PASS

PASS is free and does not require a card. If you hold the Soccer Card, you may give it to a living teammate only during your own turn after your required draw. Your turn continues after the pass. Exception: an eliminated PLAYER immediately hands the ball to a living teammate, even outside their own turn.

NO PASS CARD · OWN TURN ONLY

SHOOT

On your own turn, you must have the Soccer Card to play SHOOT. Choose 1 living opponent as the target, then give the defending team a chance to respond.

DEFENSE

The target or any living teammate may play DEFENSE. Teammates may communicate and choose who plays it; play only one DEFENSE at each response opportunity. If it successfully stops the attack, the player who played DEFENSE receives the Soccer Card.

DRIBBLE PAST

Only the player who played SHOOT may play DRIBBLE PAST, after the first DEFENSE. It bypasses that DEFENSE and continues the same attack. A teammate cannot play it for you. Maximum 1 DRIBBLE PAST per SHOOT; the defending team may then play one final DEFENSE.

SECOND DEFENSE

After DRIBBLE PAST, the target or any living teammate may play the second and final DEFENSE. This may be the same defender or a different teammate. Play 1 DEFENSE + discard 1 additional Action Card if you still have one.

HAND BECOMES 0? NO EXTRA DISCARD

TACKLE

TACKLE has only one function: take the Soccer Card from another player on your own turn. It cannot stop SHOOT and is not a defensive response. DEFENSE cannot stop TACKLE. Your turn continues, with no fixed limit on regaining possession and attacking again as long as each action is legal.

YELLOW CARD

Play YELLOW only on your own turn. The next living player in the fixed turn order skips one normal turn. You do not choose the target. Multiple YELLOW cards on the same turn still skip only that same player’s one turn.

7 — YELLOW CARD

YELLOW FOLLOWS THE SEAT ORDER

Play YELLOW only during your own normal turn. The next living player in the fixed turn order skips exactly one normal turn. You do not choose a target.

GREEN 2PLAY CONTINUES HERE

In Example 2, BLUE 2 is the next living player, so YELLOW skips that teammate. The seating order has not changed.

YELLOW DOES NOT STACK: Play 1, 2, or 3 YELLOW cards on the same turn → the same next living player still skips only 1 normal turn. Extra YELLOW cards never affect the second or third player after you.
The skipped player does not draw a card for that turn.
The skipped player cannot PASS, SHOOT, TACKLE, play DRIBBLE PAST, or take any other normal action on that turn.
The skipped player is still in the game and may play DEFENSE as a reaction if their team is attacked.
After the skipped player's turn, continue to the next living player in the fixed turn order.

  • 0 Tokens: still in the game; Role Card stays hidden from opponents.
  • 1 Token: still in the game; Role Card stays hidden from opponents.
  • 2 Tokens: still in the game; Role Card stays hidden from opponents. Do not reveal it to opponents.
  • 3 Tokens: eliminate the player first, then reveal the Role Card.
  • If the revealed role is PLAYER: that player is out. They immediately pass the Soccer Card to a living teammate of their choice and give their remaining Action Cards to a living teammate. OPPONENTS ONLY: Teammates may privately share their roles at any time. Reveal your Role Card to opponents only after elimination at the third Token.

9 — WHAT IF SOMETHING RUNS OUT?

🔄DRAW PILE EMPTY

Shuffle all Action Cards from the Discard Pile into a new face-down Draw Pile, then continue drawing normally. Never shuffle Role Cards, the Soccer Card, Tokens, cards in players' hands, or cards that are currently resolving.

0️⃣YOU HAVE 0 CARDS

You are still in the game. At the start of your next normal turn, draw 1 card first. If you have the Soccer Card, you may still PASS because PASS does not require a card — but you may PASS only during your own turn.

🛡️SECOND DEFENSE = LAST CARD

That is legal. Play DEFENSE. If that leaves your hand empty, you do not have to pay the extra discard.

10 — QUICK FAQ

CAN A TEAMMATE PLAY DEFENSE FOR ME?Yes. Any living teammate may play DEFENSE for you.
WHO CHOOSES THE DEFENDER?The target and living teammates discuss and choose one player.
DOES THE SAME PLAYER HAVE TO PLAY DEFENSE TWICE?No. The target and living teammates may agree on a different defender for the second DEFENSE.
CAN DEFENSE STOP TACKLE?No.
CAN YELLOW SKIP MY TEAMMATE?Yes.
CAN A PLAYER WHO WAS SKIPPED BY YELLOW STILL PLAY DEFENSE?Yes. DEFENSE is a reaction card.
DOES PASS CHANGE WHOSE TURN IT IS?No. Normal PASS is free and allowed only during the Soccer holder’s own turn. An eliminated PLAYER immediately hands the ball to a living teammate as an exception; this does not start the recipient’s turn.
WHAT HAPPENS TO AN ELIMINATED PLAYER'S HAND?Give the eliminated player's remaining Action Cards to a living teammate. If they received the Soccer Card from that goal, they immediately pass it to a living teammate of their choice before play continues. This elimination handoff is allowed outside their own turn and does not start the recipient’s turn. The eliminated player may continue giving advice, but may not take turns or play cards.
DOES LOSING POSSESSION END MY TURN?No. You may use TACKLE to regain possession and keep taking legal actions until you say “END TURN.”
