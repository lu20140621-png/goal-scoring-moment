MATCH MODE

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

A complete rulebook for new players. Learn positions, attacks, defense, possession and scoring.

1. GAME OBJECTIVE

Two teams compete for Soccer possession. Use teamwork, passing and tactical cards to break defensive lines and score goals.

This mode is designed for players who want a deeper soccer strategy experience.

2. FIELD POSITIONS

ATTACKER → DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER
Defender 1: First defensive wall.
Defender 2: Second defensive wall.
Goalkeeper: Last chance. Must protect the goal.

3. CARD ENCYCLOPEDIA

SOCCER

The ball. The team holding Soccer controls possession.

SHOOT

Starts an attack toward the opponent goal.

DEFENSE

Stops this SHOOT only. Soccer stays with attacker.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer.

DRIBBLE PAST

Used to beat a defender after a challenge.

YELLOW

Cancels DRIBBLE or YELLOW on the current defensive line.

4. STARTING THE MATCH

Players receive equal cards. Extra cards are discarded. Teammates may exchange cards before the match starts.

Use Rock-Paper-Scissors to decide who starts with Soccer.

5. COMPLETE ATTACK PROCESS

SHOOT → D1 → D2 → GK → GOAL

D1 Defense

DEFENSE stops the SHOOT. The attacker keeps Soccer.

TACKLE steals Soccer. New possession starts from D1.

D2 Defense

D2 has the same choices. If D2 does nothing, the attack continues.

Goalkeeper

GK DEFENSE requires Rock-Paper-Scissors to decide SAVE or GOAL.

6. DRIBBLE AND CHASE BACK

After a defender challenges the attack, the attacker may use DRIBBLE PAST.

DEFENDER CHALLENGE → DRIBBLE → BREAK LINE

After being beaten, the defender may discard one card to chase back. If they do not, the attack continues.

7. YELLOW CARD

Yellow is defensive timing, not a universal cancel.

D1 Yellow only affects D1. D2 Yellow only affects D2.

Yellow cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.

Yellow can cancel another Yellow.

8. POSSESSION CHANGE

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW SOCCER OWNER → RESET TO D1

Only TACKLE changes possession. DEFENSE only stops the current attack.

9. GOALKEEPER RULES

The goalkeeper may also attack when holding Soccer.

If GK loses Soccer while attacking, GK must discard a card to return before defending.

10. GAME END

The match ends after 10 minutes, or when both teams cannot create a valid attack.

A team losing only SHOOT cards does not immediately end the match.

11. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

When tied, use a five-round penalty shootout.

Each shooter draws one card. The opposing goalkeeper draws one card.

Highest goal count after five rounds wins. Continue sudden death if tied.

12. EXAMPLES

Q: Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
No. Only TACKLE steals Soccer.
Q: After TACKLE where does attack restart?
Always from Defender 1.
Q: Can Yellow stop SHOOT?
No.
