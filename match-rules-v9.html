MATCH MODE RULEBOOK V9

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

Control Soccer. Break Lines. Defend Smart. Every Decision Matters.

CARD ENCYCLOPEDIA

SOCCER

The ball. The team holding Soccer controls possession.

SHOOT

Starts an attack toward D1, D2 and Goalkeeper.

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT. Soccer stays with attacker.

TACKLE

Steals Soccer and creates a new possession.

DRIBBLE PAST

Beats a defender after a defensive action.

YELLOW

Cancels DRIBBLE or YELLOW on the current defensive line only.

1. MATCH SETUP

3 players per team: Defender 1, Defender 2, Goalkeeper.

Deal equal cards. Teammates may exchange cards one-for-one. Extra cards are discarded.

Rock-Paper-Scissors decides the first Soccer possession.

2. POSSESSION & PASS

PLAYER WITH SOCCER → PASS → TEAMMATE

PASS does not require a card. During a pass, opponents may use TACKLE.

TACKLE SUCCESS: Pass stopped. Soccer stolen. Old attack ends. New attack starts from Defender 1.

3. COMPLETE SHOOT FLOW

SHOOT → DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER

D1 / D2 DEFENSE

DEFENSE blocks the current SHOOT only. The attacker keeps Soccer.

D1 / D2 TACKLE

TACKLE stops the attack and steals Soccer.

No Defense

If a line does nothing, the attack moves forward automatically.

D1 passes. D2 has no DEFENSE or TACKLE. No DRIBBLE is needed. Attack goes directly to GK.

4. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

DRIBBLE is used when a defender has actually challenged with DEFENSE or TACKLE.

D1 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D1 beaten → D1 may discard one card to chase back.

If the defender does not chase, the attack continues to the next line.

5. YELLOW CARD RULES

YELLOW belongs to the current defensive line.

D1 YELLOW only affects actions against D1. D2 cannot help D1.
D2 YELLOW only affects actions against D2. D1 cannot return.

YELLOW cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.

YELLOW can cancel another YELLOW, creating a chain.

6. GOALKEEPER RULES

GK without DEFENSE: Goal.

GK with DEFENSE: Rock-Paper-Scissors decides SAVE or GOAL.

GK ATTACK

When GK owns Soccer, GK may PASS or SHOOT.

If GK attacks and loses Soccer by TACKLE, GK is OUT OF POSITION. Before defending the next attack, discard one card to return.

7. TURNOVER RESET

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW POSSESSION → D1 → D2 → GK

Previous defensive progress is removed. A new attack never continues from the old position.

8. MATCH END

Match ends after 10 minutes or when both teams cannot create a valid attack.

One team having no SHOOT cards alone does not end the match.

9. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

Real soccer format: 5 rounds.

Each shot: Shooter draws one card. Opposing GK draws one new card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL.

SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE.

After five rounds, higher score wins. Tie continues with sudden death.

10. COMMON RULINGS

Q: Does D2 need DRIBBLE if D2 does nothing?
A: No. The attack goes directly forward.
Q: After TACKLE, where does the next SHOOT start?
A: Always from Defender 1.
Q: Can D1 use YELLOW against D2?
A: No. YELLOW follows the current defensive line.
Q: Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
A: No. Only TACKLE changes possession.
Q: Can Goalkeeper attack?
A: Yes. But losing Soccer while attacking creates a recovery risk.
