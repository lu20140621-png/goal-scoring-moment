MATCH MODE · COMPLETE RULEBOOK V9

A strategic 3v3 soccer battle. Control Soccer, break defensive lines, protect your goal, and manage every card during a 10-minute match.

1. Match Setup

Each team has 3 positions: Defender 1, Defender 2, and Goalkeeper. Players receive equal cards. Before the match starts, teammates may exchange cards one-for-one. Extra cards are discarded so all players begin with the same hand size.

2. Starting Possession

Rock-Paper-Scissors decides the first Soccer holder. The player holding Soccer controls the attack.

3. PASS FLOW

PASS requires no card. Soccer can be passed to any teammate. During a pass, opponents may use TACKLE to steal Soccer.

Successful TACKLE:
Pass stops immediately. The defender takes Soccer. The old attack ends and the next attack restarts from Defender 1.

4. SHOOT ATTACK FLOW

Every SHOOT follows this order:

Attacker → Defender 1 → Defender 2 → Goalkeeper

Defender Options

DEFENSE: Stops this SHOOT only. Soccer stays with the attacking team.

TACKLE: Stops the action, steals Soccer, and creates a new possession.

No defense: The attack continues.

5. DRIBBLE PAST

DRIBBLE PAST is only needed when a defensive line actually uses DEFENSE or TACKLE. If a line does nothing, that line did not defend and no DRIBBLE is required.

D1 defends → attacker DRIBBLES → D1 is beaten → D1 may discard one card to chase back or allow the attack to continue to D2.

6. Defender 2 Rule

Defender 2 follows the same rules as Defender 1.

Important: If Defender 2 does not play DEFENSE or TACKLE, Defender 2 provides no defense. The attacker does not need DRIBBLE and moves directly to the Goalkeeper.

7. YELLOW CARD

YELLOW belongs to the current defensive line.

D1 can only respond while the attack is at D1. D2 can only respond while the attack is at D2.

YELLOW may cancel DRIBBLE or an opponent YELLOW. It cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.

Example: SHOOT → D1 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D1 may play YELLOW. If the attack reaches D2, only D2 has the YELLOW window.

8. Goalkeeper Rules

When the attack reaches the Goalkeeper, the GK may use DEFENSE. A successful GK DEFENSE is decided by Rock-Paper-Scissors.

Goalkeepers may also attack when they control Soccer. They can PASS or SHOOT like other players.

9. Goalkeeper Out Of Position

If a Goalkeeper attacks and loses Soccer because of TACKLE, the goalkeeper is out of position.

When the opponent reaches the GK area, the goalkeeper must discard one card to return to goal. This does not count as DEFENSE. The GK still needs DEFENSE to attempt a save.

10. Turnover Reset

Any successful TACKLE resets the entire attack. Previous DRIBBLE, chase decisions, and defensive progress are removed.

D2 steals Soccer → new attack does NOT start against the opponent D2. It always starts from Defender 1.

11. Match Ending

The match ends when time expires, both teams cannot create an attack, or both sides have no playable attacking options. One team having no SHOOT alone does not end the match.

12. Penalty Shootout

Five rounds, following real soccer penalty structure.

Each attempt: shooter draws one card. Opposing goalkeeper draws one new card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL. SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE. No SHOOT = MISS.

After five rounds, the higher score wins. If tied, continue with sudden death.

13. Quick Examples

SHOOT → DEFENSE: attack stops, Soccer stays with attacker.
SHOOT → TACKLE: defender steals Soccer, new attack begins from D1.
D1 passed → D2 does nothing: D2 is skipped, no DRIBBLE required.
GK attacks → loses Soccer → must return before defending again.
