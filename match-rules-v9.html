Match Mode Rulebook V9

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

Control Soccer. Break Lines. Defend Smart. Choose Your Moment.

Card Battle Guide

1. Match Setup

Each team has three positions: Defender 1, Defender 2, and Goalkeeper. Players reveal positions. Deal equal cards. Teammates may exchange cards one-for-one before the match starts. Extra cards are discarded so all players begin with the same number of cards.

Rock-Paper-Scissors decides who starts with Soccer.

2. Attack Flow

SHOOT → DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER

Every attack follows this order. Defensive lines only act when the attack reaches them.

3. Defender Rules

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT only. Soccer stays with the attacker.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer. The old attack ends immediately.

Successful TACKLE = TURNOVER RESET. The next attack always starts again from Defender 1.

No Defense

If a defensive line does nothing, the attacker passes that line automatically.

4. Dribble Past

DRIBBLE is needed only when a defender actually blocks with DEFENSE or TACKLE.

D1 DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → D1 is beaten.

The defender may discard one card to chase back, or allow the attack to continue.

D2 does not defend = no DRIBBLE required. The attacker goes directly to Goalkeeper.

5. Yellow Card

YELLOW belongs to the current defensive line.

D1 can only use YELLOW while defending D1. D2 can only use YELLOW while defending D2.

YELLOW can cancel DRIBBLE or another YELLOW. It cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.

6. Goalkeeper

GK without DEFENSE = Goal.

GK with DEFENSE starts Rock-Paper-Scissors.

GK wins = SAVE. Shooter wins = GOAL.

GK Attack

When GK owns Soccer, GK may PASS or SHOOT.

If GK attacks and loses Soccer by TACKLE, GK leaves the goal. Before defending the next attack, GK must discard one card to return.

7. Match End

The match ends after 10 minutes, or when neither team can create a valid attack.

One team having no SHOOT cards does not automatically end the match.

8. Penalty Shootout

Real soccer style: 5 rounds.

Each attempt: Shooter draws 1 card. Opposing GK draws 1 new card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL.

SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE.

No SHOOT = MISS.

After 5 rounds, higher score wins. Tie goes to sudden death.

Final Rule Reminder

Every card matters. The strongest team is not the team with the most attacks, but the team that makes the right decision at the right moment.
