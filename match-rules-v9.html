MATCH MODE RULEBOOK V9

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

Control Soccer. Break Defensive Lines. Create Your Attack.

CARD ENCYCLOPEDIA

SOCCER

The ball. The team holding Soccer controls possession.

SHOOT

Starts an attack through D1, D2 and Goalkeeper.

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT. Soccer stays with attacker.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer. New attack resets.

DRIBBLE PAST

Used to beat a defender after a defensive challenge.

YELLOW

Cancels DRIBBLE or YELLOW on the current line only.

ATTACK MAP

SHOOT ⚽ → DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER
D1 / D2 DEFENSE: Stops this SHOOT only. Soccer remains with attacker.
D1 / D2 TACKLE: Steals Soccer. Old attack ends. New attack starts from opponent D1.
No defense: The attack moves forward. No DRIBBLE required.

DRIBBLE & CHASE BACK

DRIBBLE is played after a defender uses DEFENSE or TACKLE.

DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → Defender beaten → Defender may discard 1 card to chase back.
If the defender does not chase, the attack continues to the next line.

YELLOW RULES

Yellow belongs to the defensive line currently being attacked.

D1 Yellow cannot protect D2. D2 Yellow cannot protect D1.
Yellow cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.
Yellow can cancel another Yellow.

GOALKEEPER RULES

GK DEFENSE: Play DEFENSE then Rock-Paper-Scissors decides SAVE or GOAL.
GK ATTACK: Goalkeeper may PASS or SHOOT when holding Soccer.
If GK loses Soccer while attacking, GK is out of position and must discard a card to return before defending.

TURNOVER RESET

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW POSSESSION → D1 → D2 → GK

All previous attack progress is removed.

MATCH END

10 minutes complete, or both teams cannot create a valid attack.

PENALTY SHOOTOUT

5 rounds. Shooter draws 1 card. Opposing goalkeeper draws 1 new card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL
SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE

Tie after 5 rounds goes to sudden death.

RULING EXAMPLES

Q: Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
A: No. Only TACKLE changes possession.
Q: After TACKLE where does the next attack start?
A: Always from Defender 1.
Q: Does D2 need DRIBBLE if D2 does nothing?
A: No. The attack moves directly forward.
Q: Can Yellow cancel SHOOT?
A: No.
