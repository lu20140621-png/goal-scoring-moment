MATCH MODE COMPLETE RULEBOOK

3v3 HIGH DIFFICULTY SOCCER BATTLE

A complete guide for players learning Match Mode for the first time.

1. GAME OVERVIEW

Two teams compete to control Soccer, create attacks, break defensive lines, and score goals. Match Mode focuses on teamwork, positioning and tactical decisions.

Every card has a specific purpose. Understanding when NOT to play a card is just as important as knowing when to use one.

2. TEAM SETUP

DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER
Defender 1: The first defensive line.
Defender 2: The second defensive line.
Goalkeeper: The final defense before a goal.

Players receive equal cards. Teammates may exchange cards before the match. Extra cards are discarded.

Rock-Paper-Scissors decides the first Soccer owner.

3. CARD GUIDE

SOCCER

The player holding Soccer controls possession.

SHOOT

Starts an attack. Requires control of Soccer.

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT only. It does NOT steal Soccer.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer. Possession changes.

DRIBBLE PAST

Used to beat a defender after defensive action.

YELLOW

Used by the current defensive line to cancel DRIBBLE or YELLOW.

4. POSSESSION AND PASS

The Soccer owner controls the attack.

SOCCER OWNER → PASS OR SHOOT

PASS moves Soccer to a teammate. During a pass, opponents may use TACKLE according to the current game rule.

Successful TACKLE during a pass: pass fails, Soccer is stolen, new attack begins.

5. COMPLETE SHOOT FLOW

SHOOT → D1 → D2 → GK → GOAL

D1 / D2 Defense

DEFENSE: The SHOOT is stopped. Soccer remains with the attacking team.
TACKLE: The attack ends. Defender steals Soccer. Reset the attack.
No Defense: The attack moves to the next line. No DRIBBLE is required.

Important Example

D1 is passed. D2 does nothing. The attacker does not need DRIBBLE. The attack continues directly to goalkeeper.

6. DRIBBLE PAST AND CHASE BACK

DEFENDER CHALLENGE → DRIBBLE PAST → NEXT LINE

When a defender actively blocks with DEFENSE or TACKLE, the attacker may use DRIBBLE PAST.

After being beaten, the defender may discard one card to chase back and defend again. If they do not chase, the attack continues.

7. YELLOW RULES

Yellow belongs to the defensive line currently being attacked.
D1 Yellow cannot protect D2. D2 Yellow cannot protect D1.
Yellow cannot cancel SHOOT. Yellow cannot cancel DEFENSE.
Yellow can cancel another Yellow.

8. GOALKEEPER RULES

GK may defend with DEFENSE. After GK DEFENSE, Rock-Paper-Scissors decides SAVE or GOAL.
GK may attack when holding Soccer.
If GK attacks and loses Soccer through TACKLE, GK is out of position. GK must discard a card to return before defending.

9. TACKLE RESET

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW SOCCER OWNER → D1 → D2 → GK

All previous attack progress is removed.

10. MATCH END

The match ends after 10 minutes, or when both teams cannot create a valid attack.

One team having no SHOOT cards alone does not immediately end the match.

11. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

Five rounds. Each shooter draws one card. Opposing goalkeeper draws one card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL
SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE

Higher score wins. If tied, continue sudden death.

12. COMMON RULINGS

Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
No. Only TACKLE changes possession.
Where does a new attack start after TACKLE?
Always from Defender 1.
Can Yellow cancel SHOOT?
No.
Can Goalkeeper attack?
Yes, but losing Soccer creates a defensive risk.
