MATCH MODE · COMPLETE RULEBOOK V9

Official Match Mode rules. This version removes the old loading system and displays directly.

1. Attack Flow

Every SHOOT follows this order: Attacker → Defender 1 → Defender 2 → Goalkeeper.

2. Defense

DEFENSE stops the current SHOOT but does not steal Soccer. Soccer stays with the attacking team.

TACKLE stops the action, steals Soccer, and creates a new possession. The next attack always restarts from Defender 1.

3. Defensive Lines

If a defensive line does not play DEFENSE or TACKLE, that line does not defend. No DRIBBLE is required.

4. Yellow Card

YELLOW belongs to the current defensive line. It can respond to DRIBBLE or YELLOW, but cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE.

5. Goalkeeper

Goalkeepers may attack when holding Soccer. If a goalkeeper attacks and loses Soccer by TACKLE, they are out of position and must discard one card to return before defending again.

6. Penalty Shootout

Five rounds. Each shot is one player versus one goalkeeper. The goalkeeper draws a new card every attempt. SHOOT versus no DEFENSE scores a goal.

7. Turnover Reset

Any successful TACKLE resets the entire attack. Previous DRIBBLE and defensive progress are cleared.
