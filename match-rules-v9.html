MATCH MODE RULEBOOK

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

A complete guide for your first match. Control Soccer. Build attacks. Defend your goal.

WELCOME TO MATCH MODE

Match Mode is a strategic soccer battle. Two teams compete for possession, create attacks, break defensive lines, and score goals.

Each decision matters. A defensive card can save a match. A risky attack can lose possession.

CARD ENCYCLOPEDIA

SOCCER

The ball. The player holding Soccer controls possession and starts attacks.

SHOOT

Starts an attack toward the opponent's defensive lines.

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT only. Soccer stays with the attacker.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer. This creates a new possession.

DRIBBLE PAST

Used to beat a defender after a defensive challenge.

YELLOW

Cancels DRIBBLE or YELLOW on the current defensive line.

GAME SETUP

Each team has three positions:

Defender 1 → Defender 2 → Goalkeeper

Deal equal cards. Teammates may exchange cards one-for-one before the match begins. Extra cards are discarded.

Use Rock-Paper-Scissors to decide the first Soccer possession.

HOW ATTACKS WORK

SHOOT ⚽ → D1 → D2 → GOALKEEPER

STEP 1: SHOOT

The attacking player plays SHOOT while holding Soccer.

STEP 2: DEFENDER 1

D1 may use DEFENSE, TACKLE, or allow the attack to continue.

DEFENSE: The SHOOT stops. Soccer remains with attacker.
TACKLE: The attack stops. Defender steals Soccer. New attack begins from D1.
No action: The attack moves to D2.

STEP 3: DEFENDER 2

D2 follows the same choices.

If D2 does nothing, the attacker does NOT need DRIBBLE. The attack moves directly to goalkeeper.

STEP 4: GOALKEEPER

If goalkeeper has DEFENSE, play DEFENSE and use Rock-Paper-Scissors to decide SAVE or GOAL.

DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

DRIBBLE is used when a defender has actively challenged the attack.

DEFENSE/TACKLE → DRIBBLE → BEAT DEFENDER
After being beaten, the defender may discard one card to chase back. If they do not chase, the attack continues.

YELLOW CARD RULE

Yellow belongs to the defensive line currently being attacked.

D1 can only use Yellow against actions at D1.
D2 can only use Yellow against actions at D2.
Yellow cannot cancel SHOOT. Yellow cannot cancel DEFENSE.
Yellow can cancel another Yellow.

GOALKEEPER RULES

The goalkeeper can defend and can also attack.

GK Defense: DEFENSE + Rock-Paper-Scissors decides the result.
GK Attack: When GK owns Soccer, GK may PASS or SHOOT.
GK Out Of Position: If GK loses Soccer while attacking, GK must discard one card to return before defending.

TACKLE RESET RULE

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW POSSESSION → D1 → D2 → GK

Any successful TACKLE completely resets the attack. Previous progress disappears.

MATCH END

The match ends after 10 minutes, or when neither team can create a valid attack.

One team having no SHOOT cards alone does not end the match.

PENALTY SHOOTOUT

If the match is tied, play a real soccer style penalty shootout.

Five rounds. Each shooter draws one card. The opposing goalkeeper draws one new card.

SHOOT + no DEFENSE = GOAL
SHOOT + DEFENSE = SAVE

Higher score after five rounds wins. If tied, continue with sudden death.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
No. Only TACKLE changes possession.
Where does a new attack start after TACKLE?
Always from Defender 1.
Can Yellow cancel SHOOT?
No.
Can Goalkeeper attack?
Yes, but losing possession creates a defensive risk.
