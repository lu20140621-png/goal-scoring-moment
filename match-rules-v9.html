MATCH MODE RULEBOOK V9

HIGH DIFFICULTY 3v3 SOCCER BATTLE

A tactical soccer card battle. Control possession, break defensive lines, and make every decision count.

CARD ENCYCLOPEDIA

SOCCER

The ball. The player holding Soccer controls possession.

SHOOT

Starts an attack. The attack moves through D1, D2, and GK.

DEFENSE

Stops the current SHOOT only. Soccer stays with the attacker.

TACKLE

Stops the action and steals Soccer. The next attack resets.

DRIBBLE PAST

Used to beat a defender after a defensive challenge.

YELLOW

Only affects the current defensive line. Cannot cancel SHOOT.

1. SETUP

Each team has Defender 1, Defender 2, and Goalkeeper.

Deal equal cards. Teammates may exchange cards one-for-one. Extra cards are discarded.

Rock-Paper-Scissors decides first Soccer possession.

2. POSSESSION AND PASS

SOCCER HOLDER → PASS → TEAMMATE

PASS requires no card. Opponents may use TACKLE during the pass.

TACKLE SUCCESS:
Pass is stopped. Soccer changes owner. Old attack ends. New attack starts from Defender 1.

3. COMPLETE ATTACK FLOW

SHOOT → DEFENDER 1 → DEFENDER 2 → GOALKEEPER

DEFENDER 1

DEFENSE: Stops SHOOT. Soccer stays with attacker.

TACKLE: Stops attack and steals Soccer.

No action: Attack continues.

DEFENDER 2

If D2 does not use DEFENSE or TACKLE, there is no second defense.

D1 is passed. D2 does nothing. No DRIBBLE is required. Attack goes directly to GK.

4. DRIBBLE PAST AND CHASE BACK

DRIBBLE is used when a defender has challenged the attacker.

DEFENSE → DRIBBLE → BEAT DEFENDER
After being beaten, the defender may discard one card to chase back. If not, the attack continues.

5. YELLOW CARD TIMING

YELLOW belongs only to the current defensive line.

D1 can cancel DRIBBLE or YELLOW against D1 only.
D2 can cancel DRIBBLE or YELLOW against D2 only.

YELLOW cannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE. YELLOW can cancel another YELLOW.

6. GOALKEEPER RULES

GK without DEFENSE: Goal.

GK with DEFENSE: Rock-Paper-Scissors decides SAVE or GOAL.

When GK owns Soccer, GK may attack.

GK attacks and loses Soccer by TACKLE: GK becomes out of position. Before the next GK defense, discard one card to return.

7. TURNOVER RESET

SUCCESSFUL TACKLE → NEW POSSESSION → D1 → D2 → GK

Previous attack progress disappears. Never continue from the old defensive line.

8. MATCH END

Match ends after 10 minutes or when neither team can create a valid attack.

One team having no SHOOT alone does not end the match.

9. PENALTY SHOOTOUT

Five rounds. Each shooter draws one card. Opposing goalkeeper draws one new card.

SHOOT without DEFENSE = GOAL.

SHOOT with DEFENSE = SAVE.

After five rounds, higher score wins. Tie continues with sudden death.

10. COMMON RULINGS

Q: Does DEFENSE steal Soccer?
A: No. Only TACKLE steals Soccer.
Q: After TACKLE, where does the next attack start?
A: Defender 1.
Q: Can Yellow cancel SHOOT?
A: No.
Q: Can GK attack?
A: Yes, but losing Soccer creates a recovery risk.
