GOAL, TEAMS & TABLE SETUP

Build two fixed defensive lines and score more goals before time expires.

Match Mode is a tactical 3 vs 3 soccer match using the same physical Action Cards. Each team has three public positions. There are no hidden roles in this mode.

GREEN GKGOALKEEPER
GREEN 2DEFENDER 2
GREEN 1DEFENDER 1
BLUE 1DEFENDER 1
BLUE 2DEFENDER 2
BLUE GKGOALKEEPER
A BLUE attack meets GREEN D1 → GREEN D2 → GREEN GK. A GREEN attack meets BLUE D1 → BLUE D2 → BLUE GK.

WHAT TO PREPARE

TWO TEAMSChoose Blue and Green. Each team assigns Defender 1, Defender 2 and Goalkeeper.
MATCH TIMERSet a 10-minute timer. Have Rock-Paper-Scissors ready for possession and Goalkeeper saves.
WIN CONDITION: Score more goals than the other team when the main match ends. If tied, play the Penalty Shootout in Section 11.

KNOW EVERY CARD

The card's job changes depending on whether you are attacking, defending a field line or protecting the goal.

SOCCERMarks possession. It is not part of the 51-card Action Deck.
SHOOTStarts one scoring attack. Only the ballholder may play it.
DEFENSEField: stops a SHOOT. Goal: starts the GK save duel.
TACKLEChallenges a PASS or current field line. Success steals Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
YELLOWA timed reaction inside the current SHOOT line. It can chain.
CARDSTOPS SHOOT?STEALS SOCCER?DRIBBLE?YELLOW?

THE BIG DIFFERENCE: A successful field DEFENSE ends the current SHOOT but the attacker keeps Soccer. A successful TACKLE ends the action and gives Soccer to the tackler.
3

START THE MATCH

Follow this order exactly so all six hands remain equal.

  1. Shuffle all 51 Action Cards. Keep Soccer separate.
  2. Deal exactly 8 Action Cards to each of the 6 players: 48 cards total.
  3. Put the remaining 3 Action Cards face-down and out of play for the main match.
  4. Start the 10:00 timer immediately after the deal. Team trading and final setup use match time.
  5. Before locking the teams, teammates may make 1-for-1 exchanges. Every player must still have 8 cards.
  6. Play Rock-Paper-Scissors. Repeat ties. The winner chooses which teammate receives Soccer and takes first possession.
FIELD PLAYERSD1 and D2 usually need a mix of DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE and YELLOW.
GOALKEEPERProtect DEFENSE cards, but remember the GK may also PASS or SHOOT while holding Soccer.
NO RECYCLE: Played and discarded cards do not return during the main match. Collect them only if a tied match reaches the Penalty Shootout.
4

POSSESSION & ACTIVE PLAYER

The Soccer Card shows who is allowed to start the team's next move.

BALLHOLDER'S CHOICEThe Soccer holder may PASS to a teammate for free or play SHOOT from their own hand.
NO SHOOT CARDThe ballholder may still PASS until a teammate with SHOOT receives Soccer.
NO FREE TACKLEWait for an opponent PASS or your current field line during a SHOOT.
GOALKEEPER MAY ATTACKA GK can PASS or SHOOT, but losing Soccer to TACKLE creates an out-of-position risk.
NO FIXED TURN ROTATION: Possession decides which team acts. After a goal or save, Soccer goes to the defending Goalkeeper.
5

PASS — FREE, BUT NOT SAFE

PASS uses no Action Card. Opposing field players receive two ordered interception windows.

CALL PASS→OPPONENT D1: TACKLE?→OPPONENT D2: TACKLE?→PASS COMPLETE
  1. Name the teammate receiving the pass.
  2. Opponent D1 may play TACKLE or let the pass continue.
  3. If D1 plays TACKLE, the passer may play one DRIBBLE PAST. Without DRIBBLE, D1 takes Soccer.
  4. If the pass survives D1, opponent D2 gets the same TACKLE window.
  5. If both windows clear, move Soccer to the chosen teammate.
TACKLE SUCCEEDSPass ends. The tackler takes Soccer.
DRIBBLE SUCCEEDSTACKLE fails. Continue to the next window.
NO TACKLEUse no card. Do not play DRIBBLE without a TACKLE to beat.
NO YELLOW DURING A NORMAL PASS. A PASS is not a SHOOT chain.
PASS EXAMPLEBLUE 1 passes to BLUE 2 → GREEN D1 passes → GREEN D2 plays TACKLE → BLUE 1 plays DRIBBLE PAST → the TACKLE fails → Soccer reaches BLUE 2.
6

SHOOT — COMPLETE ATTACK

Resolve one defensive line completely before the next player may react.

PLAY SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER→SAVE / GOAL
  1. The Soccer holder spends one SHOOT.
  2. D1 chooses DEFENSE, TACKLE or no defense.
  3. Resolve every DRIBBLE, YELLOW and chase-back decision on D1's line.
  4. Only if D1 is cleared does D2 make the same choice.
  5. Only if D2 is cleared does the SHOOT reach the Goalkeeper.
DOING NOTHING IS A REAL CHOICE: If D1 or D2 plays neither DEFENSE nor TACKLE, that line gives no defense. The attacker spends no DRIBBLE and moves directly to the next line.
NO-DEFENSE EXAMPLESHOOT → D1 lets it through → D2 lets it through → go directly to GK. DRIBBLE is not needed against a player who played no defensive card.
7

DEFENSE, TACKLE, DRIBBLE & CHASE

These decisions belong only to the current field defender and current attacker.

A. FIELD DEFENSE

  1. The current defender plays DEFENSE.
  2. The attacker may play DRIBBLE PAST. YELLOW cannot cancel DEFENSE.
  3. Without DRIBBLE, the SHOOT ends and Soccer stays with the attacking ballholder.
  4. If DRIBBLE wins, pause for that defender's chase-back choice.

B. FIELD TACKLE

  1. The current defender may play TACKLE instead of DEFENSE.
  2. The attacker may answer with DRIBBLE PAST, or with a legal attacking YELLOW during that SHOOT line.
  3. If TACKLE remains active, the SHOOT ends and the tackler takes Soccer.
  4. If DRIBBLE wins, pause for that defender's chase-back choice.

C. CHASE BACK AFTER DRIBBLE

DEFENDER BEATEN→DISCARD ANY 1→PLAY NEW DEFENSE / TACKLE→RESOLVE AGAIN
  • Every successful DRIBBLE creates a fresh chase choice for the exact defender beaten.
  • Chasing costs one extra discarded card. That payment is not the new defensive card.
  • After paying, play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE. If beaten again, ask and pay again.
  • DRIBBLE PAST never bypasses Goalkeeper DEFENSE.
TWO-CARD MINIMUM TO CHASE: One card pays the chase cost; a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE challenges again.
8

YELLOW — EXACT TIMING & CHAINS

YELLOW is not an anytime card. Its owner and target depend on the current SHOOT line.

CURRENT LINE ONLYAt D1, only D1 owns defending-side YELLOW. At D2, D1's window closes and D2 owns it.
DEFENDING-SIDEThe current defender may YELLOW an attacker's DRIBBLE or YELLOW against that exact line.
ATTACKING-SIDEThe current attacker may YELLOW the current defender's TACKLE. That defender may answer with YELLOW.
NEVER LEGALCannot cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE; cannot be used during PASS or for another line.

HOW THE CHAIN RESOLVES

Each new YELLOW cancels the immediately previous YELLOW. Every added YELLOW flips whether the original cancellable action is active.

TACKLE ACTIVE→ YELLOW →TACKLE CANCELED→ YELLOW →TACKLE RESTORED
D2 LINE EXAMPLESHOOT → D1 cleared → D2 DEFENSE → attacker DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW cancels DRIBBLE → attacker YELLOW cancels D2's YELLOW → D2 may answer again. D1 cannot use YELLOW because play already moved to D2.
STOPPING THE CHAIN: When nobody adds another YELLOW, resolve the original action using its final active/canceled state.
9

GOALKEEPER RULES

The Goalkeeper is the final line. Field TACKLE, field DRIBBLE and defensive-line YELLOW end at the goal.

NORMAL GK STAGE

NO GK DEFENSEAutomatic GOAL +1.
GK PLAYS DEFENSEShooter and GK play RPS. Repeat a tie.
RPS RESULTGK wins = SAVE. Shooter wins = GOAL +1.

After every GK-stage result — save, RPS loss or no-DEFENSE goal — Soccer goes to the defending Goalkeeper for the new possession.

GOALKEEPER JOINS THE ATTACK

A GK may PASS or SHOOT. The special risk starts only when an opponent's successful TACKLE directly steals Soccer from that attacking GK.

GK LOSES TO TACKLE→GK OUT OF POSITION→COUNTER REACHES GOAL→DISCARD 1 TO RETURN→SEPARATE DEFENSE + RPS
  1. The opponent begins a new possession through D1, then D2, then the out-of-position GK.
  2. If the GK's team regains Soccer before the attack reaches goal, the GK returns free.
  3. At the GK stage, discard any 1 card to return.
  4. That is only the recovery cost. Play a separate DEFENSE to attempt an RPS save.
  5. No return, no discard, or no DEFENSE after returning = automatic goal.
  6. After the goal or save, the out-of-position state ends.
CRITICAL: If the GK discards their last DEFENSE to return, they are back in goal but cannot start RPS. The attacker still scores.
10

AFTER A SUCCESSFUL TACKLE

A turnover completely ends the old action. It never resumes where Soccer was stolen.

  1. Stop the current PASS or SHOOT.
  2. The tackler takes Soccer.
  3. Clear the old attack, beaten players, chase status and old YELLOW windows.
  4. The tackler's team now has possession and may PASS or SHOOT.
  5. Its next SHOOT starts at the opponent's Defender 1, then D2, then GK.
TACKLE WINS→OLD ATTACK ENDS→NEW POSSESSION→NEXT SHOOT → OPPONENT D1
GK EXCEPTION: If an attacking GK personally lost Soccer to that TACKLE, also mark that GK OUT OF POSITION.
11

ENDING & PENALTY SHOOTOUT

Finish the active chain, compare scores, then break a tie with alternating kicks.

MAIN MATCH END

  • Clock reaches 00:00. Finish any active PASS, SHOOT, YELLOW chain, chase choice, GK return or GK duel first.
  • Both teams have no Action Cards remaining.
  • Both teams can no longer create a legal attack. A team can still attack if any teammate has SHOOT.
DO NOT END EARLY: One team having no SHOOT does not end the match. Continue while the other team can attack; DEFENSE and TACKLE still matter.

FIVE-ROUND PENALTY SHOOTOUT

  1. Collect and shuffle all 51 Action Cards, including hands, played cards, discards and the 3 extras.
  2. GREEN takes a kick, then BLUE takes the matching kick. Alternate for up to five kicks each.
  3. Every kick uses a fresh kicker draw and a fresh opposing-GK draw.
  4. GOAL: kicker draws SHOOT and GK does not draw DEFENSE. All other combinations score 0.
  5. Both teams use kicker order D1, D2, D1, D2, D1.
  6. End early if the trailing team cannot mathematically catch up.
  7. If tied after five each, play sudden-death pairs until one team scores and the other does not.
SHOOT + NO GK DEFENSEGOAL +1
SHOOT + GK DEFENSESAVE · 0
NO SHOOTMISS · 0
FRESH DUEL: The Goalkeeper draws a new card against every penalty. One GK card never covers several kicks.

COMMON TABLE RULINGS

Open a question for a fast answer during play.

D2 does nothing. Does the attacker need DRIBBLE?

No. D2 gave no defense. Go directly to the Goalkeeper.

Does field DEFENSE steal Soccer?

No. It ends only this SHOOT. The attacker keeps Soccer, and a later SHOOT restarts at D1.

Can YELLOW cancel SHOOT or DEFENSE?

No. It may cancel current-line TACKLE, DRIBBLE or another YELLOW at the correct timing.

Can D1 use YELLOW after play reaches D2?

No. D1's window is closed. D2 now owns defending-side YELLOW for D2's line.

Can TACKLE be played whenever an opponent has Soccer?

No. It needs a PASS interception window or the tackler's current field line after SHOOT.

Where does a new attack start after TACKLE?

The old action ends. The new team's next SHOOT starts fresh at the opponent's D1.

Can the Goalkeeper attack?

Yes. The GK may PASS or SHOOT, but losing Soccer directly to TACKLE causes out-of-position risk.

When does an out-of-position GK return free?

If their team regains Soccer before the immediate counterattack reaches goal.

Where does Soccer go after a save or goal?

To the defending Goalkeeper in every GK-stage outcome.

Do played cards recycle in the main match?

No. Collect all 51 only for a tied-match Penalty Shootout.

What if time expires during an action?

Finish that current action or reaction chain, then compare scores.
