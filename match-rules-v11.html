GAME GOAL — SCORE MORE GOALS THAN YOUR OPPONENT

YOUR OBJECTIVE: Score as many goals as possible before the match ends. The team with the higher score wins. If the scores are tied, use the Penalty Shootout to decide the winner.

TEAMS
Two teams. Both teams must use the same role formation: 2v2, 3v3, or 4v4. Each team always has 1 Goalkeeper + 1–3 field Players. Roles are public.
ACTION DECK
51 cards total: SHOOT ×10, DEFENSE ×12, TACKLE ×10, DRIBBLE PAST ×10, YELLOW ×9.
DEAL
Use 48 of the 51 Action Cards and put the remaining 3 face-down and out of play. Deal equally to every active role: 2v2 = 12 each · 3v3 = 8 each · 4v4 = 6 each.
10-MINUTE TIMER
Take out a phone and set a timer for 10:00. Start it immediately after all 48 Action Cards have been dealt. Any teammate hand trading / card exchanges and final setup happen while the timer is already running and count as part of the same 10 minutes. Do not finish trading first and start the timer afterward.
TEAM TRADE
Teammates may exchange Action Cards 1-for-1 before normal play begins. Everyone must still have the same number of cards. This trading time is included in the 10:00 phone timer.
FIRST POSSESSION
Rock-Paper-Scissors decides opening possession. Repeat ties. Winner receives Soccer.

GK HAND MANAGEMENT: The Goalkeeper may attack in Match Mode, so SHOOT / DRIBBLE / attacking YELLOW can have value in the GK hand. DEFENSE remains critical because the Goalkeeper may need it after returning to goal.

2. POSSESSION & WHO MAY ACT

WHOEVER HOLDS SOCCER CONTROLS THE BALL
PASSMove Soccer to a teammate for free. No Action Card is spent.
SHOOTIf the ballholder has SHOOT, they may start an attack.
GOALKEEPER MAY ATTACKA GK holding Soccer may PASS or SHOOT like another ballholder.
TACKLE DURING SHOOTTACKLE is a defensive choice on the current SHOOT field line. A normal PASS is not intercepted.

4. PASS — TACKLE INTERCEPTION

PASS → TACKLE → YELLOW OR TURNOVER
1DECLARE PASS

The Soccer holder chooses a teammate. PASS costs no Action Card.

→
2OPPONENT MAY TACKLE

During the pass, an opponent may play TACKLE to try to steal Soccer.

→
3PASSER MAY PLAY YELLOW

If the passer has YELLOW, they may play it to cancel that TACKLE.

→
4RESOLVE THE PASS

YELLOW cancels TACKLE → PASS completes. No YELLOW → TACKLE succeeds and the tackler wins Soccer.

  1. Only the current Soccer holder may declare PASS.
  2. PASS itself is free; no PASS Action Card is spent.
  3. While the pass is happening, an opponent may play TACKLE to try to steal the Soccer Card.
  4. Only YELLOW can cancel this PASS TACKLE. DRIBBLE PAST does not cancel a TACKLE played during PASS.
  5. If the passer plays YELLOW, that TACKLE is canceled and the pass completes to the chosen teammate.
  6. If the passer has no YELLOW or chooses not to use it, TACKLE succeeds. The pass fails and the tackler immediately takes Soccer.
  7. After a successful pass, the receiving teammate becomes the new ballholder. If they play SHOOT, that new attack starts at the opponent's D1.

KEY: PASS is free, but it is not automatically safe. TACKLE can steal the ball during the pass; YELLOW is the pass-side counter to that TACKLE.

5. COMPLETE SHOOT / ATTACK FLOW

FIELD PLAYERS GET 3 CHOICES · GOALKEEPER GETS 1 DEFENSE CHOICE
1SHOOT

The Soccer holder plays SHOOT. This starts one scoring attack.

→
2D1 CHOOSES

DEFENSE, TACKLE, or NO DEFENSE. If D1 plays nothing, the attack immediately moves to the next active field defender.

→
3D2 / D3 — IF USED

Each field defender gets the same 3 choices: DEFENSE, TACKLE, or NO DEFENSE. Playing nothing lets the attack continue.

→
4GOALKEEPER

The GK has one defensive card choice: play DEFENSE. If the GK does not play DEFENSE, it is an automatic GOAL.

D1 — DEFENSE
D1 may play DEFENSE. Resolve that DEFENSE completely. The attacker may use the legal SHOOT-line responses. If D1's DEFENSE ultimately succeeds, this SHOOT ends.
D1 — TACKLE
D1 may play TACKLE instead. If that TACKLE ultimately succeeds, the SHOOT ends immediately, D1 takes Soccer, and possession changes.
D1 — NO DEFENSE
D1 may choose to play nothing. In that case D1 is cleared automatically. The attacker does not need DRIBBLE PAST and the attack moves directly to the next active field defender.
D2 / D3
Each later field defender follows the same rule as D1: play DEFENSE, play TACKLE, or choose NO DEFENSE. If that defender plays nothing, the attack immediately continues to the next line.
GOALKEEPER
The Goalkeeper does not choose DEFENSE / TACKLE / NO DEFENSE like a field player. At the goal, the only defensive card the GK may use is DEFENSE. Play DEFENSE → start the Goalkeeper Duel. Do not play DEFENSE / have no DEFENSE → automatic GOAL +1.
  1. The Soccer holder plays SHOOT and spends that SHOOT card.
  2. Start with the opponent's first active field defender: D1.
  3. At each field line, that defender chooses exactly one path: DEFENSE, TACKLE, or NO DEFENSE.
  4. If the defender chooses NO DEFENSE, that line is cleared immediately. Do not spend DRIBBLE PAST.
  5. If DEFENSE or TACKLE is played, fully resolve that card and all legal responses before moving to the next line.
  6. Continue through only the field positions used by the chosen formation.
  7. After the final active field defender is cleared, the SHOOT reaches the Goalkeeper.
  8. At the Goalkeeper: DEFENSE = Goalkeeper Duel. No DEFENSE = automatic GOAL.

FORMATION ORDER: 2v2 = D1 → GK. 3v3 = D1 → D2 → GK. 4v4 = D1 → D2 → D3 → GK.

DO NOT CONFUSE FIELD PLAYERS WITH THE GK: D1 / D2 / D3 may DEFENSE, TACKLE, or decline to defend. The Goalkeeper cannot use TACKLE at the goal — the GK either plays DEFENSE or concedes the goal.

6. DEFENSE — FIELD RESULT

DEFENSE vs TACKLE — DO NOT CONFUSE THESE RESULTS

DEFENSE

  • Stops the current SHOOT.
  • Cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  • Attacker may DRIBBLE to beat it.
  • If DEFENSE succeeds, the Soccer Card stays with the attacker.
SHOT STOPPED · BALL NOT WON

TACKLE

  • May be played to intercept a PASS, or by the current field defender during a SHOOT.
  • Can be beaten by DRIBBLE.
  • During SHOOT, attacking YELLOW may cancel it.
  • If TACKLE succeeds, the tackler takes the Soccer Card.
POSSESSION CHANGES

KEY: DEFENSE stops the shot but does not win the ball. Only a successful TACKLE changes possession.

7. TACKLE — WINNING SOCCER

1TACKLE PLAYED

Legal to intercept a PASS, or for the current field defender during a SHOOT.

→
2ATTACKER RESPONDS

During a SHOOT, DRIBBLE may beat the current defender’s TACKLE, and attacking YELLOW may also cancel it. During a PASS, DRIBBLE does not cancel TACKLE; the passer needs YELLOW.

→
3TACKLE SUCCEEDS

The previous action ends. The tackler takes the Soccer Card.

If an attacking Goalkeeper loses the Soccer Card directly to an opponent's TACKLE, that Goalkeeper becomes OUT OF POSITION. See Section 11.

8. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

EVERY SUCCESSFUL DRIBBLE CREATES A NEW DECISION
1DEFENDER ACTS

The current defender plays DEFENSE or TACKLE.

2DRIBBLE PAST

The attacker bypasses exactly that 1 defensive attempt.

3DISCARD ANY 1 CARD?

That same defender may discard ANY 1 card from their hand to chase back, or let the next defensive line respond. This is not a YELLOW effect.

4DEFEND AGAIN

If the defender chases back, the discarded card only pays the chase cost. The defender must still play a new DEFENSE or TACKLE.

  1. One DRIBBLE PAST bypasses exactly 1 field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
  2. If a defender does not play DEFENSE or TACKLE, no DRIBBLE PAST is needed.
  3. Whenever a field defender is beaten by DRIBBLE PAST during a SHOOT, pause immediately.
  4. Ask that same defender: CHASE BACK or LET THE NEXT LINE DEFEND?
  5. CHASE BACK costs 1 additional discarded card. After paying that cost, the defender must still play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE.
  6. If the same defender is beaten again, ask again and pay again.
  7. DRIBBLE PAST does not bypass Goalkeeper DEFENSE.

EVERY DRIBBLE MATTERS: A defender who is beaten twice must make the chase decision twice. Each CHASE BACK costs another card.

9. YELLOW — SHOOT LINES + PASS TACKLE EXCEPTION

✓ YELLOW CAN

  • Cancel DRIBBLE on the current SHOOT line.
  • Cancel the current defender's TACKLE when played by the attacker.
  • Cancel another YELLOW in the same live chain.
  • Follow D1 while D1 is current, then D2 when play moves to D2.

✕ YELLOW CAN'T

  • Cancel SHOOT.
  • Cancel DEFENSE.
  • Cancel the PASS itself.
  • Jump from D1 to D2 or from D2 back to D1.
  • Be used by the Goalkeeper as defending-side YELLOW at the goal.
PASS EXCEPTION
If an opponent plays TACKLE during your PASS, the passer may play YELLOW to cancel that TACKLE. DRIBBLE PAST does not cancel a PASS TACKLE.
D1 LINE
While D1 is current, only D1 has the defending-side YELLOW window for that line.
D2 LINE
Once play moves to D2, D1's window is over. D2 has the defending-side YELLOW window.

D2 EXAMPLE: SHOOT → D1 cleared → D2 DEFENSE → attacker DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW. D2 cancels the DRIBBLE, so D2 DEFENSE succeeds and the SHOOT ends. D1 cannot use YELLOW because the attack already moved to D2.

NO GK DEFENSE

GK DEFENSE
→ RPS DUEL
AFTER SAVE OR GOAL:
SOCCER → DEFENDING GK
  1. The GK stage begins only after D1 and D2 have been cleared or chosen not to defend.
  2. If GK has no DEFENSE, the attack scores automatically.
  3. If GK plays DEFENSE, shooter and GK play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
  4. After any GK-stage result — save, RPS loss, or no-DEFENSE goal — the Soccer Card goes to the defending Goalkeeper.
  5. The Goalkeeper becomes the new ballholder and may PASS or play SHOOT if they have a SHOOT card.

GK POSSESSION RULE: At the Goalkeeper stage, Soccer goes to the defending GK whether the GK saves or concedes.

11. GOALKEEPER JOINS ATTACK — OUT OF POSITION RULE

SPECIAL COUNTERATTACK STORYBOARD
1GK HAS SOCCER

The Goalkeeper may move forward, PASS, or SHOOT.

2OPPONENT TACKLE WINS

The Soccer Card is taken directly from the attacking GK.

3GK OUT OF POSITION

If the counterattack reaches the goal, the GK may discard ANY 1 card from their hand to return. This is not a YELLOW effect.

4THEN DEFEND

Returning only restores the GK's position. A separate DEFENSE card is still required for the Goalkeeper Duel.

  1. The trigger is a successful opponent TACKLE that directly steals Soccer from the attacking Goalkeeper.
  2. The opponent takes the Soccer Card and begins a new possession.
  3. The counterattack still goes through D1 → D2 in the normal order.
  4. If your field defenders win Soccer back before the counterattack reaches GK, your GK returns to position without paying a card.
  5. If the attack reaches the Goalkeeper while that GK is still OUT OF POSITION, pause before the Goalkeeper DEFENSE step.
  6. The GK may discard any 1 card to RETURN TO GOAL.
  7. That discarded card pays only the recovery cost. It is not the DEFENSE card.
  8. After returning, if the GK still has DEFENSE, play it and start RPS. If no DEFENSE remains, the attack scores automatically.
  9. If the GK chooses not to discard 1 card, or has no card to discard, the goal is empty: automatic GOAL +1.
  10. After that goal, Soccer goes to the defending GK and the OUT OF POSITION state ends.

IMPORTANT RISK: If your only remaining card is DEFENSE and you discard it to run back, you return to goal — but now have no DEFENSE left, so the attacker still scores automatically. Recovery and saving are two separate costs/actions.

EXAMPLE: BLUE GK uses SHOOT while holding Soccer → GREEN D1 TACKLE succeeds during that SHOOT → GREEN D1 takes Soccer → BLUE GK is OUT OF POSITION → GREEN immediately begins the new possession.

12. AFTER A TURNOVER

1TACKLE SUCCEEDS

The previous PASS or SHOOT ends immediately.

→
2TACKLER TAKES SOCCER

The player who won the TACKLE becomes the new ballholder immediately.

→
3NEW POSSESSION STARTS NOW

The new ballholder may immediately PASS for free, or play SHOOT if they have a SHOOT card.

  1. The previous attack, DRIBBLE status, chase status, and YELLOW windows all end.
  2. The tackler immediately becomes the new ballholder and their team becomes the attacking team.
  3. The new ballholder may immediately PASS or start a new SHOOT.
  4. Any new SHOOT starts fresh at the opponent's first active field defender: D1.

GK EXCEPTION: If the player who lost Soccer to that TACKLE was the attacking Goalkeeper, also apply the OUT OF POSITION rule in Section 11.

BOTH CAN'T ATTACK

The team with the higher score wins. If the score is tied, use the Penalty Shootout below.

SUDDEN DEATH
If tied, both teams take one matching kick per round until one scores and the other does not.
  1. Teams alternate kicks. GREEN takes the first kick.
  2. Each kick uses two fresh draws from the shuffled Action Deck.
  3. A kick is a GOAL only if the kicker draws SHOOT and the opposing GK does not draw DEFENSE.
  4. Each team normally takes 5 kicks. The sequence is D1, D2, D1, D2, D1.
  5. If one team becomes mathematically impossible to catch, the shootout ends early.
  6. If tied after 5 kicks each, begin sudden death. Finish both matching kicks in the round before deciding the winner.

15. COMMON TABLE RULINGS

D2 DOES NOTHINGNo DRIBBLE PAST is required. Go straight to the Goalkeeper.
FIELD DEFENSE WORKSThe current SHOOT ends. The same attacking ballholder keeps the Soccer Card.
FIELD TACKLE WORKSThe previous attack ends immediately. The tackler takes the Soccer Card, becomes the new ballholder, and may immediately PASS or start a new SHOOT.
WHO MAY DEFENDING-YELLOW?Only the defender on the line currently being attacked, and only in response to opponent DRIBBLE/YELLOW on that line.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL SHOOT?No.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL DEFENSE?No.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL TACKLE?During a SHOOT line, the attacking ballholder may YELLOW the current defender's TACKLE.
CAN A PASS BE TACKLED?Yes. During a PASS, an opponent may play TACKLE to try to steal Soccer.
HOW DO YOU STOP A PASS TACKLE?The passer may play YELLOW to cancel that TACKLE. DRIBBLE PAST does not cancel a PASS TACKLE. Without YELLOW, the TACKLE succeeds and the tackler takes Soccer.
CAN GK ATTACK?Yes. The GK may PASS or SHOOT while holding the Soccer Card.
WHEN IS GK OUT?When an opponent's TACKLE directly steals the Soccer Card from the attacking GK.
RETURN COSTDiscard any 1 card when the counterattack reaches GK. Then, separately, use DEFENSE if available.
NO RETURN?Empty goal. Automatic GOAL +1.
TEAM WINS SOCCER BACK FIRST?The immediate counterattack ends and the GK returns to position without paying a card.
AFTER GK SAVE OR GOAL?The Soccer Card goes to the defending Goalkeeper for the restart either way.
ONE TEAM HAS NO SHOOT?Do not end the match if the opponent can still attack. Defensive cards still matter.
PLAYED CARDS RETURN?There is no normal recycling during the 10-minute main match. Played cards return only for a tied-match Penalty Shootout.
