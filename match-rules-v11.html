TEAMS
Two teams of 3: Defender 1, Defender 2, Goalkeeper. Roles are public.
ACTION DECK
51 cards total: SHOOT ×10, DEFENSE ×12, TACKLE ×10, DRIBBLE PAST ×10, YELLOW ×9.
DEAL
Deal exactly 8 Action Cards to each of the 6 players = 48 cards. Put the remaining 3 face-down and out of play during the main match.
CLOCK
Start the 10:00 clock immediately after the deal. Team trading and final setup are part of those 10 minutes.
TEAM TRADE
Teammates may exchange Action Cards 1-for-1 before play begins. Everyone must still have the same number of cards.
FIRST POSSESSION
Rock-Paper-Scissors decides opening possession. Repeat ties. Winner receives Soccer.

GK HAND MANAGEMENT: The Goalkeeper may attack in Match Mode, so SHOOT / DRIBBLE / attacking YELLOW can have value in the GK hand. DEFENSE remains critical because the Goalkeeper may need it after returning to goal.

If both windows clear, the teammate receives Soccer.

  1. PASS costs no Action Card.
  2. D1 gets the first TACKLE interception window.
  3. If D1 does not TACKLE, or D1's TACKLE is beaten by DRIBBLE, D2 gets the second TACKLE window.

1SHOOT

→
3DEFENDER 2

After D1 is cleared, D2 gets the same choices.

→
4GOALKEEPER

GK DEFENSE starts RPS. No DEFENSE = automatic goal.

  1. Ballholder plays SHOOT. The SHOOT card is spent.
  2. D1 may play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or choose not to defend.
  3. Resolve D1 completely before D2 acts.
  4. After D1 is cleared, D2 may play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or choose not to defend.
  5. If a defender plays nothing, that defender provided no defense. The attacker does not need DRIBBLE for that line.
  6. If D2 plays nothing, the attack goes directly to Goalkeeper.

SECOND-LINE RULE: Once the attack reaches D2, if D2 does not play DEFENSE or TACKLE, no DRIBBLE is required. Go straight to GK.

6. DEFENSE — FIELD RESULT

DEFENSE vs TACKLE — DO NOT CONFUSE THESE RESULTS

DEFENSE

  • Stops the current SHOOT.
  • Cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  • Attacker may DRIBBLE to beat it.
  • If DEFENSE succeeds, Soccer stays with the attacker.
SHOT STOPPED · BALL NOT WON

TACKLE

  • May be used on current field line or PASS window.
  • Can be beaten by DRIBBLE.
  • During SHOOT, attacking YELLOW may cancel it.
  • If TACKLE succeeds, the tackler takes Soccer.
POSSESSION CHANGES

KEY: DEFENSE stops the shot but does not win the ball. Only a successful TACKLE changes possession.

8. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

EVERY SUCCESSFUL DRIBBLE CREATES A NEW DECISION
1DEFENDER ACTS

Current defender plays DEFENSE or TACKLE.

2DRIBBLE PAST

Attacker beats exactly that one defensive attempt.

3CHASE BACK?

That exact defender chooses: discard ANY 1 card to chase, or let the next line defend.

4DEFEND AGAIN

If chasing, the cost card is separate. Defender must still play a new DEFENSE or TACKLE.

  1. One DRIBBLE beats exactly one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
  2. If a defender played no DEFENSE/TACKLE, no DRIBBLE is needed.
  3. Every time a field defender is beaten by DRIBBLE during SHOOT, pause immediately.
  4. Ask that exact defender: CHASE BACK or LET NEXT LINE DEFEND?
  5. CHASE BACK costs 1 extra discarded card. The defender must still play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE after paying the chase cost.
  6. If the same defender is beaten again, ask again and pay again.
  7. DRIBBLE does not bypass Goalkeeper DEFENSE.

EVERY DRIBBLE MATTERS: A defender who is beaten twice must make the chase decision twice. Each chase-back costs another card.

9. YELLOW — CURRENT LINE ONLY

✓ YELLOW CAN

  • Cancel DRIBBLE on the current SHOOT line.
  • Cancel the current defender's TACKLE when played by the attacker.
  • Cancel another YELLOW in the same live chain.
  • Follow D1 while D1 is current, then D2 when play moves to D2.

✕ YELLOW CAN'T

  • Cancel SHOOT.
  • Cancel DEFENSE.
  • Be used during a normal PASS.
  • Jump from D1 to D2 or from D2 back to D1.
  • Be used by the Goalkeeper as defending-side YELLOW at the goal.
D1 LINE
While D1 is current, only D1 has the defending-side YELLOW window for that line.
D2 LINE
Once play moves to D2, D1's window is over. D2 owns the defending-side YELLOW window.

D2 EXAMPLE: SHOOT → D1 cleared → D2 DEFENSE → attacker DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW. D2 cancels the DRIBBLE, so D2 DEFENSE succeeds and the SHOOT ends. D1 cannot use YELLOW because the attack already moved to D2.

AFTER SAVE OR GOAL:
SOCCER → DEFENDING GK
  1. GK stage starts only after D1 and D2 are cleared or choose not to defend.
  2. If GK has no DEFENSE, the attack scores automatically.
  3. If GK plays DEFENSE, shooter and GK play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
  4. After any GK-stage result — save, RPS loss, or no-DEFENSE goal — Soccer ends in the defending Goalkeeper's hand.
  5. The Goalkeeper becomes the new ballholder and may PASS or personally SHOOT if holding SHOOT.

GK POSSESSION RULE: At the Goalkeeper stage, Soccer goes to the defending GK whether the GK saves or concedes.

11. GOALKEEPER JOINS ATTACK — OUT OF POSITION RULE

SPECIAL COUNTERATTACK STORYBOARD
1GK HAS SOCCER

Goalkeeper may push forward, PASS or SHOOT.

2OPPONENT TACKLE WINS

Soccer is directly stolen from the attacking GK.

3GK OUT OF POSITION

If counterattack reaches goal, GK may discard ANY 1 card to return.

4THEN DEFEND

Returning is only recovery. A separate DEFENSE is still needed for RPS.

  1. The trigger is a successful opponent TACKLE that directly steals Soccer from the attacking Goalkeeper.
  2. The opponent receives Soccer and begins a normal new possession.
  3. The counterattack still goes through D1 → D2 in the normal order.
  4. If your field defenders win Soccer back before the counterattack reaches GK, your GK returns to position without paying a card.
  5. If the attack reaches GK while that GK is still OUT OF POSITION, pause before Goalkeeper DEFENSE.
  6. The GK may discard any 1 card to RETURN TO GOAL.
  7. That discarded card pays only the recovery cost. It is not the DEFENSE card.
  8. After returning, if the GK still has DEFENSE, play it and start RPS. If no DEFENSE remains, the attack scores automatically.
  9. If the GK refuses to discard 1 card, or has no card to discard, the goal is empty: automatic GOAL +1.
  10. After that goal, Soccer goes to the defending GK and the OUT OF POSITION state ends.

IMPORTANT RISK: If your only remaining card is DEFENSE and you discard it to run back, you return to goal — but now have no DEFENSE left, so the attacker still scores automatically. Recovery and saving are two separate costs/actions.

EXAMPLE: BLUE GK SHOOT → GREEN D1 TACKLE succeeds → GREEN D1 takes Soccer → GREEN attacks through BLUE D1 and BLUE D2 → reaches BLUE GK. BLUE GK discards YELLOW to RETURN TO GOAL → BLUE GK still has DEFENSE → plays DEFENSE → RPS.

SUDDEN DEATH
If tied, both teams take one matching kick per round until one scores and the other does not.
  1. Teams alternate kicks. GREEN takes the first kick.
  2. Each kick uses two fresh draws from the shuffled Action Deck.
  3. A kick is a GOAL only if the kicker draws SHOOT and the opposing GK does not draw DEFENSE.
  4. Each team normally takes 5 kicks. The sequence is D1, D2, D1, D2, D1.
  5. If one team becomes mathematically impossible to catch, the shootout ends early.
  6. If tied after 5 kicks each, begin sudden death. Finish both matching kicks in the round before deciding the winner.
