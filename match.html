MATCH MODE

Play with 2–8 people. Choose equal team formations from 2v2 to 4v4 roles, keep one separate hand for every role, fight through each active defensive line, and score as many goals as you can before the 10-minute clock runs out.

⚠ HIGH DIFFICULTY · FIRST-TIME PLAYERS SHOULD CHOOSE CAREFULLYNew to the game? Start with COACH TUTORIAL before entering the full VS AI match.
👥 2–8 HUMAN PLAYERS
Each team uses 1 GK + 1–3 field Players. One human may control multiple roles.
🃏 EQUAL ROLE HANDS
Use 48 Action Cards and discard 3: 2v2 = 12 each · 3v3 = 8 each · 4v4 = 6 each.
🛡️ DEFENSE ≠ TACKLE
DEFENSE stops the SHOOT but keeps Soccer with the attacker. TACKLE wins possession.
🤖 VS AI = STANDARD 3v3
The digital AI playtest uses D1 + D2 + Goalkeeper on each team.
