1. SETUP, DEAL & CLOCK

TEAMS
Two teams of 3: Defender 1, Defender 2, Goalkeeper. Roles are public.
ACTION DECK
51 cards total: SHOOT ×10, DEFENSE ×12, TACKLE ×10, DRIBBLE PAST ×10, YELLOW ×9.
DEAL
Deal exactly 8 cards to each of the 6 players = 48 cards. Put the remaining 3 face-down and out of play during the main match.
CLOCK
Start the 10:00 clock immediately after the deal. Team trading and final setup are already part of those 10 minutes.
TEAM TRADE
Before the team is locked, teammates may exchange cards 1-for-1. Everyone still begins with 8 cards.
FIRST POSSESSION
Rock-Paper-Scissors. Repeat ties. Winner receives Soccer.

GK HAND MANAGEMENT: The Goalkeeper may attack in this mode, so SHOOT / DRIBBLE / attacking YELLOW can have value in the GK hand. Defensive YELLOW and normal field TACKLE are not used at the goal itself, so the pre-match 1-for-1 trade is important.

2. POSSESSION & WHO MAY ACT

The Soccer Card represents the ball. Whoever physically holds Soccer is the active ballholder.

BALLHOLDER
May PASS for free or play SHOOT if holding SHOOT.
GOALKEEPER WITH SOCCER
The Goalkeeper is allowed to join the attack. A GK with Soccer may PASS or play SHOOT just like another ballholder.
NO FREE TACKLE
A defender cannot play TACKLE merely because the opponent has Soccer. A legal PASS window or SHOOT defensive-line window must exist.

3. CARD FUNCTIONS

SHOOTStarts one scoring attack. YELLOW cannot cancel SHOOT.
DEFENSEField: stop this SHOOT only. GK: start the RPS save duel.
TACKLEIntercept PASS or challenge the ballholder on the current field defensive line. Success wins Soccer.
DRIBBLE PASTBeats one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
YELLOWReaction card tied to the current SHOOT line. Never cancels SHOOT or DEFENSE.

4. COMPLETE PASS FLOW

SOCCER HOLDER→PASS→D1 TACKLE?→D2 TACKLE?→PASS COMPLETE
  1. PASS costs no Action Card.
  2. D1 gets the first TACKLE interception window.
  3. If D1 does not TACKLE, or D1's TACKLE is beaten by DRIBBLE, D2 gets the second TACKLE window.
  4. If both windows clear, Soccer reaches the intended teammate.
  5. Goalkeeper does not TACKLE a normal PASS while acting as Goalkeeper.
  6. YELLOW is not active during a normal PASS.

GK PASS EXAMPLE: BLUE GK PASS → GREEN D1 TACKLE succeeds. Because the TACKLE directly stole Soccer from the attacking BLUE GK, BLUE GK becomes OUT OF POSITION. See Section 11.

5. COMPLETE SHOOT / ATTACK FLOW

SHOOT→DEFENDER 1→DEFENDER 2→GOALKEEPER
  1. Ballholder plays SHOOT. The card is spent.
  2. D1 may play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or not defend.
  3. Resolve D1 fully before D2 acts.
  4. After D1 is cleared, D2 may play DEFENSE, TACKLE, or not defend.
  5. If a defender plays nothing, that defender did not defend. The attacker does not need DRIBBLE for that line.
  6. If D2 plays nothing, the attack goes directly to Goalkeeper.

SECOND-LINE RULE: Once the attack reaches D2, if D2 does not play DEFENSE or TACKLE, D2 provided no defense. No DRIBBLE is required. Go straight to GK.

6. DEFENSE — FIELD RESULT

  1. Current field defender plays DEFENSE.
  2. DEFENSE cannot be canceled by YELLOW.
  3. Attacker may play DRIBBLE PAST to beat that DEFENSE.
  4. If DEFENSE succeeds, the current SHOOT ends immediately.
  5. Soccer remains with the same attacking ballholder.
  6. All temporary beaten/chase status from that SHOOT resets.
  7. The attacker may PASS or later SHOOT again. A new SHOOT always restarts at D1.

KEY: DEFENSE stops the shot but does not win the ball.

7. TACKLE — WINNING SOCCER

TACKLE is the possession-winning defensive card. It is legal during a PASS interception window or on the current field defensive line after SHOOT.

TACKLE SUCCEEDSThe current PASS/SHOOT ends immediately. Tackler takes Soccer.
DRIBBLE BEATS TACKLETACKLE fails. During SHOOT, the defender is beaten and enters the chase-back decision.
ATTACKING YELLOWDuring a SHOOT line, the attacker may use YELLOW on the current defender's TACKLE.
GK LOSES TO TACKLEIf the attacking Goalkeeper personally loses Soccer to a successful opponent TACKLE, that GK becomes OUT OF POSITION.

8. DRIBBLE PAST & CHASE BACK

  1. One DRIBBLE beats exactly one field DEFENSE or TACKLE attempt.
  2. If a defender played no DEFENSE/TACKLE, no DRIBBLE is needed.
  3. Every time a field defender is beaten by DRIBBLE during SHOOT, pause immediately.
  4. Ask that exact defender: CHASE BACK or LET NEXT LINE DEFEND?
  5. CHASE BACK costs 1 extra discarded card, then the defender must still play a separate DEFENSE or TACKLE.
  6. If the same defender is beaten again, ask again and pay again.
  7. DRIBBLE does not bypass Goalkeeper DEFENSE.

9. YELLOW — CURRENT LINE ONLY

YELLOW follows the defensive line currently being attacked.

D1 LINE
While D1 is current, only D1 has the defending-side YELLOW window for that line.
D2 LINE
Once play moves to D2, D1's window is over. D2 now owns the defending-side YELLOW window.
DEFENDING-SIDE YELLOW
The current defender may use YELLOW only after the attacker plays DRIBBLE or YELLOW against that exact line.
ATTACKING-SIDE YELLOW
The current attacker may use YELLOW on the current defender's TACKLE. The same current defender may answer that YELLOW with YELLOW.
NOT LEGAL
YELLOW cannot cancel SHOOT, cannot cancel DEFENSE, cannot be used on a normal PASS, and cannot be used by another defensive line.
GOALKEEPER
A GK cannot use defensive YELLOW at the goal. If the GK is currently attacking with Soccer, the GK may use attacking-side YELLOW under the same TACKLE/YELLOW timing as any other attacker.

D2 EXAMPLE: SHOOT → D1 cleared → D2 DEFENSE → attacker DRIBBLE → D2 YELLOW. D2 cancels the DRIBBLE, so D2 DEFENSE succeeds and the SHOOT ends. D1 cannot use YELLOW here because the attack already moved to D2.

10. NORMAL GOALKEEPER FLOW

  1. GK stage starts only after D1 and D2 are cleared or choose not to defend.
  2. If GK has no DEFENSE, the attack scores automatically.
  3. If GK plays DEFENSE, shooter and GK play Rock-Paper-Scissors.
  4. GK wins = SAVE. Shooter wins = GOAL +1. Tie = repeat RPS.
  5. After any GK-stage result — save, RPS loss, or no-DEFENSE goal — Soccer ends in the defending Goalkeeper's hand.
  6. The Goalkeeper is now the new ballholder and may PASS or may personally SHOOT if holding SHOOT.

11. GOALKEEPER JOINS ATTACK — OUT OF POSITION RULE

The Goalkeeper may push forward and attack. This creates a special counterattack risk.

GK HAS SOCCER→GK ATTACKS / PASSES→OPPONENT TACKLE WINS SOCCER→GK OUT OF POSITION
  1. The trigger is a successful opponent TACKLE that directly steals Soccer from the attacking Goalkeeper.
  2. The opponent receives Soccer and begins a normal new possession.
  3. The counterattack still goes through D1 → D2 in the normal order.
  4. If your field defenders stop the counterattack by winning Soccer back before it reaches GK, the immediate counterattack is over and your GK returns to position without paying a card.
  5. If the attack reaches your GK stage while the GK is still OUT OF POSITION, pause before any Goalkeeper DEFENSE.
  6. The GK may discard any 1 card to RETURN TO GOAL.
  7. That discarded card pays only the return/recovery cost. It is not a DEFENSE card.
  8. After returning, resolve the normal GK stage. If the GK still has a DEFENSE, the GK may play it and start RPS. If the GK has no DEFENSE, the attack scores automatically.
  9. If the GK refuses to discard 1 card, or has no card to discard, the goal is empty: automatic GOAL +1.
  10. After that goal, Soccer goes to the defending GK for the restart and the OUT OF POSITION state ends.

IMPORTANT RISK: If your only remaining card is DEFENSE and you discard it to run back, you successfully return to goal — but you now have no DEFENSE left, so the attacker still scores automatically. Recovery and saving are two separate costs/actions.

EXAMPLE: BLUE GK SHOOT → GREEN D1 TACKLE succeeds → GREEN D1 takes Soccer → GREEN attacks through BLUE D1 and BLUE D2 → reaches BLUE GK. BLUE GK discards YELLOW to RETURN TO GOAL → BLUE GK still has DEFENSE → plays DEFENSE → RPS.

12. AFTER A TURNOVER

  1. A successful TACKLE ends the old action immediately.
  2. Tackler physically takes Soccer.
  3. Old attack, beaten status, chase status, and old YELLOW windows end.
  4. The tackler's team becomes the attacking team.
  5. The new ballholder may PASS or SHOOT if holding SHOOT.
  6. A future SHOOT starts fresh at the opponent's D1.

GK EXCEPTION: If the player who lost Soccer to that TACKLE was the attacking Goalkeeper, also apply the OUT OF POSITION rule in Section 11.

13. WHEN THE MAIN MATCH ENDS

  1. Clock reaches 00:00. If a PASS, SHOOT, YELLOW chain, chase-back, GK-return decision, or GK duel is already resolving, finish that current chain first.
  2. Both teams have no Action Cards left.
  3. Both teams can no longer attack. For this playtest, a team can attack if any teammate still has SHOOT.

DO NOT END EARLY: If BLUE has no SHOOT but GREEN still has SHOOT, continue. BLUE's DEFENSE and TACKLE cards still matter.

Higher score wins. If tied, use the Penalty Shootout below.

14. PENALTY SHOOTOUT — FOUR PAIRED DRAWS

For a tied match, collect all 51 Action Cards: cards still in hands, every played/discarded card, chase costs, GK return costs, and the 3 setup extras. Shuffle the full deck.

1. GREEN 1 draws 1 ↔ BLUE GK draws 1
2. GREEN 2 draws 1 ↔ BLUE GK draws a NEW card
3. BLUE 1 draws 1 ↔ GREEN GK draws 1
4. BLUE 2 draws 1 ↔ GREEN GK draws a NEW card
  1. Every field Player gets exactly one paired duel.
  2. Each opposing Goalkeeper draws a fresh card for each field Player, so each GK draws twice in 3v3.
  3. A paired duel is a GOAL only if the field Player draws SHOOT and the opposing GK's paired draw is not DEFENSE.
  4. If the field Player does not draw SHOOT, that duel scores 0.
  5. If the field Player draws SHOOT but the GK draws DEFENSE, that duel is saved and scores 0.
  6. No duel can create a negative score.
  7. Add the two goals for GREEN and the two goals for BLUE. Higher total wins.
  8. If tied, collect all 51 again, reshuffle, and repeat all four paired duels.

15. COMMON TABLE RULINGS

D2 DOES NOTHING
D2 did not defend. No DRIBBLE required. Go straight to GK.
FIELD DEFENSE WORKS
Current SHOOT ends. Attacker keeps Soccer.
FIELD TACKLE WORKS
Tackler takes Soccer. Old attack ends.
WHO MAY DEFENSIVE-YELLOW?
Only the defender on the line currently being attacked, and only in response to opponent DRIBBLE/YELLOW on that line.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL SHOOT?
No.
CAN YELLOW CANCEL DEFENSE?
No.
CAN GK ATTACK?
Yes. GK may PASS or SHOOT when holding Soccer.
WHEN IS GK OUT?
When an opponent TACKLE directly steals Soccer from that attacking GK.
RETURN COST
Discard any 1 card when the counterattack reaches GK. Then, separately, use DEFENSE if available.
NO RETURN?
Empty goal. Automatic GOAL +1.
TEAM WINS SOCCER BACK FIRST?
The immediate counterattack ends and the GK returns to position without paying a card.
PENALTY GK DRAWS
Each GK draws twice: one fresh card against each opposing field Player.
PLAYED CARDS RETURN?
No normal recycle during the main 10-minute match. They return only for a tied-match Penalty Shootout.
