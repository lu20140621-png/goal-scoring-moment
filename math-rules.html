  1. Shuffle the card deck.

  3. Before the first turn, each player reveals any 2 cards in their own grid.
  4. Place the remaining cards as the Draw Pile. Start a Discard Pile beside it.

2. WHAT HAPPENS ON YOUR TURN

TURN RULE: You do not build a hand. Your grid is your game board. Every turn changes exactly one grid position.

5. FOUR MATCHING CARDS BONUS

LEFT PAIR +5
RIGHT PAIR +5
BONUS +5
= 15 TOTAL

If all four cards in one 2 × 2 block match, first score both vertical columns normally, then add an extra +5 bonus.

DO NOT REPLACE THE PAIR SCORES: the +5 is added on top of the two vertical-pair scores.

6. YELLOW CARD — UNMATCHED PENALTY

UNMATCHED YELLOW = −3

If a YELLOW card is left as an unmatched single at final scoring, subtract 3 points.

Other unmatched single cards are worth 0.

WATCH YOUR FINAL GRID: a YELLOW card that does not belong to a scoring vertical pair can lower your total.

7. WHEN THE GAME ENDS

9️⃣
AFTER 9 ROUNDS

The game may end normally after 9 rounds.

👁️
ALL CARDS REVEALED

If one player reveals every card in their grid, Final Turn begins.

🏁
ONE FINAL TURN EACH

Every other player gets exactly one last turn, then all remaining cards are revealed.

  1. Finish the normal end condition or Final Turn.
  2. Reveal every remaining face-down card.
  3. Score each vertical pair.
  4. Add any 2 × 2 four-matching bonuses.
  5. Subtract −3 for each unmatched YELLOW.
  6. Add the final total. Highest score wins.

9. QUICK TABLE SUMMARY

  1. Choose grid size: 2×4 for 2–6 players, 2×3 for 7–8 players.
  2. All cards start face-down; reveal any 2 before play.
  3. Each turn: draw from Draw Pile or take top Discard.
  4. Use that card to replace exactly one grid card.
  5. Only vertical pairs score.
  6. Four matching cards in a 2×2 block = both pair scores +5 bonus.
  7. Unmatched YELLOW = −3; other unmatched singles = 0.
  8. End after 9 rounds, or when one player reveals all cards and everyone else takes one Final Turn.
  9. Reveal everything, total the score, highest score wins.
