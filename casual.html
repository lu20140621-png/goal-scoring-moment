1 — Goal

Draw SOCCER twice and you are eliminated. The last active player wins. Casual Party Mode is free-for-all and has no Goalkeeper special ability.

2 — Set Up

  1. 2–8 players.
  2. Shuffle Action Cards.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  4. Keep hands secret.
  5. Prepare Draw and Discard Piles.
  6. Put SOCCER into the Draw Pile, not on top.

3 — Your Turn

  1. Play any legal Action Cards you want: none, one, or several.
  2. When finished, draw exactly 1 card from the top.
  3. That own final draw ends your turn.
A forced draw caused by SHOOT does not end the active player's turn.

4 — SOCCER

SOCCER triggers immediately, whether drawn normally or because of SHOOT. The player who drew it may immediately use DEFENSE. Without DEFENSE, take 1 Soccer Strike. The second Strike eliminates you.

5 — DEFENSE

  1. Player who drew SOCCER plays DEFENSE.
  2. No Strike is added.
  3. The player who played DEFENSE puts SOCCER back.
  4. That player chooses position 2 or deeper. Position 1 is not allowed.
I played DEFENSE → I choose where SOCCER goes back.

6 — DRIBBLE PAST

Look at the actual top 3 Draw Pile cards in order. Do not rearrange them. Put them back in the same order and continue your turn.

7 — SHOOT

  1. Play SHOOT.
  2. Choose any active player, including yourself.
  3. Allow immediate YELLOW responses before resolving.
  4. If not canceled, target draws 1 card immediately.
  5. If SOCCER is drawn, resolve SOCCER immediately and allow DEFENSE immediately.
  6. After the forced draw is fully resolved, the original active player's turn continues.

8 — TACKLE

Choose another active player with at least 1 hand card. Steal exactly 1 random card from that player's hand and add it to yours.

9 — YELLOW CARD

YELLOW is a reaction that cancels the immediately previous card/effect.

ACTION → YELLOW = ACTION CANCELED
YELLOW must be played immediately before the previous effect is fully resolved. No late YELLOW after the effect is finished.

10 — YELLOW Chains

SHOOT → YELLOW → YELLOW → YELLOW

Each YELLOW cancels the card/effect immediately before it. When nobody plays another YELLOW, resolve the final result.

11 — Multiple Cards

DRIBBLE PAST → TACKLE → SHOOT → DRAW 1 TO END TURN

This is only an example. You do not have to use this order. Finish each card and any YELLOW chain before playing the next Action Card.

12 — Draw Pile Empty

Keep the top Discard card separate. Shuffle the other discarded Action Cards into a new Draw Pile. If SOCCER must be returned, the DEFENSE player puts it at position 2 or deeper.

13 — Elimination

At 2 Soccer Strikes, you are eliminated immediately. Discard every card left in your hand. You take no more turns and cannot be chosen as an active SHOOT or TACKLE target.

14 — How to Win

The last active player wins.

15 — Full Turn Example

You DRIBBLE PAST, TACKLE, then SHOOT Player 3. Everyone gets a moment for immediate YELLOW responses. If SHOOT resolves, Player 3 draws. If that card is SOCCER, Player 3 resolves SOCCER immediately and may use DEFENSE. After the forced draw is finished, it is still your turn. When you are done, make your own final draw. That ends your turn.

16 — Ready Check

✓ 5 cards each.
✓ Play cards, then your own final draw ends your turn.
✓ SOCCER twice = out.
✓ DEFENSE player puts SOCCER back at position 2 or deeper.
✓ SHOOT forced draw does not end the active turn.
✓ YELLOW must be immediate and can cancel another YELLOW.
✓ Eliminated player discards the whole hand.
✓ Last active player wins.
