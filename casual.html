Chapter 1 — What Is the Goal?

Stay in the game longer than everyone else.

Draw SOCCER twice → you are eliminated. The last player still in the game wins.

This is a free-for-all party mode. You do not need soccer knowledge.

There is no Goalkeeper special ability in Casual Party Mode.

Chapter 2 — Set Up

  1. Play with 2–8 players.
  2. Shuffle the Action Cards.
  3. Give each player 5 Action Cards.
  4. Keep your hand secret.
  5. Prepare the Draw Pile and Discard Pile.
  6. Put the SOCCER card into the Draw Pile. In a normal full game, do not place it on the very top.
Everyone begins with 0 Soccer Strikes.

Chapter 3 — What Happens on My Turn?

  1. Look at your hand.
  2. Play any legal Action Cards you want. You may play none, one, or several.
  3. When you are finished playing cards, draw exactly 1 card from the top of the Draw Pile.
  4. That final draw ends your turn.
  5. The next living player takes a turn.
Do not mix this up with SHOOT. A card drawn because of SHOOT is a forced draw. It does not automatically end the active player's turn. Only the active player's own final end-of-turn draw ends that turn.
You play DRIBBLE PAST, then TACKLE, then SHOOT. You decide you are finished. Now you draw 1 card. Your turn ends.

Chapter 4 — What Happens When Someone Draws SOCCER?

  1. Stop immediately.
  2. The SOCCER effect happens before normal play continues.
  3. If that player has DEFENSE, they may use it.
  4. If they do not use DEFENSE, they take 1 Soccer Strike.
  5. If that becomes their second Strike, they are eliminated.
SOCCER can be drawn from your normal end-of-turn draw or from a forced draw caused by SHOOT. Either way, SOCCER triggers immediately.

Chapter 5 — DEFENSE

DEFENSE is used when you are the player who just drew SOCCER.

  1. Play 1 DEFENSE card.
  2. The SOCCER Strike is stopped.
  3. You do not add a Strike.
  4. Return SOCCER to the Draw Pile.
  5. SOCCER may not be placed at position 1, the very top.
  6. Choose position 2 or deeper.
Casual Party Mode has no free Goalkeeper DEFENSE and no Goalkeeper role skill.

Chapter 6 — DRIBBLE PAST

DRIBBLE PAST lets you look at the actual top 3 cards of the Draw Pile.

  1. Play DRIBBLE PAST during your turn.
  2. Look at the top 3 cards in order.
  3. Do not rearrange them.
  4. Put them back in the same order.
  5. Keep taking your turn.
If you see SOCCER is second from the top, you now know the next player may draw a safe card and the player after that may be in danger.

Chapter 7 — SHOOT

SHOOT makes one active player draw 1 card immediately.

  1. Play SHOOT.
  2. Choose any active player. You may even choose yourself.
  3. The chosen player immediately draws the top card.
  4. If it is SOCCER, resolve SOCCER immediately.
  5. After the forced draw is resolved, the original active player's turn continues.
SHOOT's forced draw is not the same thing as the active player's final end-of-turn draw.
It is your turn. You SHOOT Player 2. Player 2 draws DEFENSE. The card goes to Player 2's hand. It is still your turn.

Chapter 8 — TACKLE

TACKLE steals 1 random card from another active player.

  1. Play TACKLE during your turn.
  2. Choose another active player who has at least 1 card.
  3. Take 1 card from that player's hand at random.
  4. Add it to your hand without choosing which card you get.
  5. Keep taking your turn.

Chapter 9 — YELLOW CARD

YELLOW CARD is a reaction card. It cancels the card or effect played immediately before it.

ACTION → YELLOW = ACTION CANCELED
Player 2 plays SHOOT on you. You play YELLOW. The SHOOT is canceled, so you do not make the forced draw.
YELLOW CARD can also cancel another YELLOW CARD.

Chapter 10 — YELLOW CARD Chains

Always look at the card played immediately before the new YELLOW.

SHOOT → YELLOW → YELLOW → YELLOW
  1. SHOOT is played.
  2. First YELLOW cancels SHOOT.
  3. Second YELLOW cancels the first YELLOW, so SHOOT is active again.
  4. Third YELLOW cancels the second YELLOW, so the first YELLOW works again and SHOOT is canceled.
Easy memory rule: every YELLOW cancels the card/effect immediately before it.

Chapter 11 — Play Multiple Cards

You may play several legal Action Cards before your final draw.

DRIBBLE PAST → TACKLE → SHOOT → DRAW 1 TO END TURN

You do not have to use this exact order. It is only an example of one turn.

After every card, finish resolving that card and any YELLOW responses before playing the next Action Card.

Chapter 12 — What If the Draw Pile Runs Out?

  1. Keep the card currently on top of the Discard Pile separate.
  2. Collect the other discarded Action Cards.
  3. Shuffle them to make a new Draw Pile.
  4. Continue the game.

If SOCCER must be returned, place it into a legal position in the new Draw Pile, never position 1.

Chapter 13 — Elimination

  • 0 Soccer Strikes: still playing.
  • 1 Soccer Strike: still playing.
  • 2 Soccer Strikes: eliminated immediately.

An eliminated player no longer takes turns and cannot be chosen as an active target.

Chapter 14 — How to Win

Keep eliminating players until only one player remains.

The last active player is the winner.

Chapter 15 — One Full Turn Example

  1. It is your turn.
  2. You play DRIBBLE PAST and see the top 3 cards.
  3. You play TACKLE and steal 1 random card.
  4. You play SHOOT on Player 3. Player 3 draws 1 card immediately.
  5. A YELLOW response could cancel that SHOOT before the forced draw happens.
  6. After all responses are resolved, it is still your turn.
  7. You decide you are finished.
  8. You draw 1 card from the Draw Pile.
  9. Your turn ends.

Chapter 16 — Ready Check

✓ Everyone starts with 5 cards.
✓ Play any legal Action Cards you want, then draw 1 to end your turn.
✓ SOCCER twice = eliminated.
✓ DEFENSE stops your SOCCER Strike and returns SOCCER to position 2 or deeper.
✓ DRIBBLE PAST only looks at the top 3; no rearranging.
✓ SHOOT forces a draw but does not end the active player's turn.
✓ TACKLE steals 1 random card.
✓ YELLOW cancels the immediately previous card/effect and can cancel another YELLOW.
✓ Last player standing wins.
