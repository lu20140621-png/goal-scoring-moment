CASUAL PARTY MODE

COMPLETE RULEBOOK

Goal

Draw SOCCER twice and you are eliminated. The last active player wins. This mode is free-for-all and has no Goalkeeper special ability.

Set Up

  1. 2–8 players.
  2. Shuffle the Action Cards.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  4. Keep hands secret.
  5. Put SOCCER into the Draw Pile, not on top.

Your Turn

Play any legal Action Cards you want, including none. When finished, draw exactly 1 card from the top. Your own final draw ends your turn.

A draw forced by SHOOT does not end the active player's turn.

SOCCER

SOCCER resolves immediately. The player who drew it may immediately use DEFENSE. Without DEFENSE, take 1 Soccer Strike. A second Strike eliminates that player.

DEFENSE

The player who drew SOCCER may play DEFENSE. No Strike is added. That player puts SOCCER back into position 2 or deeper.

DRIBBLE PAST

Look at the actual top 3 Draw Pile cards. Do not rearrange them. Put them back in the same order and continue your turn.

SHOOT

Choose any active player. If SHOOT is not canceled, that player immediately draws 1 card. Resolve SOCCER immediately if drawn. Then the original active player's turn continues.

TACKLE

Choose another active player with at least 1 card and steal exactly 1 random hand card. TACKLE does not steal the Soccer Card in Casual Party Mode.

YELLOW CARD

YELLOW cancels the immediately previous Action Card or YELLOW before that effect resolves. YELLOW may cancel another YELLOW, creating a chain.

Multiple Cards

You may play several legal Action Cards during your turn. When finished, make your own final draw to end the turn.

Elimination & Win

At 2 Soccer Strikes, discard the eliminated player's remaining hand. The last active player wins.
