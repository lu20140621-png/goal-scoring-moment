YOUR HAND
4-PLAYER CASUAL PARTY MODE

HOW TO PLAY

1Each player starts with 5 cards.
2On your turn, play as many Action Cards as you want.
3DRAWING 1 CARD ENDS YOUR TURN IMMEDIATELY. When you are finished playing Action Cards, tap DRAW 1 CARD — END TURN.
4If you draw the SOCCER card, you take 1 Soccer Strike unless you use DEFENSE.
5Draw SOCCER twice and you are eliminated.
6The last player still in the game wins.

Action Cards: DRIBBLE PAST views the top 3 cards. SHOOT makes a chosen player draw 1 card. TACKLE steals 1 random card from another player. DEFENSE can save you when you draw SOCCER.
