CASUAL PARTY MODE

COMPLETE RULEBOOK

Learn with the 9-step tutorial first. Use this Rulebook whenever you need to check a rule.

1 — Goal

Draw SOCCER twice and you are eliminated. The last active player wins. Casual Party Mode is free-for-all and has no Goalkeeper special ability.

2 — Set Up

  1. Play with 2–8 players.
  2. Shuffle Action Cards.
  3. Deal 5 Action Cards to each player.
  4. Keep hands secret.
  5. Prepare Draw and Discard Piles.
  6. Put SOCCER into the Draw Pile, not on top.

3 — Your Turn

  1. Play any legal Action Cards you want: none, one, or several.
  2. When finished, draw exactly 1 card from the top.
  3. Your own final draw ends your turn.
A forced draw caused by SHOOT does not end the active player's turn.

4 — SOCCER

SOCCER triggers immediately, whether drawn normally or because of SHOOT. The player who drew it may immediately use DEFENSE. Without DEFENSE, take 1 Soccer Strike. The second Strike eliminates you.

5 — DEFENSE

The player who drew SOCCER may play DEFENSE. No Strike is added. The player who played DEFENSE puts SOCCER back and chooses position 2 or deeper. Position 1 is not allowed.

I played DEFENSE → I choose where SOCCER goes back.

6 — DRIBBLE PAST

In Casual Party Mode, DRIBBLE PAST means look at the actual top 3 Draw Pile cards. Do not rearrange them. Put them back in the same order and continue your turn.

7 — SHOOT

Choose any active player, including yourself. If SHOOT is not canceled, that player immediately draws 1. If SOCCER is drawn, resolve SOCCER immediately and allow DEFENSE immediately. After the forced draw is resolved, the original active player's turn continues.

8 — TACKLE

In Casual Party Mode, TACKLE does not steal the Soccer Card. Choose another active player with at least 1 card and steal exactly 1 random hand card.

9 — YELLOW CARD

YELLOW is a reaction that cancels the immediately previous card/effect. It must be played before that effect fully resolves.

ACTION → YELLOW = ACTION CANCELED

10 — YELLOW Chains

SHOOT → YELLOW → YELLOW → YELLOW

Each YELLOW cancels the card/effect immediately before it. When nobody plays another YELLOW, resolve the final result.

11 — Multiple Cards

DRIBBLE PAST → TACKLE → SHOOT → DRAW 1 TO END TURN

This is only an example. You do not have to use this order.

12 — Draw Pile Empty

Keep the top Discard card separate. Shuffle the other discarded Action Cards into a new Draw Pile. If SOCCER must be returned, the DEFENSE player puts it at position 2 or deeper.

13 — Elimination

At 2 Soccer Strikes, you are eliminated immediately. Discard every card left in your hand. You take no more turns and cannot be chosen as an active SHOOT or TACKLE target.

14 — How to Win

The last active player wins.
